# Clarity read A3: "What we prove" v2 (fresh reader, Opus)

Read: `results_v2/what_we_prove_v2.pdf` (23 pp.; main text §1–6 = pp. 1–12, ending with the references on p. 12), via `pdftotext -layout` plus page renders for layout. I did not check any claim against sources. Quotes are from the PDF text. Math is written in plain TeX-like notation.

## 1. Most important items, ranked

1. **Result 5 breaks the promised structure.** §1 says "Sections 3–6 state them, one section each, in the same format". But Result 5 is a run-in paragraph inside §3 (p. 6), labelled a *Remark*, with no "In words" line. The reader meets it before Result 2. Fix: give it its own subsection 3.1 and an "In words" line, or renumber it as Result 2 in the Table, the abstract and the text. Then correct the §1 sentence (rows 1–2).
2. **Symbol collisions in the theorems themselves.** The constant `C_1` (Thm 3.2) clashes with Convention (C1). `C_3` in `ρ_n` sits two lines above "Conventions (C1) and (C3)" in Thm 6.2. `π` means both the posterior and 3.14 in (7), and the 3.14 meaning is glossed only after the display. `Z` is the evidence and also the statistic `Z_j` (rows 16, 17, 19, 20).
3. **"Leakage" and "visibility" are used before they are defined, and §2 does not define them.** The abstract, Table 1, the §6 title and §6 "In words" all use them. The definitions come only in §5 (p. 9) and in (NL) (p. 11). The design names (S1, IND, AR, BLK, FAC; "block", "locally correlated") are never glossed in this document (rows 10–12).
4. **Table 1 and the text disagree.** Row 2 drops the bounded-signal condition that §4 lists. Row 5 says the rate is "attained" on two families, but the text proves a lower bound on both and exact order only on orthogonal designs. Row 1 says "small sets" where the floor is on sets of size O(log n). Row 3 says "masked", while the text says "visible" (rows 5–9).
5. **"Checked" is used for two things.** The Table 1 caption says "Checked" means independently checked. Yet the status column and the boxes contrast "proved and checked" with "proved and independently checked" (Thm 5.3 box), and Row 1 says "verified ... or checked" (rows 3–4).
6. **The Result 4 "why it matters" line does not land.** "every printed summary converges to the truth" is wrong for the evidence estimate, whose target is the exact evidence `Z`, not a truth. In the same result, "weakly correlated" undersells (NL), which needs a partial covariance of order `n^{-1/2}` (rows 8, 31, 32).
7. **§4 is hard to read on the first pass.** The "In words" sentence repeats `n^{-c_H M}` twice inside one sentence. In the Setting paragraph, "this law" has no clear antecedent (rows 21–23).
8. **The page budget is at its limit.** The main text ends exactly on p. 12, so any gloss added above must be paid for. About 15 lines can be cut with no loss: "In primitive terms" (p. 5), the numerics of Remark 3.3(c), Remark 4.3's last sentence, Remark 5.4(i), and the (C2+) detail in §2.3 (rows 13, 14, 18, 23, 29).
9. **The Thm 5.3 delivery clause misuses "admissible".** It applies "admissible" to `n`, but §2.3 defines it for a run. `𝒟` is defined only in Appendix C (row 26).
