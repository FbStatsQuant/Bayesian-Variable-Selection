# Clarity read: assumptions.pdf (A2 v2), fresh reader (Opus)

Scope: clarity only, no source fidelity. Read from `pdftotext -layout` and page renders; tex lines are given as `L<n>`.
Cited numbers I spot-checked in what_we_prove_v1.pdf all exist (Thms 3.1, 3.4, 3.9, 3.13, 4.2, 5.7, 5.12 (d1), 5.19; Assumptions 3.3, 4.1(c)–(e), 4.5(a), 5.6; Prop. 4.3; Rem. 3.2, 5.21; Lemma 2.1; Cor. 4.7).
Length: about 5 text pages plus the table, so the document is at the limit. Every addition proposed below should be paid for by a cut elsewhere (§2 "The primitive form" and §4's internal-process wording are the cheapest cuts).

## Ranked items

1. **§1 ¶2 says "the theorem … holds" where it means "the theorem's assumptions hold" (L41–44).** "holds … and not on pervasive common factors" reads as though the theorem is false there. The advisor's question is about assumptions, so the verb should say so. The same paragraph then says "On every design … the evidence estimate is unbiased" straight after saying that consistency of the printed estimates needs no leakage. On a first reading the two sentences look contradictory. The reader also cannot tell that unbiasedness is a separate result, or that §4 lists two unchecked inputs behind it.
2. **The regime split for the eigenvalue floor (EF) and the collinearity condition (NC) leaves a gap in §3.2, and the table fills it with "otherwise" (L72, L182–200).** In the text, EF has "no larger" (r̄n+1 ≤ 5(A+1)(s0+1)log n) against "larger" (r̄n ≻ log n). NC has r̄n ≥ 3(A+1)(s0+1)log n against r̄n ≺ log n. Neither pair covers r̄n of exact order log n. The EF table cell says "otherwise neither implies the other", so table and text disagree. Either make the text split exhaustive or say that the gap is not compared. Do not merge the split.
3. **Several table cells cannot be read without §2–§3.** The cells rely on "their sets" (r̄n is first defined on p. 3), "Pn ≻ nθ" (Pn and ≻ are never defined), "cψ/n" (defined only in §3.2), and "imitators of the signal" (used nowhere else). The verdict words "No ordering", "neither implies the other" and "No counterpart" are never distinguished. "Inside" is defined only after the table. All of this can be fixed with one caption sentence.
4. **The Theorem S comparison in §2 has no verdict (L132–133).** "within one posterior, … a stronger form of statement" asks the advisor to work out the comparison, and it comes close to an overall "ours are stronger". The Theorem 3 sentence has a similar problem: "add specificity" (add to what?) sits uneasily with "neither implies the other".
5. **Signal size (§3.2, L172–) is the hardest paragraph to read.** It cites five tags (BC, FSβ, FSθ, 4.1(e), BM) and glosses none of them. "one fixed sequence" is self-contradictory on a first reading. Theorem 3.4 is missing from the list of theorems. The table cell keeps only the "fixed signals" branch, so the "3.1 and 4.2 admit more" split is invisible there. The opening "Song and Liang state no lower bound" sits next to the fixed headline's "nonzero coefficients that are not too small". The paragraph needs to say how the two fit together.
6. **The SP evidence (L166) says "It cannot be dropped" and then shows that s0 = O(√log n) is still compatible with the rate.** The reader needs a second pass to see that what cannot be dropped is the rate. K is undefined.
7. **There are two causal or anaphoric slips.** For FAC, "a search found a margin below the aligned-sign bound, so its signs are mixed" reads backwards. In NL, "that result does not apply" has no clear antecedent, and "nothing recorded" is internal jargon.
8. **Visible typesetting defects.** The 5.6 headings have no comma ("Polynomial leakage set Assumption 5.6(a)."). The DIM evidence has r_n without the bar. "S3, S4 (… 0.7, 0.85)" needs "respectively". The "Inside:"/"Outside:" labels are bold in §3.3 leakage and §3.4 but plain in Visibility.
