# Clarity read A5 (Opus): restructured manuscript, §1–6 and supplement A.2–A.4

Reader: a statistician who knows Bayesian variable selection, new to this project. Source: `manuscript.pdf` (pdftotext -layout), draft of 17 Nov 2026; the main text ends on p. 25. Fidelity to sources was not checked. No item strengthens or weakens a claim, removes a status box, changes theorem mathematics or §5.1's first paragraph, alters the three fixed phrases, or collapses a conditional comparison.

## 1. Ranked items

1. **The rule paragraph (§5.3, pp. 20–21) seems to break its own rule.** It says every main-text assumption must be of kind (1), (2a) or (2b). It then says two conditions of Result 3 "are of none of these kinds", but Result 3 is still in the main text. The reader has to work out alone that restricting Result 3 to block and moderate AR correlation is what makes those conditions kind (2b). "The owner's rule" is also project jargon, and "(2a)/(2b)" suggests a hierarchy that is never used.
2. **§6 says the opposite of Result 5 about conditioning.** §6 says "nothing is claimed conditionally on" the calibration succeeding. Result 5 is stated "given the calibration": E[Ẑ | calibration] = Z on E_cal. A careful reader will take this as a contradiction. In the same sentence, §6 cites "(C1)–(C3)", but the main text defines only (C2⁺), never (C2).
3. **The pointer to the consistency of the estimates is misleading, and it uses an undefined symbol.** §4.6 calls Remark A.13 "the consistency of the other reported estimates", but A.13 also covers Ẑ/Z and log Ẑ, so "other" is wrong. §5.4 then says A.13 "needs (NL)", a label that is never defined in §1–6.
4. **The output list in §3.5 lacks two outputs that later results depend on.** Result 4 is about the run's "local-optimality flag (Section 3.5)", and Result 5 mentions "the reported lower bound for log Z". Neither appears in §3.5's list of what a run reports. Result 5 also calls Ẑ "the one reported number", yet §3.5 says the run reports log Ẑ.
5. **§1 item 3 runs three clauses together and uses "visibility" before defining it.** The conditions are introduced in one form of words, and "visibility" then appears with no link back to them. The failure cases are joined to the conditions by ", and … , and".
6. **Result 4 reads as unconditional in §1 and in Table 2.** "Without either condition" and "a small reported model" hide three things: Theorem 12 is a fixed-design result, it needs a floor and signal bounds, and "small" means an analyst-chosen budget k_n. Table 2's "Key assumptions" column also leaves out the fixed design and the budget. A reader then wonders why §5.3.4 says "Not judged".
7. **The section title "The rate cannot be improved" sits next to the bound n^{-1/2}.** §1 and §4.7 give an n^{-1/2} floor on every design and a √(log n/n) rate attained on the witness designs. Nothing explains the √log n gap between the two, or says on which designs the rate is shown to be sharp.
8. **The Precedents lines use page and symbol conventions they never state.** §5.2 says that page numbers are journal pages, and this holds for Song and Liang. In the Precedents lines, however, the page numbers are arXiv pages, e.g. Castillo et al., "pp. 13–14" of a paper on pp. 1986–2018. The text also uses "p ≥ n^a" with *a* undefined, though *A* is the dimension exponent.
9. **The Precedents line for (NC) gives two comparisons in one sentence, and a "lower" floor actually means a weaker condition.** The reader has to reverse "lower" into "weaker" and then catch the "and ours asks more" that follows.
10. **"The global floor (EF_Σ)" (§5.3.3, p. 24) uses the wrong label.** (EF_Σ) is a floor on sets of size O(log n), while Assumption 9(b) needs a floor on Q ∪ {l}, a set of up to n^b + 1 predictors. Only the "for instance λ_min(Σ_n) ≥ φ²" form of (EF_Σ) is global. The heading of §5.3.4 also lacks its parentheses: "…signal bounds Theorem 12."
