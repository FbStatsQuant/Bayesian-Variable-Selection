# Clarity read: "Are the assumptions reasonable?" (assumptions.pdf, draft 16 Nov 2026)

Reader: fresh, clarity only (claims not checked against sources). Page numbers are PDF pages; `tex:N` is the source line in assumptions.tex.

## Ranked items (most important first)

1. **No key from theorem nicknames to theorem numbers.** Page 1 says "the two theorems about the posterior", then "the first theorem", "the distribution theorem", "the reported-model theorem" and "the printed-estimates theorem". Section 3 then uses "random-design theorems", "Gaussian-row theorems", "fixed-design Theorem 3.1", "Theorem 3.4", and "Theorems 3.9, 5.7 and 5.19". The advisor cannot tell which numbered theorem is "the first", or why "two" posterior theorems turn into four (3.1, 3.4, 3.9, 4.2). Add one sentence to "What 'inside' means" that maps each name to its theorem numbers, and then use only those names.
2. **The signal-size row disagrees with its own text.** The table cell "bounded; above the √(log n/n) scale" and p. 1's "a signal on the √(log n/n) scale" both say that every posterior theorem needs a lower bound at that scale. Section 3.2 says that the beta-min "appears only in the fixed-design Theorem 3.1", and that the other theorems need only fixed nonzero coefficients. The table and page 1 need to say the same thing as the text.
3. **The "Verdict" column and the per-assumption verdict lines answer a different question.** They give "same / comparable / stronger / none", which compares each assumption with Song–Liang. They never answer "is it reasonable?". In 3.1–3.2 that answer is only in the one-line lead-in to Section 3 ("Every design is inside..."). It is missing from the lines on signal size, the eigenvalue floor and (GR), the places where the advisor will most want it. Rename the column "vs. Song–Liang", and start each verdict line with the in/out answer for our designs.
4. **Page 1 uses project jargon that is never defined.** "the 10⁶ plan", "our two simulation studies", "S5", "island" and "evidence estimate" all appear before Section 2, and "island" is never defined anywhere. The advisor meets them in the first paragraph and cannot decode them from page 1 alone.
5. **"Bounded leakage set" and "locality" clash with the companion document.** The set has size up to n^b, so it grows with n, but the heading says "bounded"; the companion calls Assumption 5.6 a "Polynomial leakage set". The companion also uses "locality" for 5.6(a)(ii). Here, "Locality" is the name for 5.6(b),(c), while 5.6(a), which contains that locality clause, is called "Bounded leakage set". An advisor who checks the numbers will see a mismatch.
6. **One word is used for two things: "block".** "block design" / "blocks of five" means the correlation structure of the data. "the method's blocks have at most 16" (p. 4) means the sampler's update blocks. In the Visibility evidence both senses appear in two consecutive sentences.
7. **Symbols appear without definition in Section 3.** These are W_j and W_U (Visibility, NL), p_raw, M and "the radius" (NC), L (GR), c_ψ (NC; the table writes c), Q and ϖ_n (leakage set), and P_n and ≻ (DIM footnote). Each one makes the reader stop and look it up in the companion.
8. **Some sentences read as hedging or apology, against the brief.** These are "Exponential p is an open question for us", "its supplement was not inspected", "a search is not a proof", "(internal, partly pending)", "nothing recorded says that this mass is small" and "a candidate repair, not yet proved". Each can be rewritten as a plain status statement without changing the claim, or moved to Section 4.
