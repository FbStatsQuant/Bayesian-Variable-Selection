# Clarity review of D1 — "The SMC-BVS method, as coded"

Reviewer: a statistician new to the project, reading `methodology/smc_bvs_method.pdf` (18 pp., draft of 2026-11-16) as the methodology section of a journal paper. I read only the PDF, its LaTeX source and `shared/smcbvs.sty`, and did not open the code. Page numbers are those of the PDF.

**Arithmetic checked and correct.** Example 2.1 (all prior weights, $\bar w_K=1$ and $19/20$). The identity $\mathcal B(k+1,2p-k)/\mathcal B(1,p)=w(k)$. The prior odds ratio $(k+1)/(2p-1-k)$. Eq. (6): the determinant ratio, $\SSq_S$ (by Woodbury) and $C_n$ all follow from (2), (5) and the IG prior. $\gamma_0e^{d}=e^{F}/m(\emptyset)$. The MH ratios of the neighbour swap and the independence move. The $\rho_V$ formula as an ESS fraction. All Table 1 example values at $n=200,p=10^4$: $K=100$, $B=14$, $\ell_n=14$, $L=8$, $N^c_0=2048$, $(5N^c_0+4N_0)p=5.02\times10^8$, $(\ell_n+K+1)(p+p^2/4)=2.88\times10^9$, $86400/(2\cdot2.88\times10^9)=1.5\times10^{-5}$ s, threshold $0.30$, $10{,}016$ moves, $400{,}001$. The Figure 2 bar labels equal the ratios of the two rows of the Example 5.1 table. The two integer-limit statements on p. 15. The method side of the document is mathematically sound. The problems below are about clarity, completeness and consistency.

---

## The 10 most important items

1. **I cannot implement the grid construction (Algorithm 1, steps 3–4, and "The candidate set and the grid", p. 10).** The text never says how the pilot's temperatures and the grid relate. Is the grid always a refinement of the pilot temperatures, or can it skip some? On which interval does the bisection run? Does a rebuild start again from the pilot temperatures or from the current grid? Does "returns the same grid" mean exact floating-point equality? See 3.9–3.12.
2. **The stage numbers disagree between the figure, the section headings and the text.** In Figure 1, box 5 is the final run ("four islands from $\pi_0$ to $\pi$") and box 6 is the two local searches. In the text, §5.4 is "Stage 5: the sampler, one island" and §5.6 is "Stage 6: the final run and the local searches". So the final run is stage 5 in one place and stage 6 in the other. The sections also run 5.4 = Stage 5, then 5.5 = Stage 4. See 4.6–4.7.
3. **The start mode is placed in two different stages.** Figure 1, box 3, and the heading of §5.3 put it in stage 3. The text says "It is computed at the end of the run, where the second local search needs it (stage 6)" (p. 7). See 4.8.
4. **A typesetting bug on p. 6.** The source has a literal TAB in `\times` (lines 269–270), so the PDF prints "2.9imes10^9" and "1.5imes10^{-5}". These are the two key numbers of the time check. See 4.5.
5. **"Stage" has two meanings.** It names a pipeline stage (1–7) and also one temperature step $t$ of the SMC sampler ("For each stage $t$", "stage ceiling", "some stage below $0.5N$"). In §5.1 the two meanings sit in adjacent paragraphs. See 4.9.
6. **The calibration's control flow has gaps.** The text does not say what happens at tier 1 when a trigger fires or the grid has not converged. It does not say whether a failed construction at step 3, at tier 0, skips the rebuild loop. It gives no move cap for the calibration's local searches, and "the best model found" has no stated scope. See 3.13–3.17.
7. **The model stores are named and counted inconsistently.** The text uses "Stores of leading models" (never defined), "the best $40p+1$ models of the calibration, the leading models by $\gamma_0$ and by $F$" (p. 11), Table 1's "stored models: best $40p+1$ by $F$", and $V$ = "$20p$ best by $\gamma_0$ and $20p$ best by $F$". It also says "carried into the final run", which suggests the models seed particles, but the final run starts from fresh draws from $\pi_0$. See 2.17, 4.17, 1.17.
8. **The start law cannot be reproduced as written.** The cut-and-pack rule for blocks is not specified. In the exact sampler the backward split is not given as a formula, and the text does not say that the within-block draw is restricted to subsets of the drawn size. See 3.1–3.4.
9. **"Evidence" has two meanings.** It is defined as $Z$ (p. 4), but "the evidence for a model" (pp. 7, 8) means $m(S)/m(\emptyset)$. The symbols $r$, $N$ and $V$ are also each used for 3–4 different things. See 4.10–4.13.
10. **The output terminology contradicts itself.** "Runs without output" return a model. Neither list of what is reported mentions the pooled $\widehat{\mathrm{PIP}}_j$ or what an exact run reports. The global certificate is said both to be "reported" and to be "set". See 4.20–4.23.
