\section{Scope, and what is not claimed}\label{sec:scope}

The results are asymptotic in $n$, with $p$ at most polynomial in $n$; one lower bound (Remark~\ref{rem:sharp}(a)) holds at
every $n\ge3$. The explicit constants of the proofs are conservative. The results cover the posterior that the software targets
and, for Results~3--5, the software itself in its default configuration, under Conventions \as{C1}, \as{C2p} and \as{C3}, which
idealise the arithmetic, the random numbers and the allocation. A run that
stops early returns no estimates and is not covered. Which designs are inside each result is in
Section~\ref{sec:who-outside}. The consistency of the reported inclusion probabilities, median model and evidence estimate,
and the guarantee for the list a run returns, are remarks in the supplement. Statements about a run's estimates concern the event that the
calibration succeeds, or that the run completes; no lower bound on the probability of that event is claimed. Apart from
Result~5, which holds given each calibration outcome that freezes a grid, nothing is claimed conditionally on success or
on completion. No theorem covers a parallel engine, runs with more than the four
islands, or the engineered variant used at $p=10^6$ (Section~\ref{sec:supp-1m}); results about those are empirical and
reported elsewhere.
