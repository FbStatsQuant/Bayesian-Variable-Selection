% Simulation designs (S1–S3), draft for later use
The first design follows \citet{YangWainwrightJordan2016} for the coefficients and their scaling with the sample size, and \citet{GriffinLatuszynskiSteel2021} for the correlation $\rho=0.6$ between neighbouring covariates.
The second design follows \citet{HastieTibshiraniTibshirani2020}, with autoregressive covariates and a grid of signal-to-noise ratios.
The third design uses equal pairwise correlation $0.5$ as in \citet{GeorgeMcCulloch1993} and the harder value $0.8$ of \citet{DezeureBuhlmannMeierMeinshausen2015}.
