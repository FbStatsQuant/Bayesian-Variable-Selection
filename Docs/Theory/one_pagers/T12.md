## Statement (as proved)
Source: proofs/T12_T8_frozen_run_identities/STATEMENT.md (condensed here from LOCKED_PROOFS.md and FROZEN_LIST.md) (repo paths under `Docs/Theory/`). Faithful condensation; the source file is authoritative.
- (per LOCKED_PROOFS.md) The frozen-run evidence estimate is unbiased in product form: E[Z-hat q-hat(f)] = Z q(f). For the recorded Rao-Blackwell PIP, the islandwise unnormalised product is unbiased: E_c[Z-hat_r PIP-hat_rj] = Z PIP_j. The normalised PIP estimate itself is not asserted to be unbiased, and the identity does not hold for pooled Z-hat × pooled PIP. Pooled evidence is unbiased (E_c[Z-hat] = Z for every successful calibration value), with Markov and Jensen consequences. The statement also gives limits: stopping rules that depend on the state, normalised PIPs, conditioning on favourable diagnostics, and numerical perturbation each break an identity.
- Restatement notes (FROZEN_LIST): freeze everything before the final randomness; use the islandwise product identity (it is NOT an identity for the pooled Z-hat times the primary pooled PIP); drop the secondary PIP; validate floating point separately.
