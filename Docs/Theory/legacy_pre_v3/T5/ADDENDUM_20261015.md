# Addendum (writing review, 2026-10-15): T5(v) with stage-dependent leakage

Source: Sol team lead, proposal A7, confirmed by reviewer S1 against §5 "Proof of (v)" (`writing/sol/LEAD_REPORT.md`). This addendum adds to §5 and leaves the rest of PROOF.md unchanged.

**Claim.** Let $\eta_{t,\ell}=\sup_{S\notin\mathcal M}K_{t,\ell}(S,\mathcal M)$. For a fixed schedule,
$$\Prob(\text{some particle ever in }\mathcal M)\le N\Big\{q_0(\mathcal M)+\sum_{t}\sum_{\ell\le m_t}\eta_{t,\ell}\Big\}.$$

**Proof.**
1. The proof of (v) bounds the probability that particle $j$ first enters $\mathcal M$ at move $(t,\ell)$ by the entrance event $\mathcal B_{t,\ell,j}$, with $P(\mathcal B_{t,\ell,j})\le\eta$.
2. That bound uses only $K_{t,\ell}(S,\mathcal M)\le\eta$ at the current move. It therefore holds with $\eta_{t,\ell}$ in place of $\eta$.
3. A union bound over $j$ and $(t,\ell)$, plus the initial event (probability $\le Nq_0(\mathcal M)$), gives the claim. $\square$

The adaptive extension (vi) is stated in the draft for a uniform $\eta$ only.
