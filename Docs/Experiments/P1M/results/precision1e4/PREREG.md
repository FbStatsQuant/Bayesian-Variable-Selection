### 3.2 The rule (exactly what `precision_freeze.py` computes)
Baseline B = (N 2,500, m 1, I_max 3), the spec defaults. Per (dataset, cell) value of each metric = the mean of the 2 replicates (both must be delivered **and identity-verified**). **r_X** = geometric mean over the three datasets of value(X)/value(B), per metric; **c_X** = mean billed **`islands`** core-h of the arm's fits / the baseline's (cost files; **calibration is excluded**, R16: for m = 4 the island ratio and the whole-fit ratio agree, 2.1 vs 1.97 on the overlap-based model).
- **Cost-raising knob X ∈ {m = 4, N = 10⁴}:** **ADOPT iff for each of M1, M2, M3: r_X ≤ 1/√c_X, and r_X < 1 in every dataset, and the HPM set is the same in every fit of X ∪ B** (**per knob, over the fits of that arm and the baseline only; a mismatch in another arm cannot flip this knob, R1**). Otherwise KEEP the baseline value. *Reason:* for the island-pooled estimator the sd falls as 1/√R′, so spending a factor c_X more on islands buys a factor 1/√c_X; a refinement is worth adopting only if it beats that. With the modelled costs (m = 4 ≈ 2.2–2.3×) the limit is ≈ 0.66; for N = 10⁴ (4×) it is 0.5; the script uses the **measured** c_X.
- **I_max = 0 (a NON-INFERIORITY margin, not a cost rule, R4):** **ADOPT iff for each of M1, M2, M3: r ≤ 1.15, and r ≤ 1.30 in every dataset, and the HPM set is the same in every fit of the arm ∪ B.** Otherwise KEEP I_max = 3 (the spec default). *Reason and limit:* the margin says "not materially worse"; it is not derived from a cost ratio. At R′ = 64 the calibration is ≈ 10 % of a fit (0.4–1.1 of 6.3 core-h), at the production R′ = 16 it is 35–45 %, and a 15 % sd loss costs 32 % more islands; the leg report states the measured R′ = 16 cost saving beside the decision so HQ can weigh it.
- **Resampling:** systematic resampling is not available in the frozen binary (B3): **frozen OFF (D-14 default), reported "not tested"** (HQ Q5). **N = 10⁴ arms: NOT RUN in this leg (HQ Q1: held; the V-N mini-leg runs them under this same rule with their measured c_X); N is frozen at 2,500 by default (D-1), "provisional".**
- **Resolution and operating characteristics (R3; the first draft's "≈ 9 % per fit, 4 % per arm" assumed normal theory on 63 df and is withdrawn).** Observed on the 9 non-NULL overlap V datasets (two seeds each, R′ = 16): the seed-to-seed spread of the metrics is **M1 0.23, M2 0.14, M3 0.27 (sd of the log, per fit, i.e. 14–27 %)**, with single seed ratios 0.59–2.02; it includes grid-to-grid variability and Ẑ_r tails. At R′ = 64 expect ≈ 0.10–0.15 per fit, hence sd(log r_X) ≈ 0.06–0.09 after the 3-dataset geometric mean and ≈ 0.10–0.15 per dataset. Consequences: with 9 per-dataset conditions an I_max = 0 arm that is truly identical to the baseline is ADOPTed with probability **≈ 0.8, not ≈ 1**; m = 4 needs a true ratio **≲ 0.55** to be adopted reliably. The rule is a decision rule, not a significance test, and it never rests on one dataset. `evidence.sigma_hat_robust` is reported beside M3.
- **Interaction not tested:** m × I_max are not crossed (each knob is compared with the baseline). If both are adopted the frozen configuration (m 4, I_max 0) is a combination the study did not run; the first science wave after FZ is then a 7-fit pilot of that combination before the rest (LEG_PLAN §4 rule: re-quote).

## 4. THE pre-declared measurement: the V − T log Ẑ offset against N, m, I_max and R′ (H-25 (3): ONE measurement)
### 4.1 Why and what
The overlap verdict reported, post hoc and outside every criterion, V − T log Ẑ = −0.00394 over the 10 overlap datasets (t(9) −2.80, p 0.021; 7/10 negative; every dataset inside its own 3-s.e. bound) and could not tell whether it is noise, a T-escalation effect, a finite-N / finite-R′ effect, or a systematic difference between the arms. This measurement is the plan's answer. It is **a MEASUREMENT, not a pass/fail criterion** (and, **R9**, its N dimension is limited here to T's own N tiers because V cannot run N = 10⁴ at F; HQ Q1 moves the V N-contrast to the V-N mini-leg); the spec makes no criterion of it (E1 of the overlap is closed; §0-v2.3 evidence targets are §3).

### 4.2 Estimand (all log Ẑ on the same dataset d)
- **V block mean.** For a V fit f with R′_f islands and an integer k that divides R′_f: L_f(k) = the mean over the R′_f/k **disjoint consecutive blocks** of [log Σ_{r∈block} exp(log Ẑ_r) − log k] (a block's Ẑ is the mean of its islands' Ẑ_r). k = R′_f is the fit's own `evidence.logZ`. k ∈ {4, 8, 16} on the overlap V fits (R′ = 16) and {4, 8, 16, 32, 64} on the leg's fits (R′ = 64). **No extra run is needed: this is a re-reading of the stored `evidence.logZ_r`.**
- **T reference** T̄_d = the mean over the two T seeds of `evidence.logZ` of the overlap T fit(s) (R′ = 4; tier 0 N = 10⁴, tier 1 N = 4 × 10⁴: AR r01 s1, ARweak r01 s1 and IND r01 s2 are tier 1). The tier mix is printed; a **sensitivity with the tier-0 T fits only** is a column of every row.
- **Offset** D_{cell,k,d} = mean over the cell's fits on d of [L_f(k) − T̄_d]; **δ_{cell,k}** = mean over the datasets of D_{cell,k,d} (datasets weighted equally; the dataset is the independent unit). **cell = (N, m, I_max, src)** from `calib.bin.json`; src = `ovl` (the overlap's V fits, 10 datasets) or `prec` (the leg's fits, 3 datasets: AR, ARweak, ADJ-OPP).
- **Contrasts (the T reference cancels), paired by dataset:** R′ slope D_{cell,k2} − D_{cell,k1} for consecutive k; **m**: (2,500, 4, 3) − (2,500, 1, 3) at k = 64; **I_max**: (2,500, 1, 0) − (2,500, 1, 3) at k = 64; **N**: (10⁴, m, 3) − (2,500, m, 3) at k = 64 (only if the N = 10⁴ fits exist).
- **Companions, reported with no inference:** the Jensen term −v̂/(2k) (v̂ = the relative variance of Ẑ_r over the fit's islands; the first-order prediction of the R′ slope if Ẑ is unbiased), and the full-pool offset of log Ẑ_occ (M = 1,000) V − T.

### 4.3 Yardsticks and what is reported
- **Yardsticks:** s_T = pooled seed-to-seed sd of T's log Ẑ over the datasets with two T seeds (overlap: 0.00324, 10 df); s_rep = pooled sd of the two replicates of a precision cell at k = 64; the island-based sd. Every δ is also printed in units of s_T.
- **Uncertainty:** dataset-clustered: mean, sd over datasets, 95 % t-interval (df = datasets − 1; t table in the script), negative counts, and **every per-dataset value** (3 datasets give a very wide interval: say so).
- **Reported tables** (`offset_report.md`, `offset.json`, `offset_per_fit.csv`): (a) δ for every cell × k with the interval; (b) the contrast table; (c) T tiers; (d) dropped fits and duplicates resolved. **Three headline lines, fixed now (no other result is headlined):**
  **P1** δ(ovl, 2,500, 1, 3, k = 16) on the 10 overlap datasets (must reproduce −0.00394; it is the continuity check);
  **P2** the R′ **span** k_min → k_max with the Jensen prediction beside it and the slope minus the prediction (R11): δ(ovl, k = 4) → δ(ovl, k = 16) on the 10 overlap datasets, and δ(prec, 2,500, 1, 3, k = 4 → 64) on the 3 leg datasets (`kind P2_span`); the consecutive steps (`R_step`) are descriptive only;
  **P3** the three knob contrasts (m, I_max, N) at k = 64 on the 3 datasets.

### 4.4 How the result may be read (declared in advance; no post-hoc reading)
- *If δ is the same at k = 4 and k = 64 within the Jensen term and the knob contrasts include 0:* the offset is **not** a finite-R′, finite-N, grid or calibration effect at these settings; it is either noise or a difference between the V and T arms that none of the studied knobs changes (the arms differ in the kernel arithmetic, the calibration rule, N, R′, escalation).
- *If a P2 span minus its Jensen prediction has an interval excluding 0 (the only slope reading made; R_step contrasts are not read):* the pooled estimator has an R′-dependent bias beyond the log-of-mean term.
- *A knob contrast whose interval excludes 0* is a FINDING (§3.3): the log Ẑ of V moves with a tuning knob although each Ẑ_r is unbiased for a frozen grid; it goes to HQ and Theory. Multiplicity (R11): the report prints the number of contrasts by kind (P2_span, R_step ≈ 12, knob); the false-flag expectation is 0.05 × the count for each kind, and only P2_span and knob contrasts are read.
- No statement of the form "V is biased low" is made from δ alone: no truth for log Z exists, and T carries its own noise and tier effect.

### 4.5 Resolution (so the measurement is not over-read)
T̄_d has se s_T/√2 ≈ 0.0023; a V fit's log Ẑ at R′ = 64 has sd ≈ 0.003 (seed-to-seed at R′ = 16 is 0.0058, /2 for 64 islands); a precision cell (2 replicates) ≈ 0.002. A dataset offset therefore has se ≈ 0.003, and **3 datasets with t(2) = 4.30 give a 95 % half-width of ≈ 0.008: the leg's 3-dataset cells can only resolve an offset roughly twice the overlap's −0.0039.** The R′ slope and the knob contrasts do not carry the T noise (it cancels) and are far tighter; the 10-dataset overlap cell has df 9 (half-width 0.003). A third T replicate would sharpen the V − T level; it is not in this leg. **With df = 2 the realised half-width is itself very variable** (a variance budget from the overlap gives ≈ 0.0076 for the 3-dataset cells, R12).
