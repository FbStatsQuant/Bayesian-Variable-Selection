3. **Offset.** The estimator is valid. It is one pre-declared measurement with no pass/fail. ±0.008 is honest (R12). It reproduces −0.00394 exactly. The P2 contrast and multiplicity need the doc and script touch in R11.
4. **Quote.** Reproduced exactly. The n-scaling (planning nf 0.7/1.0/1.6/2.8, T growth (n/200)^g) is an unmeasured assumption, correctly gated by the S6 and S8 pilots. Even a 2× error everywhere leaves the leg at ≈ 1,200 against 4,127. The manifest overstatement of 3–8× is confirmed. **Self-release on cost: YES.**

6. **Ops and timeline.** Waves and pacing comply with H-25 and NOTS_FACTS. The timeline is ≈ 1 day optimistic but stays inside H-14's window (R17).
7. **Registered scripts.** Shas and self-tests are OK. Two decision-flipping bugs in `precision_freeze.py` (R1, R2); P2 and multiplicity gaps in `precision_offset.py` (R11). `precision_offset.py` itself is otherwise correct (regexes match the generated run ids, including `-I0-`; dedup by mtime; tier-0 sensitivity; t-table).
