**3.2 Robustness to the rule's own uncertainty (stated, not a re-decision).**
- **The M3 penalty is real, and the margin is thin.** All 6 I_max = 0 M3 values exceed both I_max = 3 values of their dataset.
- Replicate noise measured at R′ = 64, as pooled within-cell sd of the log over the 9 cells (9 df; includes grid-to-grid variation): **M1 0.058, M2 0.089, M3 0.059**. That is better than PREREG's 0.10–0.15 guess and far below the 0.14–0.27 that the A0 check / R3 saw at R′ = 16.
- So the se of log geo-M3 is ≈ 0.034, and the **M3 geo ratio is 1.130, 95 % CI ≈ [1.05, 1.22]**. The margin log(1.15 / 1.130) = 0.017 is **≈ 0.5 se**. Per dataset, the max of 1.155 is ≈ 2 se below 1.30.
- **A fresh replication would ADOPT with probability ≈ 0.6–0.7** (0.58 at PREREG's pessimistic se of 0.09). If the arms were truly identical it would be ≈ 0.94–1.0. Of the 64 single-replicate re-draws (one replicate per arm per dataset), 38 ADOPT.
- So the ADOPT is **not replication-stable**, and in the strict sense non-inferiority at 15 % is **not demonstrated**: the upper CI bound of 1.22 exceeds 1.15.
- But PREREG §3.2 registered a point-estimate *decision rule*, "not a significance test", with exactly this operating characteristic disclosed. The registered outcome is therefore ADOPT, and **it stands**. Re-running to "confirm" it, or overriding it on judgment, would both be the post-hoc substitution §3.3 forbids.
- Sensitivity, not part of the rule: robust σ̂ gives ratios of 1.04 / 1.08 / 1.19, geo 1.10.
