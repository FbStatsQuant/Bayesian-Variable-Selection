2. **§7 overstates what Z_T / Ẑ measures, and says no criterion uses log Z_T, which is not quite right (check 4).** *Severity: low (wording); not blocking.*
   *Evidence:* The engine's own field note on `store.pi_T_hat` reads: "noisy diagnostic Z_T/Zhat (ratio of estimates), unclipped; not a bound on the store mass; never certifies P-STORE". §7 says "the store holds about **76 %** of the posterior mass … the rest, ≈ 24 %, sits in models outside the top 40 M". The sentence "the unstored mass grows with p at fixed n … the posterior spreads over more near-S₀ models" states a mechanism as fact, but it compares one 1M dataset with five different 10⁵ datasets whose store caps also differ (40p + 1).
