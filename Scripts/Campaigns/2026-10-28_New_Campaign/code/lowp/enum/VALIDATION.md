## Fallback paths

In exact arithmetic Q_S ≥ y′y/(n+1) (master §I.2), far above the 1e-8·y′y rule, and the unit ridge keeps H and A positive definite, so the fallback to `Scorer::score` never fired on any dataset here (`n_fallback_v3 = 0` in every run, including the full p = 30 run). It is exercised only through test hooks: `fail_vars` (forces the pivot of given variables to "fail", so all descendants are rescored by `Scorer::score`, counted in `n_fallback_slow_subtree`) and `qthr` (raises the Q threshold, counted in `q_recomputed`). In both, every F equals `Scorer::score`'s F. The natural `q_nonneg` and QR routes are therefore not exercised.

### Extrapolation from the 3% subset (`tests/t6_extrapolate.R`, reads the saved records)

Method: run a random 3% of the 1,024 prefix tasks on one thread (default settings, so the production re-checks are included in the times), record per-task elapsed times, fit a quadratic in the number of included prefix variables |P|, and sum the prediction over all 1,024 tasks. Results: single-thread **elapsed** projection 976 s (±42 s, conditional standard error of the fit; the naive mean-task × 1,024 is also 976 s; R² of the fit is 0.00, so the quadratic adds nothing and the per-task times (636 to 1,467 ms) are dominated by machine noise); single-thread **CPU** projection 792 s (subset CPU × 1,024/31). The measured full run used 886 s of CPU, 1.12 times the CPU projection, and its tasks summed to 1,925 s elapsed, 1.97 times the single-thread elapsed projection (descheduling, SMT and E-core placement when 14 threads share a loaded 14-thread machine). A greedy dynamic-schedule simulation of 14 threads on the predicted task times gives 70 s with no slowdown and 139 s with the 1.97 factor; **that factor was read off the full run itself, so the 139 s reproduces the measurement by construction and is not an independent prediction**. The ±42 s is the fit uncertainty only; the real uncertainty in a campaign wall time is the state of the machine (observed ×1.6 between two full runs).

Indicative cost per p = 30 dataset (both scales): about 790 to 890 core-seconds of CPU in this session (1,084 s in the earlier run), so roughly 130 to 180 core-hours for 600 datasets at this machine's speed; elapsed on 14 local threads from about 70 s (idle machine, ideal scheduling) to 140 to 222 s (measured under load). ## `lowp_td`: swaps at |S| = K, `toward` variant, real example

Swaps are feasible at |S| = K (size unchanged); only additions are skipped there. `toward`: at S ≠ S₀, margin = (best gain among moves that strictly reduce |S Δ S₀|: add a truth, delete a null, swap null-out/truth-in) − (best gain among the moves that do not); at S₀ the literal clause (c) margin. The top-level result fields are the literal T37 wording; `r$toward` has the same structure; `r$diagnosis` tabulates the best competing move of the literal violators.
