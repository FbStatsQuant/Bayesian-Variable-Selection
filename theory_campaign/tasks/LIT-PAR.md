# LIT-PAR: is there already a parallel, correlation-robust BVS with strong guarantees?

**Why this task matters.** The paper's thesis (Felipe, 2026-11-03): sequential MCMC for BVS, however good, cannot scale p indefinitely; the next generation of samplers must be parallel AND keep MCMC-level guarantees AND work under correlated designs. Felipe's belief: methods with guarantees are sequential; parallel-friendly methods with guarantees (e.g. Song–Liang 2015 split-and-merge) break under correlated designs. Our method (METHODOLOGY_v3 §6.3–6.9) is local-to-global: exact enumeration of correlation-clustered blocks (independent, parallel) → product start π₀ → SMC tempering to the exact posterior, run as independent islands with unbiased Ẑ (Ẑ-weighted pooling consistent as islands R → ∞ at fixed N; T31(i)); per-stage particle moves are parallel; the sequential depth is the stage count T (O-STAGES: T* = O(1) favourable, log₂A₀ + O(1) masked). **Your job is to try hard to FALSIFY the gap claim**, then state precisely what survives.

**Scope.** Variable-splitting / feature-partitioning BVS: Song–Liang 2015 (SaM; read its theory and exactly which correlation conditions it needs), Gutierrez–Guhaniyogi 2022, DECO (Wang–Dunson–Leng 2016, decorrelated feature splitting), message passing / distributed feature methods, screening-then-select. Data-splitting Bayes (consensus Monte Carlo, WASP, median posterior, Minsker; Scott et al. 2016; Srivastava et al.) and whether any handles model spaces. Embarrassingly parallel BVS: Bayesian bootstrap spike-and-slab LASSO (Nie–Ročková), weighted likelihood bootstrap, parallel/vectorised Gibbs (e.g. Johndrow–Orenstein–Bhattacharya scalable horseshoe), GPU BVS, SuSiE/IBSS and variational BVS with guarantees (Ray–Szabó, Yang–Pati–Bhattacharya) — are they parallel, exact, correlation-robust? Parallel SMC/tempering theory: island particle models (Vergé et al. 2015), divide-and-conquer SMC (Lindsten et al. 2017; Kuntz–Crucinio–Johansen), parallel tempering on GPUs and NRPT (Syed et al.), Whiteley–Lee–Heine on interaction in SMC, SMC on GPUs (Lee et al. 2010), and any work on the parallel DEPTH (span) of samplers vs mixing time. 2020–2026 work especially.

**Key questions.** (1) Table every candidate on four axes: parallel (what is parallel; sequential depth as a function of p, n), target (exact posterior vs approximation), guarantees (what is PROVED, under which design conditions — especially correlation conditions), and evidence under correlated designs. (2) Verdict on the gap claim: TRUE / FALSE / TRUE WITH QUALIFICATIONS, with the strongest counterexample candidate argued out. (3) How the parallel-scaling claim should be phrased so a referee accepts it (work vs depth; what to measure; which theorem would carry it — e.g. depth = stage count while width = R·N). (4) Threats: anything that already does what we claim.
