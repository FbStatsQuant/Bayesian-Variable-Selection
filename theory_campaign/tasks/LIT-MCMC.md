# LIT-MCMC: literature on BVS samplers and their mixing/discovery guarantees

**Scope.** MCMC for Bayesian variable selection and its theory: Yang–Wainwright–Jordan (2016, rapid mixing), Zhou–Yang–Vats–Roberts (2022, dimension-free mixing / informed proposals), Zanella–Roberts (2019, tempered Gibbs / importance tempering), Griffin–Łatuszyński–Steel (2021, adaptive MCMC), locally informed and multiple-try proposals (Zanella 2020, Liang et al. 2022+), add-delete-swap theory, recent 2023–2026 mixing results (including high-dimensional, correlated designs, and lower bounds / slow-mixing constructions). Key questions: (a) under which conditions do these guarantee fast discovery of S₀, and do the study designs or the linked-hub family satisfy them (API-POS says yes on LH)? (b) Is there a known family where standard BVS MCMC provably mixes slowly but a tempered/SMC method succeeds? (c) What does that imply for the value of our masked-regime theorems?

**Our results (read first, briefly):** `theory/W8-SYNTH:theory_campaign/W8-SYNTH/BRIEF.md` (campaign summary), `theory/T38-RECORD:theory_campaign/T38-RECORD/T38_STATEMENT.md` (main theorem of record) and `LOCKABLE_v2.md`, `theory/F-REFINE:theory_campaign/F-REFINE/ARCHITECTURE.md` (paper structure), `theory/API-POS:theory_campaign/API-POS/REPORT.md` (on the linked-hub family the masking is a property of the start, not the posterior; rapid-mixing theorems apply there), `theory/API-N200:theory_campaign/API-N200/REPORT.md` (finite-n theorem matching the n = 200 counts), and `Docs/Methodology/METHODOLOGY_v3.md` §1–3 for the method. `git fetch origin` first.
