# W1-T24G: Gibbs-path repair (restated T24): from near-truth to the truth being scored

**Paper role:** Thm 3 (repair), alternative route. The preliminary data favour it: about 98% of fresh scores come from Gibbs flips, every reported HPM was first found by Gibbs or swap moves, and the final local search repaired 0 fits (`Docs/THEORY_PRIORITIES.md` §5). It feeds T38(b2) and T21 route (e), whose open input is H24.

## Problem
T24 (`Docs/Theory/theorems/T24_T18p_terminal_repair.md`, one-pager `Docs/Theory/one_pagers/T24.md`) bounds P(truth never scored) by (1−a)^H + Hη for a particle in 𝓡_d at the terminal rounds. As written it is useless:
- its constant is 1/(s₀+d+B+2)! = 1/31! at s₀ = 14;
- LK_d fails in correlated designs: a look-alike of a missing truth has leakage δ² ≈ (n−1)θ²r².

Fable's proposed repair (`Docs/Theory/PAPER_SELECTION_20261028/FABLE_REVIEW.md`, Thm 4 line):
- evaluate LK_d only at states containing S₀ (zero leakage);
- charge look-alike additions at incomplete states to the budget d;
- replace the full-ordering event with a **one-correct-Gibbs-step-per-round progress argument**, giving (1−c′)^{H/(s₀+d)} with c′ ≈ 1/(B+3);
- the stated failure point is EXIT_d bounding a non-Gibbs undo of a just-made correct step.

## Goals, in order
1. **Model the round.** Write down exactly what one terminal round does at φ = 1 (randomly ordered Gibbs sweep, swaps and independence moves, per `METHODOLOGY_v3.md` and T09). Define a progress potential on 𝓡_d.
2. **Restated T24, statement and proof.** For a particle in 𝓡_d at the start of the terminal rounds, show
   - P(S₀ not scored within H rounds) ≤ (1 − c′)^{⌊H/(s₀+d)⌋} + (leakage/exit terms),
   - with c′ explicit and polynomial (not factorial), and every hypothesis named and checkable.
   Handle the EXIT_d issue: can a non-Gibbs move undo a correct step, and how is it charged? Use the T10 addition-hazard floor r_k and the T09 kernel facts.
3. **Probabilistic inputs.** Show which premises hold with probability → 1 under G, R, SE and BM-∞ (fixed s₀ and d), and give finite-n expressions where you can.
4. **Numerical check.** Exact small-p computation (p ≤ 20) of the per-round progress probability on designs that mimic S1–S6, at c = 1 and c = 0.5. Compare it with your c′.
5. **Wiring.** Show how the restated T24 discharges H24 in T21 route (e) (`Docs/Theory/proofs/T21_T14H_HPM_composition/`).
