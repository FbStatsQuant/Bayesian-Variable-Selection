# LIT-SMC: literature on SMC samplers and their guarantees

**Scope.** SMC samplers and their theory: Del Moral–Doucet–Jasra (2006) and successors; SMC on binary/discrete spaces (Schäfer–Chopin 2013 and later); adaptive tempering, ESS-based schedules and stage-count results (e.g. Jasra et al., Beskos et al., Giraud–Del Moral, Dai et al. review 2022); unbiasedness and variance of the normalising-constant estimator and non-asymptotic bounds (Cérou–Del Moral–Guyader, Whiteley, Marion et al.); divide-and-conquer / local-to-global SMC (Lindsten et al. 2017, Kuntz et al.); SMC for variable selection specifically; any a-posteriori certificates or diagnostics with guarantees. Key question: which of our results about the evidence (T12/T31, PW2, masked-regime Ẑ), the stage count (Theorem G, O-STAGES) and discovery (T38, Theorem B) are already known for general SMC, and which are new because they are specific to the scored-HPM BVS setting?
