# THM-INV: full inventory of the method and its theory, against the literature (Track B, step 1)

**Project goal (Felipe, 2026-11-03; read carefully).** Paper 1 is not "another BVS". Sequential samplers (MCMC), however good, cannot keep scaling p; the aim is to start replacing them with PARALLEL samplers that keep MCMC-level guarantees AND work under correlated designs. Song & Liang (2015, JRSS-B, "split-and-merge", `theory_campaign/inputs/SongLiang2015.pdf`) is parallel but breaks under correlation (stage-1 screening needs |cov(x_t,y)| > 0 for every true predictor, Condition 5; the merged posterior is not the full posterior). Our method is local-to-global (proposal: `theory_campaign/inputs/Local_to_Global_proposal.pdf`): exact enumeration of correlation-clustered blocks (parallel) → product start π₀ (the merge) → SMC tempering to the EXACT posterior (the correction), run as independent islands with unbiased Ẑ. Thesis sentence: "In split-and-merge, correlation breaks correctness; in local-to-global SMC, correlation costs only sequential depth and particles, never correctness." Objectives in order: (1) accuracy of the posterior (Ẑ, PIPs, joint inclusion), (2) parallel scalability, (3) selection accuracy. Existing theory goals are KEPT; the parallel/accuracy guarantees are added on top.
