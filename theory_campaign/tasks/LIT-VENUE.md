# LIT-VENUE: what comparable methodology papers prove, by journal

**Scope.** A sample of 12–20 methodology papers on computational Bayesian variable selection or SMC/MCMC for model spaces published 2016–2026 in JRSS-B, JASA (T&M), Biometrika, Annals of Statistics, Bayesian Analysis, EJS, JCGS. For each: the theoretical results they present (type: consistency of the target, mixing/complexity of the algorithm, finite-sample guarantees, certificates), how many main theorems, how asymptotic, what simulation and real-data evidence they show, and how they handle the gap between asymptotic theory and finite-n simulations. Also search specifically for a-posteriori certificates / verifiable guarantees for individual runs in Bayesian computation. Key question: given our results (see below), which venue tier does our theory + simulation package realistically support, and what is typically expected that we lack?
