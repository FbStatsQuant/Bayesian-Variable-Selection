## Objections

**1. A2’s quantitative conclusion is missing from both representations.**

The source states more than arrival within the initial potential:

\[
\tau(S)=m(S)+e(S)-\sigma(S),
\qquad
\max\{m(S),e(S)\}\le\tau(S)\le\Phi(S),
\]

where `σ` counts extra-to-truth swaps (`S:238–240`).

`Greedy.theoremA2` (`T37Greedy:244–250`) supplies decreasing potential, closure, arrival, and an upper bound. `Lattice.theoremA2_lattice` (`T37Lattice:91–96`) additionally supplies lattice containment. Neither defines or counts `σ`, states the exact identity, or states the lower bound.

This loses the source’s accounting of how swaps shorten the repair path. It is a coverage failure rather than an objection to the existing abstract potential argument. A-T37-1 explains the abstraction but does not record omission of this part of A2.

**Proposed fix:** retain the abstract result as a core lemma and strengthen the concrete theorem. Define `σ` over the actual moves before termination and conclude, preferably without natural-number subtraction,

\[
\tau+\sigma=\operatorname{mis}(S)+\operatorname{ext}(S),
\qquad
\max(\operatorname{mis}(S),\operatorname{ext}(S))\le\tau.
\]

The existing upper bound and lattice conclusion should remain.

**2. A3 and Lemma 0(c) never connect certified repair to the reported HPM.**

The source concludes that the post-search reported HPM is the truth, subject to exact score ties (`S:193–195,241–243`). That conclusion uses the production context: the initial centre is the global best previously scored model (`S:175,189`).

`Greedy.theoremA3` (`T37Greedy:298–301`) establishes certified return and bounds scores of centres and scanned neighbours. `Lattice.theoremA3_lattice` (`T37Lattice:102–105`) establishes only return and certification. Neither statement contains the pre-search scored set, its relationship to the initial centre, the accumulated post-search scored set, or the reported maximiser.

Those missing objects matter. An abstract instance may satisfy every present hypothesis while some separately previously scored model has score greater than `F(S₀)`. Certified repair and the search-only score bound still hold, but that model remains the reported HPM. The source rules this situation out through its initial-centre condition; Lean has no corresponding connection.

**Proposed fix:** introduce the pre-search ledger and the source-justified hypothesis that `Sc` maximises it. Define the accumulated ledger using the centres and scanned neighbourhoods, then conclude that `S₀` maximises this ledger. Connect that result to the reported `IsLeastArgmax`: obtain the appropriate exact-tie qualification, and equality when the tie rule selects `S₀`.

**3. The generality of Lemma 0(c), and A3’s alternative lattice hypothesis, remain uncovered.**

Lemma 0(c) requires that the path reaches the truth and stops there certified (`S:193–195`). It does **not** require progress from every state in a region.

Its nearest Lean representation, `Greedy.theoremA3`, assumes full `TDP M Φ R S₀`. This excludes successful certified paths that contain neutral moves, or whose surrounding region contains unsuccessful starts. The source expressly distinguishes reaching within a potential bound from decreasing potential at every move (`S:255–264`). Consequently, the restricted result cannot stand in for the general Lemma 0(c).

Similarly, the concrete A3 theorem assumes `TDP'` throughout `Rad` (`T37Lattice:84–85,103`), whereas the source also permits the condition solely on `Lat(Sc)` (`S:243–244`). No statement in the packet expresses that alternative using the source’s `D_S` condition. The arbitrary-region abstract framework is suitable for deriving it, but the source-specific application is absent.

The concrete theorem also assumes `ℓ ≥ Φ(Sc)`, rather than expressing the source’s weaker sufficient condition `ℓ ≥ τ(Sc)`. Sharp-cap ingredients exist in the abstract path results; this is an incomplete concrete statement, not an off-by-one error.

**Proposed fix:** separate a general certified-reach lemma from the `TDP` corollary. Add a concrete lattice-only corollary under `(L)` and the source’s one-state progress conditions on `Lat(Sc)`, with the actual move count determining the cap. This would preserve the source’s weaker hypotheses without requiring progress elsewhere in `Rad`.
