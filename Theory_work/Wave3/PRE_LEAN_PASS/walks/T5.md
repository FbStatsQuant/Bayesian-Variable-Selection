## 1. Lemma H (Hellinger reduction; deterministic) — `DIST/STATEMENT.md:322-326`, `DIST/work/B_transfer/H.md`

## 2. Lemma T (distribution-free transfer on sparse directions) — `DIST/STATEMENT.md:328-340`, `DIST/work/B_transfer/T.md`

Claim: for i.i.d. rows from **any** law on $\mathbb R^{p_{\mathrm{raw}}}$, $1\le d\le\min(p_{\mathrm{raw}},n-1)$, with probability $\ge1-\delta$, simultaneously for all $a$, $c>0$ and all $u$ with $|\operatorname{supp}u|\le d$: $E_x\min\{1,(a+u^\top x)^2/c\}\le2n^{-1}\sum_i\min\{1,(a+u^\top x_i)^2/c\}+\eta_n^2(d,\delta)$.

- **Growth function (T.md:79-93).** $\{|a+u^\top x|>r\}$ is the union of two open half-spaces in the $d$ active coordinates; half-spaces in $\mathbb R^d$ have VC dimension $\le d+1$ (Dudley's linear-dependence argument, proved); Sauer–Shelah (proved by the standard induction); so the number of traces on $m\ge d+1$ points is $\le\binom{p_{\mathrm{raw}}}d\Phi_{d+1}(m)^2\le(ep_{\mathrm{raw}}/d)^d(em/(d+1))^{2(d+1)}$; at $m=2n$ this is $e^L$. Checked.
- **Proposition R (T.md:95-132).** Ghost sample: for the first index $\tau$ with $\nu(A_\tau)>2P_n(A_\tau)+\eta^2$, Cantelli gives $P(Z<\tfrac45np)\le1/(1+np/25)\le\tfrac12$ when $n\eta^2\ge25$; $\tau$ is a function of the first sample, $Z$ of the ghost sample, so the product formula is legitimate. Swaps: the condition after the swap is $13W_A>3N_A+8n\eta^2$ (recomputed), Hoeffding with $\|a\|^2\le N_A$ and $(3N+8n\eta^2)^2\ge96Nn\eta^2$ gives $\exp(-48n\eta^2/169)\le\exp(-n\eta^2/4)$; union over at most $e^L$ traces. So $P(\exists A:\nu(A)>2P_n(A)+\eta^2)\le2e^Le^{-n\eta^2/4}=\delta/2$ with $n\eta^2/4=L+\log(4/\delta)$. Checked.
- **Layer cake and limits (T.md:134-170).** $g=\int_0^1\mathbf 1\{g>s\}ds$ and $\{g>s\}=\{|a+u^\top x|>\sqrt{sc}\}$; rational parameters first, real thresholds by monotone limits, real $(a,u)$ by dominated convergence; measurability of the supremum through a countable dense set. Checked.

The sparsity level $d$ must be deterministic — DIST12 D.1 takes care of this (§5). No moment or eigenvalue condition is used. No finding.
