## Statement

Fix n ≥ 3 and a raw design x = (x_1, …, x_n)ᵀ ∈ ℝ^{n×p_raw} with p_ret(x) ≥ 1 retained (non-constant) columns. Let
α* ∈ ℝ, σ* > 0, β* ∈ ℝ^{p_raw}. For a non-constant response vector y^r ∈ ℝⁿ put

  ỹ := (y^r − α*1)/σ*,   equivalently y^r = α*1 + σ*ỹ.

Write Π(· | x, y^r) for the exact posterior of (S, θ_S, v) on the response scale (`STATEMENT.md:29-32`), and for a draw
(S, θ, v) let b(θ; x) ∈ ℝ^{p_raw} (b_j = θ_j/ŝ_j on S, 0 elsewhere and on dropped columns), â(θ; x, y^r) := ȳ^r − x̄ᵀb and
f^{y^r}_{S,θ,v}(x, y) := ν_x(dx) φ(y; â + xᵀb, v) be the objects of `STATEMENT.md:37-39`. Put f* := ν_x(dx)φ(y; α* + xᵀβ*, σ*²)
and f̃* := ν_x(dx)φ(y; xᵀβ̃*, 1), β̃* := β*/σ*. Let T_c(S, θ, v) := (S, cθ, c²v).

(a) ỹ is non-constant, and Π(· | x, y^r) = T_{σ*}♯ Π(· | x, ỹ) (image measure); the model posteriors coincide:
    π(· | x, y^r) = π(· | x, ỹ).
(b) For every (S, θ, v): b(σ*θ; x) = σ*·b(θ; x) and â(σ*θ; x, y^r) = α* + σ*·â(θ; x, ỹ).
(c) For every (S, θ, v) with v > 0:  d_H(f^{y^r}_{T_{σ*}(S,θ,v)}, f*) = d_H(f^{ỹ}_{S,θ,v}, f̃*).
(d) Consequently, for every Borel set B ⊆ [0, 1] and every set 𝒮 of models,
    Π( d_H(f, f*) ∈ B, S ∈ 𝒮 | x, y^r ) = Π( d_H(f, f̃*) ∈ B, S ∈ 𝒮 | x, ỹ ),
    where on the left f is built from (x, y^r) and on the right from (x, ỹ). In particular, for all r ≥ 0, k ∈ ℝ, η ∈ ℝ:
    {Π(d_H(f, f*) > r or |S| > k | x, y^r) > η} = {Π(d_H(f, f̃*) > r or |S| > k | x, ỹ) > η} (as events of the data),
    and 1 − π(S₀ | x, y^r) = 1 − π(S₀ | x, ỹ).
(e) (Law of the reduced data.) If the rows satisfy (RD) (`STATEMENT.md:17-20`) — or the reader assumption (A1)
    (`STATEMENT.md:161-162`) — with (α*, β*, σ*), then the rows (x_i, ỹ_i) satisfy the same model with intercept 0,
    coefficients β̃* = β*/σ* and noise variance 1, the same law ν_x of x, and the same supp β̃* = supp β*. Hence every
    probability P*[𝔈] of an event 𝔈 of the form in (d) equals the probability of the corresponding event in the
    unit-noise model with truth f̃*. σ* may depend on n: the reduction is done at each n and for each data set.
(f) (Translation of the interfaces.) With μ := CX^rβ*, θ* := D_xβ* (retained columns) and μ̃, θ̃* the same objects for
    β̃*: μ̃ = μ/σ*, θ̃* = θ*/σ*, S₀ and s₀ are unchanged, and p_ret, 𝒟_n, (ND; c_D, m), the cutoff m_n(t), (TR₀; C_T)
    and all reader assumptions on x ((A2) dimension part, (A3), (A4), (A6)(a)) involve x and supp β* only, hence are
    unchanged. (E; M), (C; M′), (L₀; B) "in units of σ*²" for the original model are exactly (E; M), (C; M′), (L₀; B) for
    the unit-noise model. (A5) and (A6)(b) read |β̃*_j|·sd(x_j) ≤ b̄·1 and min_{β̃*_j≠0}|β̃*_j|·sd(x_j)/1 ≥ b_n.

So in every statement whose conclusion is an event of the form in (d) one may take α* = 0 and σ* = 1 without loss of
generality, with the interfaces read in units of σ*².

## Proof

**(a).** C1 = 0, so Cỹ = Cy^r/σ* ≠ 0 because y^r is non-constant; hence ỹ is non-constant. Apply R′(a′) to the
non-constant response ỹ with a := α* and c := σ* > 0: since α*1 + σ*ỹ = y^r, Π(· | x, y^r) is the image of Π(· | x, ỹ)
under T_{σ*}. The model marginal of T_c♯Π is the model marginal of Π because T_c does not change S; this is also R′(a)
with (a, c) = (α*, σ*). ∎(a)

**(b).** ŝ_j and the set of retained columns depend on x only. For j ∈ S: b_j(σ*θ; x) = σ*θ_j/ŝ_j = σ*b_j(θ; x); for j ∉ S
or j dropped both sides are 0. The sample mean of y^r = α*1 + σ*ỹ is ȳ^r = α* + σ*ỹ̄, where ỹ̄ := n⁻¹Σ_iỹ_i. Hence
  â(σ*θ; x, y^r) = ȳ^r − x̄ᵀb(σ*θ; x) = α* + σ*ỹ̄ − σ*x̄ᵀb(θ; x) = α* + σ*(ỹ̄ − x̄ᵀb(θ; x)) = α* + σ*â(θ; x, ỹ). ∎(b)

**(c).** *Step 1 (affine invariance of the Hellinger affinity).* Let h(u) := α* + σ*u, a bijection of ℝ with h′ = σ*.
If g is a Lebesgue density on ℝ, the density of h(U), U ~ g, is g_h(y) := g(h⁻¹(y))/σ*. For two densities g₁, g₂, the
substitution y = h(u), dy = σ*du gives
  ∫√(g_{1,h}(y) g_{2,h}(y)) dy = ∫ √(g₁(h⁻¹(y)) g₂(h⁻¹(y))) σ*⁻¹ dy = ∫ √(g₁(u) g₂(u)) du.
If U ~ N(m, w) then h(U) ~ N(α* + σ*m, σ*²w). Hence, for all m₁, m₂ ∈ ℝ and w₁, w₂ > 0,
  H²(N(α* + σ*m₁, σ*²w₁), N(α* + σ*m₂, σ*²w₂)) = H²(N(m₁, w₁), N(m₂, w₂)),  H²(P, Q) := 1 − ∫√(dP dQ).   (B0.1)

*Step 2 (the two densities).* Fix (S, θ, v), v > 0, and write b̃ := b(θ; x), ã := â(θ; x, ỹ). By (b), the draw
T_{σ*}(S, θ, v) = (S, σ*θ, σ*²v) built from (x, y^r) has regression function â(σ*θ; x, y^r) + xᵀb(σ*θ; x) =
α* + σ*(ã + xᵀb̃) and variance σ*²v. The truth has regression function α* + xᵀβ* = α* + σ*(0 + xᵀβ̃*) and variance
σ*²·1. Both f^{y^r}_{T_{σ*}(S,θ,v)} and f* are densities with respect to ν_x ⊗ Lebesgue with the same x-marginal ν_x, so,
by Tonelli,
  1 − d_H²(f^{y^r}_{T_{σ*}(S,θ,v)}, f*) = ∫ν_x(dx) ∫ √(φ(y; α* + σ*(ã + xᵀb̃), σ*²v) φ(y; α* + σ*xᵀβ̃*, σ*²)) dy
   = ∫ν_x(dx) ∫ √(φ(u; ã + xᵀb̃, v) φ(u; xᵀβ̃*, 1)) du                     (by (B0.1), pointwise in x)
   = 1 − d_H²(f^{ỹ}_{S,θ,v}, f̃*).
Since d_H ≥ 0, (c) follows. (Here x̄ and ŝ_j are the sample quantities of the observed design; the new pair (x, y) in
f has x ~ ν_x; they are different variables, as in `STATEMENT.md:45-47`.) ∎(c)

**(d).** Let F(S, θ, v) := 1{d_H(f^{y^r}_{S,θ,v}, f*) ∈ B, S ∈ 𝒮} and F̃(S, θ, v) := 1{d_H(f^{ỹ}_{S,θ,v}, f̃*) ∈ B, S ∈ 𝒮}.
By (c), F ∘ T_{σ*} = F̃ (T_{σ*} keeps S). By (a) and the change-of-variables formula for image measures,
  Π(F | x, y^r) = ∫ F d(T_{σ*}♯Π(· | x, ỹ)) = ∫ F ∘ T_{σ*} dΠ(· | x, ỹ) = ∫ F̃ dΠ(· | x, ỹ).
(Measurability of F, F̃ is the measurability of d_H as a function of the draw, which the master statements already
presuppose; (B0.1) shows F is measurable iff F̃ is.) The event identity follows by taking B = (r, 1] and adding
{|S| > k} to 𝒮 in the obvious way: {d_H > r or |S| > k} is the union of {d_H ∈ (r, 1]} (all S) and {S : |S| > k} (all
d_H), and both sides are computed with the same image-measure identity applied to the indicator of the union. The
last claim is (a) for the model marginal. ∎(d)

**(e).** Under (RD): y^r_i = α* + x_iᵀβ* + ε_i, ε_i ~ N(0, σ*²) independent of the design and i.i.d. Then
ỹ_i = (y^r_i − α*)/σ* = x_iᵀβ̃* + ε̃_i with ε̃_i := ε_i/σ* ~ N(0, 1), independent of the design, and the rows (x_i, ỹ_i) are
i.i.d. (a fixed measurable map applied row by row). supp β̃* = supp β* since σ* > 0. The map y^r ↦ ỹ is a bijection of
ℝⁿ, the data (x, y^r) and (x, ỹ) generate the same σ-field, and by (d) every event of the form in (d) is the same
subset of the sample space whether it is described with y^r or with ỹ; so its P*-probability is its probability under
the law of (x, ỹ), i.e. under the unit-noise model. (A1) is the same model statement as (RD) at the given n. ∎(e)

**(f).** μ̃ = CX^rβ̃* = μ/σ* and θ̃* = D_xβ̃* = θ*/σ* by linearity; supp θ̃* = supp θ*. (E; M) for the original model "in
units of σ*²" means ‖μ‖² ≤ nMσ*², which is ‖μ̃‖² ≤ nM; the same for (C; M′) (‖θ*‖² ≤ nM′σ*²). L_{S₀} is a quadratic form
in μ (`API_CARDS.md:14`: L_S = μ_cᵀ(I − M_S)μ_c with M_S depending on X only), so L̃_{S₀} = L_{S₀}/σ*². The remaining
objects listed are defined from x, from p_ret, s₀ and supp β* only (`STATEMENT.md:23-27, 54-62, 94`; reader
`STATEMENT.md:163-169, 196`). (A5), (A6)(b): divide by σ*. ∎(f)

## Remarks
1. Why a pathwise statement is needed (P1-05): d_H depends on the data also through ȳ^r and x̄ (plug-in intercept), so
   an identity in law for the posterior of (S, θ, v) alone would not transport events of the form in (d); (b) gives
   the intercept identity draw by draw.
2. The reader's posterior of (S, β, σ²) "in the original units" (`STATEMENT.md:139-140`) is the image of Π(· | x, y^r)
   under the x-measurable bijection (S, θ, v) ↦ (S, (θ_j/ŝ_j)_j, v), and the reader's f (`STATEMENT.md:142-144`, mean
   ȳ + (x − x̄)ᵀβ) is f_{S,θ,v} with b = β, because â + xᵀb = ȳ^r + (x − x̄)ᵀb. So (d) holds verbatim for the reader's Π.
3. If y^r is constant the posterior is undefined; under (RD) this has probability 0 (ε has a density on ℝⁿ and
   {ε : y^r constant} is contained in an affine subspace of dimension 1 < n).
4. On {p_ret = 0} the statement is not needed (outside 𝒟_n ⊂ {p_ret ≥ 1}).
