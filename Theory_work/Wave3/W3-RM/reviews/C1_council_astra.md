## Numbered findings

1. **MAJOR — REQUIRED for a pass. Misidentified L2 assumption violations.** Locations: RM/SHARPNESS.md:159; RM/numerics/SUMMARY_SHARPNESS.md:77,116–118; RM/numerics/l2_eta.py:11–15,49–75; compare RM/STATEMENT.md:82–83,95–98. The experiment chooses a *population* proxy correlation through n r²=eta log n and labels eta>eta_A as “outside (L-gain)”. The current fixed-design condition instead concerns realised delta²/(tau_U² log n), uniformly over conditioning sets, with a fixed A>=1. Here theta=1 and sigma²=1, so for the standardised sample design tau_empty²=2 exactly and delta_empty,l²=(n−1) rhat_lj². Thus the actual empty-set normalised gain is (n−1) rhat_lj²/(2 log n), not eta. Even replacing the sample correlation by its population target gives (1−1/n)eta/2: the cell labelled 2eta_A is near the tau-normalised boundary, not twice that boundary. For n=p=400, the nominal normalised value is 0.100768, below eta_A=0.101021. These numbers were recomputed in memory during this review. Further, the script sets A=log(p)/log(n) separately per cell (often below 1), and its stored quantities do not certify the sample all-U inequality or a witness to its failure. Neither the 418/420 “outside” classification nor the “control runs” classification is thereby established for v1.2. Random Gaussian design fluctuations also preclude equating population gain with the fixed-design hypothesis. **Fix:** relabel the existing data as an exploratory population-proxy-strength experiment with its nominal, cellwise threshold; remove claims that these counts certify violation/satisfaction of v1.2 L-gain or locate its threshold. This needs no rerun. Alternatively choose and document a fixed admissible A and compute valid sample violation witnesses (and appropriate verification for any claimed controls), then regenerate the classifications. Do not infer a full-method counterexample. The counts of algorithmic outcomes themselves need not change.
