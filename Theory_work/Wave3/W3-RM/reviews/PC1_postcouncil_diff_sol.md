## Direct verification and attempted breakage

### B1: visibility for GEN C6

Source design: Theory_work/Wave1/W1-GEN/lemmas/L7_counterexample.md:14–36. Its Gram representation has x0 along e0, proxies along (a/sqrt(d))e0 + sqrt(1-a^2/d)e_i, every other truth along its own e'_k, and remaining nulls along f_k. The exact-Gram map preserves these inner products. The source proves the full Gram floor 1-a>0 at :25–31.

Fix any j in S0 and any U not containing j, |U|<=15, exactly as STATEMENT.md:95–96 requires. Write r_j=(I-P_U)x_j and u_j=r_j/||r_j||.

1. If j=x0, split the other truths k between those in U and those not in U. For k in U, r_j is orthogonal to x_k by the definition of the residual. For k not in U, x_k is orthogonal to x0 and every column of X_U (there is no copy of x_k among those columns); hence it is orthogonal to r_j in span(x0,X_U). This handles U containing proxies, other truths, nulls, or a mixture. The phrase "or contained in U" in the edited text is essential: a truth in U is not orthogonal to that span, but is orthogonal to the residual.
2. If j is any other truth, x_j is orthogonal to every column other than itself. Thus P_U x_j=0 for every allowed U, including U containing proxies of x0 but not x0. This is precisely the case that broke the old argument. Then u_j=x_j/sqrt(n-1), orthogonal to every other truth.
3. In both cases u_j' mu=theta u_j'x_j=theta ||r_j||. The Gram floor implies ||r_j||^2 >= (n-1)(1-a): minimise ||x_j-X_U b||^2 >= (n-1)(1-a)(1+||b||^2) over b. Nonzero residuals and the lower bound follow simultaneously. For the nonexceptional truths the exact value is n-1.
4. Consequently delta^2 >= theta^2(n-1)(1-a). For fixed nonzero theta and a in (0,1), M_n=theta^2(n-1)(1-a)/log n tends to infinity. This verifies (V) uniformly, with the same constant used in SHARPNESS.md:80. No sign restriction on theta was silently introduced: the square removes it.

Downstream, Q is S0 plus proxies. All columns outside Q are orthogonal to mu and all other columns, so their conditional gain is zero for every allowed U, satisfying the tau-normalised condition with eta=0. The same full floor supplies FL-16 and FL-Q. |Q|=s0+d=O(log n), |Q|<=K and K>=2|Q|+32s0+1 eventually; the source's energy and spectral conditions give T1-F. Conditional on S0, the proxy residuals lie in mutually orthogonal e_i directions and the outside nulls are orthogonal to span(X_Q). Thus L2_NAF.md:77–79 supplies p exact directions, rho=0,h=0; BM-Q diverges since n/(d+log n) tends to infinity. Applying L1_fixed.md:109–117 and STATEMENT C1 is legitimate. The claim continues to use N-D, not N-chi: d=16(2 ell_n+5) is much larger than log n. The edited proof does not accidentally put this example in the old-cap exception.

The dependent claims in ASSUMPTION_MAP.md:48, SHARPNESS.md:86–87 and REPORT.md:64,126,137–138 therefore survive. There is no new false family-wide claim in L7_examples.md; its unrelated masked GEN cases remain distinguished at :89.
