# R08 — exhaustive sweep of W3-RM v1.2

Independent reviewer: Codex gpt-6-astra, high. No subagents. Only this review file was written; no git operations or source edits were performed. Analytic sharpness content was reviewed; the pending numerical appendix is not counted as a defect. Verdict: **REVISE**. No counterexample to (C1) or (C2) under their full hypotheses was found. The important defects concern necessity/sharpness claims and the citation checker, rather than the principal proof chains. Source statuses below are retained, not upgraded.

## Findings

1. **MAJOR — ASSUMPTION_MAP.md:19. Posterior concentration is incorrectly called essentially necessary.** The assertion “without posterior concentration S₀ is not the F-maximiser” is false: being the largest individual posterior mass does not require that mass to tend to one. For example masses .4, .3, .3 have a unique mode without concentration. Even the sufficient event π(S₀) > 1/2 does not require concentration. Neither T1 nor POST is necessary for the deterministic scored-set argument. **Fix:** replace the two necessity cells by “sufficient input used here; the pathwise argument only needs S₀ to be the unique F-maximiser among scored models with probability tending to one; necessity of T1/POST is not established.”

2. **MAJOR — ASSUMPTION_MAP.md:25; SHARPNESS.md:79–83. An LS0 counterexample is promoted to necessity of an unsaturated start / |Q| < K.** The map explicitly says “Necessary: |Q| < K.” The theorem itself permits Q = [p] = K. For fixed p with an orthogonal design and nonzero fixed signals, that choice satisfies the theorem and the conclusion holds. Saturation is not inherently a failure mechanism: starting at S₀ when s₀ = p = K is already success, and saturation with extras does not by itself force swaps. FAC-PK NEG proves failure on its particular class under (PK), (PB), not necessity of the present feasibility inequality. **Fix:** state only that the cited class supplies a counterexample to an unrestricted extension to saturated starts when K < p; retain the complete NEG hypotheses and distinguish that existential obstruction from a necessary condition on every successful design.

3. **MAJOR — SHARPNESS.md:24–29, 65–67 (definition at :4–6). “Not class-sharp” does not follow from successful designs outside an assumption.** The file defines class-sharpness by existence of a failing design just outside the assumption. Existence of successful masked-truth or large-Q designs does not refute existence of failing designs elsewhere in that class. This confuses non-necessity for particular designs with a worst-case converse. **Fix:** replace both “not class-sharp” conclusions with “not necessary for every successful design; these results do not settle a uniform worst-case converse.” Use that distinction consistently in the registry sharpness record.

4. **MAJOR — SHARPNESS.md:105–110; lemmas/L2_NAF.md:81–84. The claimed nonimplication of (N-D)(i) lacks a proof.** The only offered example is a numerical specification. Neither the stated lattice-sup approximation 2 log p + c m, c ≈ .40, nor its claimed crossover m ≳ 2.5 log n is proved there. Distinct residual directions alone do not preclude an admissible approximate net; exponentially many directions can cluster. Exceeding c_n is also not by itself proof of an actual NA violation, since the score conversion is one-way. The probe has a tuned angle and fixed finite p in SPEC, which cannot silently become an asymptotic counterexample. **Fix:** label the entire N1 argument heuristic, and replace the categorical nonimplication in L2 by “not established from the current hypotheses.” Alternatively supply an asymptotic sequence, verify the floors and V/L, and prove either the required covering lower bound or a sufficiently strong Gaussian supremum lower bound; prove score violation separately if claimed. Include the angle-dependent calculation behind .40 and 2.5.

5. **MAJOR — SHARPNESS.md:111–116. The claimed necessity of path-NA is false.** For an extra e and an eligible l, the exact identity is F(T−e+l)−F(T−e) = F((T−e)+l)−F(T−e). It compares that swap with that particular deletion, not with the best deletion at T. A swap can beat deleting e while another deletion beats the swap. Also T−e need not be a visited path set unless e is the selected deletion. Strict failure of NA can be merely a tie, which does not “beat” the deletion. **Fix:** write “NA on all deletion children T−e is a sufficient way to make every such swap worse than its corresponding deletion; it is stronger than necessary for a deletion-only greedy path.” Formulate any proposed path condition in terms of the maximum swap/addition score versus the maximum deletion score. FAC-PK PATH is a valid example of successful non-deletion paths, not a proof of this equivalence.

6. **MAJOR — citecheck.py:79–105. Resolution can silently accept the wrong file.** Before interpreting directory or ticket context, :82–83 discard all relative path components for a basename in KNOWN. Thus an erroneous `wrong-directory/L1_toolkit.md:28-32`, or an explicitly different ticket's relative `lemmas/L1_toolkit.md`, is redirected to LOC2 and can receive OK. This contradicts the advertised context-resolution rule. The `../` rule also strips exactly one prefix into the ticket root regardless of the source directory. **Fix:** preserve and normalize explicit relative paths against the citing file; handle explicit ticket-qualified shorthand first; permit a basename alias only for a genuinely bare name with an unambiguous declared mapping; reject conflicting context. Add read-only resolver checks for nonexistent-directory and conflicting-ticket examples. The present CITECHECK result is a syntactic range check, not assurance of source identity.

7. **MINOR — SHARPNESS.md:16–22; ASSUMPTION_MAP.md:23. B1/B1′ do not prove the necessity rhetoric about 15.** Their equivalence over all partitions into blocks of size at most 16 is correct, including p < 16. It does not prove that every smaller-conditioning alternative theorem fails, or that every such partition is realizable by the shipped clustering. The actual bound is B−1, not always 15; for small p the file itself acknowledges this. **Fix:** say “15 is the largest conditioning size our uniform blockwise proof must cover when B = 16. B1′ establishes equivalence of these particular universal conditions, not a necessity theorem for clustering or consistency.” For the small-p parenthesis use the full min formula for B, or explicitly say eventually in n.

8. **MINOR — SHARPNESS.md:21–22. Quantitative visibility is not proved route-necessary even at realised selections.** M_n → ∞ is a sufficient way to dominate the log n score cost; a suitably large constant multiple of log n can already dominate that cost. Thus the statement that “(V) is route-necessary ... only at the realised selections” is stronger than the proof establishes. Joint visibility, discussed immediately afterwards, supplies another reason not to use that wording. **Fix:** “Only comparisons at selected block subsets enter this proof; the stated uniform diverging visibility bound is sufficient and is not shown necessary even for Sand.”

9. **MINOR — SHARPNESS.md:100–104; ASSUMPTION_MAP.md:27. Cauchy–Schwarz does not establish the advertised route-sharpness constant.** The source proves Z² ≤ Z²_reference + χ²_Q and an upper probability bound. Equality in its first Cauchy–Schwarz step does not show simultaneous attainability after the projection-norm bound, maximization over pairs, and separate Gaussian/χ² tail bounds. **Fix:** state “the displayed common-χ² upper bound gives the coefficient-one log n sufficient range; its optimality as a bound for actual residual families is not established here.” A route-sharp claim needs a realizable family attaining the relevant upper envelope.

10. **MINOR — SHARPNESS.md:56–61. The VN paraphrase drops a source hypothesis.** APP L5_VN:23 begins “Under (F16) and VNF(κ_n)”; visibility of the nulls alone is not the cited lemma. **Fix:** add “under APP's Setting and (F16), with VNF(κ_n)” and state or precisely link the floor condition. Keep the correct LS0/full-method limitation already quoted at :60–61.

11. **MINOR — SHARPNESS.md:68–78. The GEN application omits the BM-Q verification and overstates its review status.** H0 supplies BM-∞, not automatically BM-Q for arbitrary polylogarithmic Q. The specific source design has fixed θ, so the missing verification is easy: θ² n φ₀²/[σ²(s₀+d_n+log n)] → ∞ for any fixed polylog envelope. Also “checked in the sweep” anticipates the independent verdict. **Fix:** cite GEN L7_counterexample:14–36, use its uniform floor 1−a, spell out fixed θ, bounded energy, p=n−1, K=floor(n/2), eventual K≥2(s₀+d_n)+32s₀+1, and BM-Q; distinguish the source's d_n=16(2ℓ_n+5) from the proposed extension to other polylog rates. Replace the anticipated status by the actual review citation after disposition. The displayed Schur complement is algebraically correct. Positive Schur complement alone is not a quantitative eigenfloor proof, but source W1 supplies precisely the required uniform floor. Special case (c) of NA-F is applicable: residual proxies given S₀ are orthogonal and the remaining nulls are orthogonal to Q. H0 really is proved in source W2; this is not an invalid GEN example.

12. **MINOR — SHARPNESS.md:42–47. The heuristic converse formula has unstated range and scaling assumptions.** The asserted “diverges iff η > η_A” is false for the displayed squared-difference exponent over unrestricted η: for sufficiently large η it becomes negative again, whereas the exceedance probability tends to one. Also p≤n^A does not imply p≈n^A, and noise standardized by τ has variance σ²/τ², not always one. **Fix:** explicitly specialize the heuristic to log p/log n→A and τ/σ→1 and use the positive part [√(2A+1)−√η]_+ in the exponent. Keep “heuristic, converse open.”

13. **MINOR — lemmas/L1_fixed.md:34–38. A proxy outside its truth's block is unconditionally described as entering the start mode.** Large marginal gain, without a quantitative comparison to the prior and determinant penalties and to the other block models, does not imply selection. Conditioning on other proxies can also remove it. **Fix:** replace “harmful otherwise (l then enters ... and must be in Q)” with “may then have to be included in Q to satisfy the chosen leakage condition; actual selection depends on the score comparisons.”

14. **MINOR — lemmas/L1_fixed.md:93–94. B4 uses an undefined c_n.** Its displayed probability conclusion contains c_n without defining it in this file or in STATEMENT; the reader must infer the definition from an external source. **Fix:** copy c_n := log(n+1)+2 log p−2 log(|Q|+1)−4κ_E−2 into B4, with κ_E the toolkit remainder constant.

15. **MINOR — citecheck.py:28–29, 130–151, 185–189. Coverage and process-success claims are too weak for the advertised audit.** ASSUMPTION_MAP is omitted from SRC. Bare continuation citations such as `:465` and `:473` in STATEMENT's integration paragraph are not extracted. Multiline paths are not parsed as full paths (the VN reference is rescued by a basename alias). Finally detected failures are printed but the script never exits nonzero. **Fix:** include the map, parse citations as complete logical references with explicit continuation ownership, report unsupported syntax, and exit with a failure code when any check fails. Do not describe 265 extracted ranges as all references. The status-token search is explicitly documented as existence-only and does not validate attachment to a particular theorem.
