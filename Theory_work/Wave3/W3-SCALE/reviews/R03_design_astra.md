VERDICT: DESIGN_v1 UNSOUND — the cross-fitted identity uses the wrong normalization, several numerical claims fail recomputation, and the parallel execution/reporting contract remains incomplete.

1. **CRITICAL — §6 O1: the cross-fitted identity is false with the document's W.** §4.3 defines W_r = Zhat_r / sum Zhat_s. Set h = 1: the asserted E[W_r H_r(h) | V,U_-r] = 1 cannot hold for positive evidence and R' > 1. `Wave1/W1-N1/STATEMENT.md:104` instead defines W_r = Zhat_r/Z; f01(b)(iii) inherits that notation. Fix: distinguish normalized merge weights from Zhat/Z. Prove the ratio SLLN/CLT separately; do not claim exact orthogonality for normalized island contributions. Cross-fitting alone also does not make mutually cross-dependent, leave-one-out errors orthogonal: each selected h can depend on the other error's stream.

2. **CRITICAL — §1: “within 5% on the three profiles” is contradicted by the stated constants.** Using N(p+16)(T+21.6) microseconds and the actual final T in `results/profile_v31_stages.tsv` gives 120.904, 275.184, 2532.024 s at p = 1k, 2k, 20k. `profile_v31_step_cost.tsv` reports 124.9, 308.4, 2382.6 s: errors -3.20%, -10.77%, +6.27%. Profile-specific measured constants approximately reconstruct the profiles, but that is not validation of the common constants. Fix: report the residuals and use an empirical range. The claim that islands cost one third of v0 also ignores terminal steps: at T=30, 51.6/(42*3)=0.410, not 1/3.

3. **CRITICAL — §2(1): N=p costs do not follow its T=35 formula.** At 5k/10k/20k the stated constants give **0.3943/1.5747/6.2939 h**, not 0.42/1.7/6.8. Fix: identify all assumptions and use one set of constants. The incomplete 5k profile cannot validate its extrapolated full-fit time. Escalation costs are not a universal “at least 4” multiplier on the whole fit: setup is unchanged and tier-specific grids/rebuild counts can shrink.

4. **CRITICAL — §§1,5: measured RSS is mislabeled and equated with an allocation formula.** `analysis/profile_table.R` computes `peak_*_MB = peak_kb/1024`, i.e. MiB, but §1 treats 9449.1 MiB as 9.45 GB and writes “=24p²”. Fix units throughout, including the setup comparison's 6.41 figure when its raw record is supplied. Dense allocation calculations 6.4/40/160 GB for 16p² and 3.2/80 GB for 8p² are decimal GB; do not compare them to MiB/1000.

6. **MAJOR — §§4.4,6 O2: the experiment does not establish 1/sqrt(N) scaling.** `sd_logZ_long.tsv` compares repeated adaptive diagnostic grids/streams with a single final four-island set, not matched independent runs at two N on a frozen grid. The S5 tier-1 diagnostic SD 0.3119 at H=2048 versus final 0.9973 at N=10000 also prevents treating larger N as an observed monotone improvement.

7. **MAJOR — §§1,4.4: unsupported tail explanation and extrapolation.** “Far from degenerate at p<=2k” generalizes from one replicate in 24 PROD-H cells, one tie fit and one 2k profile; the weighting table contains **no 2k fit**. 9. **MAJOR — §6 O1/O3: the strengthening needs qualifications beyond “no new mathematics.”** Conditioning on all calibration streams is supported by f01 under SI, but does not follow by tower property from the weaker frozen-object identity: independence supplies the stronger proof, and tower recovers the weaker one. Dropping B_h needs f01(a)(1)'s integrability condition for ordinary conditional expectations, or an explicitly regular-conditional-law interpretation. Stage-t identities use Z_t and q_phi_t, not final Z and pi. The L+5 population extension does not automatically prove the identity for the shipped Gibbs RB statistic. Fix: state that bridge explicitly. A V-measurable R' protects first moments, not by itself an R'→infinity theorem: specify a sequence of budgets/conditional iid islands and moment assumptions. Given fixed data and V, bounded evidence and finite h supply the usual CLT; changing V,N,p across budgets requires a separate triangular-array argument.

10. **MAJOR — §§4.3,7(ii): finite-R ratio bias and actual efficiency are missing.** For A=Zhat H, B=Zhat and mu=pi(h), the leading conditional ratio bias is [mu Var(B|V)-Cov(A,B|V)]/(R' Z²), generally nonzero. Its CLT variance is Var(A-mu B|V)/Z², not simply the equal-weight variance inflated by R'/R_eff. Evidence and PIPs are correlated. The displayed SE is a first-order sandwich estimate; with equal weights it is (R'-1)/R' times the usual unbiased variance estimate of a mean. Fix: label it asymptotic, consider the R'/(R'-1) correction, declare minimum sample size and report conditional uncertainty. Equal-weight averaging can also be consistent when individual islands are unbiased; “only” evidence weighting is consistent is too categorical. No result here establishes decreasing finite-N bias merely from N=p as p grows.

11. **MAJOR — §§4.1,4.5,6 O5: no worker or census stream contract yet.** `v3_run.R:89-93` hardcodes four islands and production budgets. `v3_core.cpp:443-452` starts each invocation at r=1/stream 10; launching it with one island in every array task repeats the same stream unless a global island-id entry point is added. It also runs final local search/LS0 internally. Census IDs must be disjoint from a potentially growing final range 10..9+R', with explicit bounds/domain separation, not merely described as “new ids”. Fix: define worker inputs, global ID, immutable frozen hash, table/cache initialization, island-only execution, output schema and stream namespace. A counter-based generator is not necessary for serial islands, but existing SplitMix-seeded stream labels do not discharge the expanded IND-T premise. Shared frozen calibration/stores cause no additional conditional dependence if they never steer proposals; unconditionally equal-weight islands can retain a shared-calibration variance floor. Audit that separation rather than asserting unconditional independence.

12. **MAJOR — §§4.1,4.3,6 O10/O17: union stores do not define a complete merge.** `v3_ledger.h:43-113` retains top-(40p+1), two top-20p leader stores, only the current HPM provenance and aggregate counters; it is not an all-score archive. Define a canonical event order consistent with serial island-major execution, not an unspecified minimum key; general top entries lack provenance. Fix: specify schema, truncation/deduplication, counts, provenance and the precise output-equivalence class.

13. **MAJOR — §§4,5,7: cost tables omit an unbounded merge and serial tail.** At p=1M, R'=256, worker top stores alone can contain 256*(40p+1) ≈10.24 billion entries before deduplication, plus leaders; eagerly unioning them is infeasible. Even the p-by-R double PIP matrix is 2.048 GB; five RB arrays add about 10.24 GB before R object overhead. `v3_core.cpp:455-481` then runs two serial local searches and start-mode computation; `v3_engine.h:379-419` scans p+k(p-k) candidates per pass. At k=100,p=1M this is 100,990,000 candidates per scan. Small measured LS0 times (0.108,0.159,2.059 s) provide no large-p bound, especially after removing Gram or on tie-DP cases. Fix: budget serialization, I/O, bounded streaming merge, reconstruction, local searches and failure admission, not only final-island core-hours. The claimed post-Gram 1–2 GB island residual is an unsourced engineering target; tables and their R copies depend strongly on block structure.
