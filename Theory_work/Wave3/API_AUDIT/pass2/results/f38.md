- **(ii) Determinant ratio (lines 25-27).** I verified the Schur-complement identity directly.

  - P_H and P_A are functions of X_{S₀}X_{S₀}ᵀ, so they commute. On the eigenspace with eigenvalue λ ≥ 0 the eigenvalues of I−P_H and I−P_A are 1/((n+1)λ+1) ≤ 1/(λ+1); on the orthogonal complement both equal 1. Hence a := uᵀ(I−P_H)u ≤ b := uᵀ(I−P_A)u.
  - Then [1+(n+1)a]/[1+b] ≤ [1+(n+1)b]/[1+b] ≤ n+1. No rank condition on G_{S₀} is needed, since H_{S₀} and G_{S₀}+I are always invertible.
