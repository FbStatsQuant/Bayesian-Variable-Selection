## W2-LOC2:CT_d (L3_CTd.md:24-28, proof 30-41; packing fact 15-21)

Checked:
- **Packing fact (L3_CTd.md:17-20).** Open balls B(x_j, ½) are disjoint under separation ≥ 1 and lie in B(x_i, R+½) when d_ij < R. The volume ratio gives (2R+1)^d, and R = m+1 gives (2m+3)^d for the shell. Correct.
- **Properties of S (line 30-33).** Each term e^{−(a−t)m}(1 − e^{−t(m+1)}) is a product of two nonnegative nondecreasing functions of t. Uniform convergence on [0, a−ε] follows from the majorant K(2m+3)^d e^{−εm}. S(0) = 0. Divergence as t ↑ a follows from the partial-sum limit ≥ K(1−e^{−2a})Σ_{m≤M}(2m+3)^d. Hence {S ≤ α/2} = [0, t*] with 0 < t* < a. Correct.
- **Conjugation (line 34-36).** E_ik = A_ik(e^{t*(φ_i−φ_k)} − 1), E_ii = 0, and |E_ik| ≤ K e^{−a d_ik}(e^{t* d_ik} − 1) by the 1-Lipschitz property of φ. Correct.
- **Schur test (line 37-39).** On the shell m ≤ d < m+1, e^{−(a−t*)d} ≤ e^{−(a−t*)m} and 1 − e^{−t*d} ≤ 1 − e^{−t*(m+1)}. Shells start at m = 1 because d_ik ≥ 1. Row sums are ≤ S(t*) ≤ α/2. E is not symmetric, but the entrywise bound is, so column sums obey the same bound and ‖E‖₂ ≤ α/2. Correct.
- **Inversion (line 40-41).** ‖A⁻¹E‖ ≤ ½ gives ‖A_t⁻¹‖ ≤ 2/α. The (i, j) entry of DA⁻¹D⁻¹ is (A⁻¹)_ij e^{t* d_ij} because φ(x_j) = 0. Correct; the diagonal case is trivially covered by 1/α ≤ 2/α.

No upper spectral bound is used, and the bound is uniform in |I| and the site map. No findings.

## W2-LOC2:TAIL (L3_CTd.md:77-80, proof 81-85)

Checked:
- **(b).** e_{T′} = e_T + D with D a linear function of W_T, and e_T is uncorrelated with W_T. Variances therefore add. Correct.
- **(a), representation of D.** D is the projection of e_{T′} onto span(W_T) = span(W_{T′}) ⊕ span(V), and e_{T′} ⊥ W_{T′}. So D = bᵀM⁻¹V with b_v = Cov(e_{T′}, V_v) = Cov(W_l, W_v | W_{T′}) and M = Cov(W_{T∖T′} | W_{T′}). Then Var D = bᵀM⁻¹b ≤ ‖b‖²/λ_min(M). The derivation is self-contained on the page, so the citation of APP L3.6 Step 3 is not needed.
- **Use of CD_d.** CD_d(b) with U = T′ and V = T∖T′ ⊆ [p]∖T′ gives λ_min(M) ≥ c. CD_d(a) with U = T′ and j = l, k = v (both outside T′, distinct) gives |b_v| ≤ K₂e^{−t₂d_lv}. Both are applied within hypotheses.
- **Shell sum.** d_lv > r implies ⌊d_lv⌋ ≥ ⌊r⌋, and d_lv ≥ 1 implies m ≥ 1. Each shell has at most (2m+3)^d sites with |b_v|² ≤ K₂²e^{−2t₂m}. This gives exactly κ(r), which tends to 0 as the tail of a convergent series.
- **Edge cases.** If T∖T′ = ∅ then D = 0. If r < 1 then T′ = ∅ and the bound still holds.

No findings.
