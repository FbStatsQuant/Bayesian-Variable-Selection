4. **(A1-post) against LSF** (STATEMENT.md:41-45). The definition of LSF is not in the quoted excerpt of L1_POST, so I could not compare it verbatim. I checked instead that (A1-post) is consistent with everything POST(a) says it delivers:

   - No upper spectral bound is imposed, and none appears among POST(a)'s listed outputs.
   - The parenthetical "Sufficient: λ_min(Σ_n) ≥ c" is correct: take φ² = ψ_n = c; principal submatrices inherit the floor.
   
   I found no inconsistency.
