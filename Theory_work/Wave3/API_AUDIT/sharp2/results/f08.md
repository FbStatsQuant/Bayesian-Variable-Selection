## W3-START2:START-F — (T1⁺), STATEMENT.md:93-110

The BASE files are not in the bundle, so points that depend on BASE definitions are hedged where they arise.

**(a) Hypotheses**

- **(AL) is asked of a larger functional than the proof uses.** The smallness condition (ϱ_n(Q; h) → 0 in (a)–(c), ϱ_n ≤ c₀ in (d), STATEMENT.md:104) is on the partition-free 𝔏_n(S₀; h), which maximises over every U ⊆ [p]∖{l} with |U| ≤ 15. The proof uses only the block-relative quantities:
  - the leading term uses the actual conditioning set U_l = U*_{b(l)} (P1_LEADING.md:41, :61);
  - the second-order term uses U ≤ λn^{−1/2}𝔏^b_n(S₀; h), and the partition-free bound enters only through (B1)(i) (P1_LEADING.md:102);
  - "small gains" needs only J_{h_l}(δ_l, τ_l) ≤ p√n at the actual U_l (P1_LEADING.md:50-52).

  The partition is a function of X (STATEMENT.md:33), so the block-relative functionals are deterministic in Setting F and are legitimate hypotheses.
- **The gap is material.** For s₀ ≤ 15 the partition-free maximum includes U = S₀ for every null l, so 𝔏_n(Q; h) ≥ √(2/π)·p⁻¹Σ_{l∉Q}h_l ≍ √(log p) with default levels. It also includes U = ∅ ∈ 𝒰 (P1_LEADING.md:14), where J ≥ ¼·exp(δ²_{∅,l}/(2τ²_∅)) by (J0). A null in the same block as the truth it is correlated with has a small actual gain but an exponentially large partition-free term. The partition-free hypothesis therefore excludes exactly the designs that blocking is meant to handle.
- **Visibility and κ_V.** The threshold c_V > 1 + 2A is matched by (N3) up to the gap between Ā and A̲ (STATEMENT.md:167-168). κ_V > ½ in (c) is needed in the worst case, because the truth-deletion odds are of order n^{−κ_V} against T₁ ≍ n^{−1/2}. I see no slack.
- **(GF-16).** An unnormalised floor is already far weaker than the sparse-eigenvalue conditions of Narisetty–He or Yang–Wainwright–Jordan. Its necessity is open (REPORT.md:114); I cannot judge it.
- **Fixed s₀, p ≤ n^A, Gaussian noise.** The literature (Castillo–Schmidt-Hieber–van der Vaart, Narisetty–He, Yang–Wainwright–Jordan) allows growing s₀ and, as I recall, log p = o(n), also with Gaussian noise.
  - The constants in s₀ are explicit: C^♯ and c₀ at P1_LEADING.md:82-84, and (4/3)^{16s₀+1} at :101. This suggests s₀ = o(log n) is trackable, but the 16-block structure and the s₀-dependence of n₀ and κ_E are in files I cannot see. Unsure.
  - Non-Gaussian noise looks hard, because exp(Z²/2) sits at the edge of integrability (P1_LEADING.md:74-75).

**(b) Conclusion**

- **Exponent ½.** Sharp for the coded method: T₁ ≥ ½(n+1)^{−1/2} deterministically (T0). It matches the n^{−1/2} Bayes-factor rate against one spurious covariate for local priors (Johnson–Rossell). Yang–Wainwright–Jordan obtain a p^{−1}-type mass bound, as I recall, only through a different prior and g, which is a method change.
- **Constant C(ε).** The 1/ε growth in the leading term is essentially necessary for bounded p, since P(e^{Z²/2} > t) ≍ 1/(t√log t) for an exceptional column. The 1/ε² in C^♯ comes from the U² term.
- **The leading functional is not the sharpest one the proof gives.** Step 4 bounds E V by p⁻¹Σ_{l∈𝒩_prop}J_{h_l}(δ_l, τ_l) and then relaxes to the maximum over all proper U (P1_LEADING.md:61).
  - Lemma LEAK-0 as stated (STATEMENT.md:82-89) is therefore incomparable with the block-relative T₁ clause of [LEAK(S₀)] it claims to sharpen: 𝔏^♯ can be exponentially larger than 𝔏^{b,0}, assuming 𝔏^{b,0} is block-relative as the notation at STATEMENT.md:86 suggests.
  - The lower bound (C2)(i) is on the actual partition (STATEMENT.md:165-166), so only a block-relative upper bound can match it.
- **Clause (a) does not receive the P1 device.** At Q = S₀ it gives (s₀+1)n^{−1/2}𝔏_n(S₀; h) ≳ n^{−1/2}√(log p), which is weaker than (b) by √(log p). For Q ⊋ S₀ the same factor is carried by every column, although only the at most 15 nulls sharing a block with all truths can have a conditioning set containing S₀ (P1_LEADING.md:30-32).
- **Where P1 itself matters.** 𝔏⁰/𝔏^♯ ≤ 1 + 60√(2/π)z_n/(p − s₀), so the v1.4 gain is a rate gain only for p = O(√log n). In the p ≫ n regime the statement is rate-equivalent to v1.3.4.
- **s₀ = 0 (open item O3).** For near-orthogonal nulls, p⁻¹Σe^{Z_l²/2} is of order √(log p) in probability (truncated-mean scaling of a tail-index-1 variable). So 𝔏⁰ is order-sharp there and no general improvement exists. This is a heuristic, not checked in detail.
- **Leaking regime (𝔏^♯ → ∞).** No matching lower bound is given. The truncated mean can exceed the typical value when few columns leak or when the Z_l are strongly dependent. The level vectors h mitigate this.

**(c) Cheapest route — easy**

- Define 𝔏^{b,♯}_n(h) := p⁻¹[Σ_{l∈𝒩_prop}J_{h_l}(δ_{U*_{b(l)},l}, τ_{U*_{b(l)}}) + |𝒩_exc|].
- Display (1.1) with Step 4 (P1_LEADING.md:60-62) gives T₁ ≤ λ(s₀+1)n^{−1/2}𝔏^{b,♯} on the same event.
- State (b) and (d) with 𝔏^{b,♯} and 𝔏^b, and (AL) with ϱ^b. The present claim follows, since 𝔏^{b,♯} ≤ 61·𝔏^♯ by P1_LEADING.md:16-17.
- Applying the same split (proper columns by Markov, at most 15 exceptional columns at a fixed cut-off) to U_Q removes the √(log p) from clause (a). This needs a re-run of the BASE Markov step for 𝓜 and is a modest step beyond the first.
- Hard: a deterministic first-order constant for √n·T₁ (a law of large numbers over columns as p → ∞), and growing s₀.

## W3-START2:F2 — F2⁺, STATEMENT.md:112-117

**(a) Hypotheses**

- **The gain condition is a supremum where an average suffices.** "δ²_{U,l} ≤ η′Δ_U log n for every l" is used only to bound each summand of 𝔏^♯_n through (J1) (P1_LEADING.md:133-135). The condition p⁻¹Σ_{l∉S₀}max_{U⊊S₀}exp(δ²_{U,l}/(2Δ_U)) ≤ Cn^{η′/2} gives the same conclusion.
  - This matters. For generic (for example i.i.d.) designs max_l δ²_{∅,l}/Δ_∅ ≈ 2 log p, so the supremum condition with η′ < 1 fails once p ≳ √n, which covers all of p ≫ n.
  - η′ = 0 requires exact orthogonality of every null to every partial residual signal. The exact-order clause needs only 𝔏^♯_n = O(1).
- **The second-order hypothesis is stronger than the rate clause needs.** n^{−1/2}𝔏_n(S₀)² → 0 is used at P1_LEADING.md:138-139 only to get n^{−1}𝔏_n² = O(n^{−(1−η′)/2}) and (AL). 𝔏_n(S₀) = O(n^{(1+η′)/4}) suffices for both; for η′ = 0, O(n^{1/4}) in place of o(n^{1/4}). The block-relative 𝔏^b would also do, as under START-F.
- **Fixed θ_lo and φ₀.** They are used only for Δ_U ≥ φ₀²θ_lo² in the prefactor C_F (P1_LEADING.md:132-133). A direct floor on Δ_U for U ⊊ S₀ suffices, and it may vanish: the rate is then n^{−(1−η′)/2}·√(1 + σ²/Δ_lo,n).
  - For exact n^{−1/2} with p → ∞ a floor of order Δ_U ≳ σ²/log p is genuinely needed, since J_{z_n}(0, τ) ≍ min(√(τ²/Δ), z_n). A fixed θ_lo is not.
  - Visibility already supplies the usual √(log p/n) beta-min of Castillo et al., Narisetty–He and Yang et al.

**(b) Conclusion**

- **η′ = 0.** Order n^{−1/2} is exact by (T0); sharp.
- **η′ > 0.** The exponent is not sharp when p is small, which is the regime F2⁺ newly advertises. (J1) is the untruncated mean, whose mass sits at z* = δσ/Δ_U. When z* > z_n, i.e. roughly Δ_U < η′σ²·log n/(2 log p), the truncated J_{z_n} is polynomially smaller.
  - For p = n^{o(1)}: J_{z_n}(δ, τ) ≤ exp((|δ| + σz_n)²/(2τ²)) = n^{η′Δ_U/(2τ_U²)+o(1)}.
  - So o₀ = O_P(n^{−1/2+η′·sup_U Δ_U/(2τ_U²)+o(1)}), a polynomial gain because Δ_U/τ_U² is bounded away from 1 under (E).
- **No lower bound for η′ > 0.** n^{−(1−η′)/2} is attained only if a positive fraction of nulls saturate the bound and p is large enough.

**(c) Cheapest route — easy**

- Replace the supremum condition by the averaged one, with the same (J1) line.
- Weaken the 𝔏_n(S₀) condition as above.
- Add the elementary bound J_h(δ, τ) ≤ exp((|δ| + σh)²/(2τ²)) and take the minimum with (J1).
- Make C_F's dependence on the Δ-floor explicit.

No new probability is needed. A matching lower bound for η′ > 0 needs a reverse of the step bound L3.3 and is moderate.

## W3-START2:TRANSFER — (T3⁺)(a), STATEMENT.md:120-133

**(a) Hypotheses**

- **Deterministic envelopes on a set of probability → 1 are more than the argument needs.** The proof applies P1.4(ii) design by design (P1_LEADING.md:147-151), so it already yields the self-normalised bound P(o₀ > C^♯·[n^{−1/2}𝔏^♯_n(h) + n^{−1}𝔏_n(S₀; h)²](X^r) + n^{−κ}) ≤ ε + b̄_n + P(X^r ∉ 𝒳_n), uniformly conditionally on X^r ∈ 𝒳_n.
  - Hence "≤ r̄_n on 𝒳_n" can be replaced by "the design functional is O_P(r̄_n)", using ε-dependent design sets.
  - The ticket does exactly this by hand for the lattice clause in P4_BR.md:81-90, because (B-R⁺) with C_L = 0 is only a tightness statement (STATEMENT.md:150-152).
- **Fixed |S₀| = s₀ on 𝒳_n.** Could be |S₀| ≤ s̄ by taking the maximum of the constants over s₀ ≤ s̄. Minor.
- **Design-measurable targets and Gaussian noise given X^r.** Necessary for the conditioning route. Response-selected targets are a different theorem.
- **Functionals.** The ϱ̄_n and r̄_n hypotheses inherit the partition-free functionals; see START-F.

**(b) Conclusion**

- Only the annealed O_P form is stated; the quenched uniform bound and the random-rate form are stronger and already proved.
- The change from 𝔏⁰ to 𝔏^♯ is immaterial unless p = O(√log n).

**(c) Cheapest route — easy**

State L8.2(ii) in its self-normalised conditional form as the theorem, with the O_P-envelope version as a corollary. With the block-relative functionals this also gives the natural input for a Gaussian-row bound on the Q = S₀ odds through 𝔏^♯.

## W3-START2:IF-START — IF-START⁺, STATEMENT.md:176-180

**(a) Hypotheses**

Nothing beyond those of (T1⁺) and (T3⁺)(b). The restriction to Q = S₀ is what P-START asks.

**(b) Conclusion**

- **Fixed-design rate.** Inherits START-F: exponent sharp, functional improvable to block-relative.
- **Gaussian-row rate.** r_n = n^{−1/2}exp(C_R√log n) is weaker than what (T3⁺)(b) itself provides. For C_L = 0 the clause at STATEMENT.md:138-139 gives 1 − π₀(S₀) = O_P((s₀+1)n^{−1/2}(log n)^{15κ_Q+1/2}), hence the same for o₀. It is an O_P statement, which is the form IF-START needs, but it is not passed on (P1_LEADING.md:159-160).
- **The Gaussian-row clause does not use the P1 functional at all.** It goes through the lattice bound and 𝔏_n(Q) with N_ref reference pairs. For Q = S₀ the leading term needs only U ⊊ S₀ ⊆ H_l, so no localisation and no N_ref count.
  - Heuristically δ²_{U,l}/Δ_U behaves like χ²₁ across independent nulls, giving 𝔏^♯ = O_P(√log p).
  - That would give o₀ = O_P(n^{−1/2}√log n) when C_L = 0, with the second-order term n^{−1}(log n)^{30κ_Q+1} negligible, against the polylog exponent 15κ_Q + ½. I have not verified this.
  - For C_L > 0 a factor of the form exp(C√log n) looks genuine in the worst case (a common-sign bias of order one tilts the truncated mean). Only the constant is in doubt, also unverified.

**(c) Cheapest route**

- Easy: add the C_L = 0 rate to IF-START⁺ by citing (T3⁺)(b), and state the fixed-design interface with the block-relative rate.
- Moderate: a Gaussian-row bridge for 𝔏^♯ (the part of L9 for U ⊊ S₀) combined with (T3⁺)(a) in O_P-envelope form, to reach n^{−1/2}√log n.
