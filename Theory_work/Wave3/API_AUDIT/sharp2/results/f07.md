## W3-START:EX — examples table (STATEMENT.md:359-371; proofs L11_EXAMPLES.md)

The five verifications are taken as correct. The examples are weaker than what the ticket's own lemmas deliver: three stated rates are loose and several family restrictions are not used. The sharper statements below are my own derivations and have not been checked line by line.

### (a) Hypotheses

**F-1 / F-3, restriction p ≤ n − 1 (STATEMENT.md:361-362; L11_EXAMPLES.md:10). Not needed.**
- The orthogonal frame is used only to get δ_{U,l} = 0 for every null (L11_EXAMPLES.md:20, 35-36) and δ²_{U,j} = Nθ*_j² (L11_EXAMPLES.md:21).
- Both survive if the truths are mutually orthogonal and the nulls are any centred, norm-√N vectors in the orthocomplement of the truth columns with pairwise coherence ≤ 1/32. Then (I − P_U)x_l lies in the nulls' span, which is orthogonal to μ.
- Gershgorin gives λ_min(R̂_T) ≥ 1 − 15/32 for |T| ≤ 16, so (GF-16) holds. Such frames exist for p = n^A with any A.
- This gives a verified fixed-design p ≫ n example with exact order n^{−1/2}, closing the gap admitted at REPORT.md:44.

**F-1, "fixed signals bounded away from zero and above" (STATEMENT.md:363-364; L11_EXAMPLES.md:17).**
- For the O_P(√(log n/n)) conclusion this is stronger than needed. Line 21 only uses Nθ_lo²/(σ² + ‖θ*‖²) ≥ c_V log n, and c_V > 2 + 2A gives κ_V > ½. A beta-min of order √(log n/n) therefore suffices, and by (N3) (STATEMENT.md:325-327) the level 1 + 2A cannot be lowered.
- For the exact-order claim the lower bound is necessary. The mean null Bayes factor is τ_U/√Δ_U by (J1) (L6_COROLLARIES.md:52-54), which diverges when Δ_U → 0, so √n·o₀ → ∞.
- The upper bound is needed only through (E), a route assumption (ASSUMPTION_MAP.md:12).

**F-1, "p/√log n → ∞" for the exact order (STATEMENT.md:364). Artefact, cosmetic.**
- It comes from the fixed term 15√(2/π)z_n/p in 𝔏⁰_n (STATEMENT.md:113; L3_LEAK.md:36-40).
- At most 15 columns are involved. A sum of at most 15 variables exp(Z²/2) is tight without truncation, so their contribution is O_P(n^{−1/2}/p).
- Formally: use constant levels h_l = M_ε on those columns in (T1)(d), whose bound ε + b_n(h) tolerates a non-vanishing budget (L5_ASM_STARTF.md:46-47, 70), together with the block-relative 𝔏^{b,0}_n(h).
- Only p = O(√log n) is affected.

**F-3 (L11_EXAMPLES.md:29-39).**
- Fixed d is forced by the route, because κ_E blows up as g_* → 0 (L2_TK.md:60). It is not shown necessary (open item O2).
- "Null" twins is necessary. A twin of a truth at distance O(1) gives a swap model with O(1) Bayes factor, so π₀(S₀) does not tend to 1.

**F-2 (L11_EXAMPLES.md:43-75). The constants ρ² = 0.4, c = 2(1 + 2A), A ∈ [0.1, 1], fifteen correlates are illustrative.**
- The verification goes through for any ρ² ∈ (0, 1), any c with 1 + 2A < c < (1 + 2A)/ρ², and any fixed number of correlates.
- The lower limit on c is the N = ∅ instance (line 56). The upper limit is F4's level ρ²c < 1 + 2A̲ (line 61), and it is sharp partition-free by (C2)(i).
- A ≥ 0.1 is used only to show that default (AL) fails (lines 70-72). A ≤ 1 comes only from the frame.
- The choice c_m = c/12 (lines 54, 57) is not the best the stated (V-m) allows: any c_m < c·q₁₅ = c/11 works, giving κ_V up to (1 + 2A)/11.

**Premise (V-m) itself (STATEMENT.md:143-146) is stronger than its proof needs, and this caps F-2.**
- In L4_VIS.md:48-50 (Step 5), for a > d the price is Π = (a − d)[log(c_Q/p) − ½ log(n + 1)], which is strongly negative. The proof bounds it by the constant 15 log(16s₀) and discards the whole credit.
- Keeping it lets the bracket in (V-m) read (1 + 2A)(|D| − |N|)₊ − (1 + 2 log(p/c_Q)/log n)(|N| − |D|)₊ + c_m.
- At the least, the credit −(|N| − |D|)₊ is always available, since it uses only the dimension penalty.

**G-1 (L11_EXAMPLES.md:79-86).** Nothing to weaken inside Setting R. Fixed |θ*_j| is a route requirement of the bridge (Δ_lo is used at L9_BR.md:61, 114-123).

**G-2 (L11_EXAMPLES.md:90-100). Three restrictions are stronger than the verification uses.**
- Bounded groups: (L-R) only asks |H_l|, |Q| ≤ (log n)^{κ_Q} (STATEMENT.md:163-164), so polylogarithmic group sizes suffice.
- The whole-group floor λ_min(Σ^{(group)}) ≥ c: only sets of size ≤ 16 + s₀ are used (L9_BR.md:35-42).
- "At most one truth per group" is used only at line 95-96 to rule out cancellation in (V-R). A within-group PTV condition suffices, and (L-gain-R) and (L-loc-R) (lines 97-98) are unaffected.
- "Nothing is claimed about π₀(S₀)" is the right partition-free scope. A group-mate of a truth placed in another block has gain of order n, and (C2)(i) then kills the start mass.

### (b) Conclusions

**G-1 and G-2 rate n^{−1/2}(log n)^{15κ_Q+1/2} (STATEMENT.md:367-368; L11_EXAMPLES.md:84, 99). Loose by (log n)^{15}.**
- The proof file says so itself for G-1 (L11_EXAMPLES.md:85-86).
- The power comes only from N_ref = (1 + (log n)^{κ_Q})^{15} (L9_BR.md:95, 138, 144). In G-1, |I_l| ≤ 2^{s₀}; in G-2, |I_l| ≤ 2^{(s₀+1)ḡ}.
- Hence 𝔏_n(Q) = O_P(√log n) and 1 − π₀(𝓛(Q)) = O_P(√(log n/n)) in both.
- I believe √(log p/n) is the true order in G-1, not n^{−1/2}. For a Gaussian null column the gain statistic (δ + σZ)/τ is asymptotically exactly N(0, 1): design fluctuation plus noise sit at the critical moment. The average of exp(ζ²/2) over p nulls is then of order √log p.
- The matching lower bound is not claimed (STATEMENT.md:380) and needs new work.

**F-2 rate "o_P(n^{−κ}), κ < (1 + 2A)/12" (L11_EXAMPLES.md:73-75). Not sharp.**
- It is limited by the visibility exponent, not by the leak term n^{−0.1(1+2A)}.
- With the Step-5 credit, the binding instances become N = ∅ (c_m < 1 + 2A) and the single swap (c·q₁ = 1.2(1 + 2A)). So κ_V rises to just under (1 + 2A)/2, which exceeds ½.
- Partition-free this gives o₀ = O_P(n^{−0.1(1+2A)+o(1)}). That is sharp: on a partition separating a correlate from the truth, T₁ alone is of that order by (f1) (L2_TK.md:84).
- On any partition keeping the correlates in the truth's block, (C1)(b),(c) give o₀ = O_P(√(log n/n)) and o₀/T₁ → 1. The example currently says (T1)(c) is "not available" (line 75).

**F-1 / F-3 "exact order n^{−1/2}" (L11_EXAMPLES.md:25, 38). The order is sharp by (T0); the constant is available.**
- (T1)(c) gives o₀/T₁ → 1.
- All but at most 15s₀ nulls have U* = ∅, and their Bayes factors are n^{−1/2}exp(σ²Z_l²/(2τ²_∅))(1 + o(1)) with independent Z_l.
- A weak law of large numbers (moments of order below 1/a are finite) gives, for p → ∞, √n·o₀ → ((s₀ + 1)/2)·√(1 + σ²/‖θ*‖²) in probability.
- This is the unharvested item (ii) of ASSUMPTION_MAP.md:48-49.

**Literature.**
- No example can beat n^{−1/2}. The coded prior charges about (k + 1)/(2p) per added variable, which only offsets the multiplicity p, and (T0) applies.
- Yang–Wainwright–Jordan obtain posterior mass 1 − O(p^{−c}) on the true model, as I recall, because their prior charges p^{−κ} per variable. That is a different method, not a sharper analysis.
- Narisetty–He and Castillo–Schmidt-Hieber–van der Vaart give selection consistency at beta-min of order √(log p/n) with growing sparsity (and Narisetty–He with log p = o(n)). I am not confident of any explicit model-mass rate there to compare with.
- The examples have fixed s₀ and p ≤ n^A. Growing s₀ is open item O3. Sub-exponential p in G-1 looks plausible but needs the toolkit redone (the requirement ω̄_n log n → 0 at L2_TK.md:32-34 becomes a constraint on log p); I am unsure of the range.
