## W1-GEN:Thm-GEN (STATEMENT.md:108-111; premises :24-44, :48-54, :56-62, :64-75)

The theorem is a sufficient-class result and several of its hypotheses and its rate are looser than its own proof needs. The move-count bound and the growing-𝒱 route below are my own sketches from the L3 inequalities and have not been checked against the unseen T22(iii) proof text.

### (a) Hypotheses

**1. BM-∞ and M_n → ∞ are stronger than the D_n part of the proof uses; fixed large constants suffice.**
- The descent uses BM-∞ only through "L_n ≥ 4H_n" (L3_descent.md:64), "L_n/(8C_R) > W_n" (L3_descent.md:65) and the (1 − c_S) versions (L3_descent.md:69-71, 105-112).
- Here H_n and W_n are explicit multiples of log n (L3_descent.md:36-40). So (n−1)φ₀⁴θ*²_min ≥ C₀ log n with a fixed C₀ = C(A, M, σ², c_S) of order (A+1)(M+σ²)/(1−c_S)² gives Corollary L3.4 unchanged.
- VIS needs M_n only in e_n ≤ 2n^A(n+1)^{1/2}e^{ρ_n}n^{−cM_n/16} with c = σ²/(8C_R) (L2_centre.md:52-53). A fixed M̄ > 16(A+½)·8C_R/σ² already makes e_n → 0; "M_n → ∞" (STATEMENT.md:67) is not needed. The same applies to the block beta-min in (C1′).
- The full conclusion P(𝒪 ∩ {Ŝ⁺ ≠ S₀}) → 0 still passes through T03♯, which is invoked under BM-∞. A constant-level statement therefore needs a BM(c) version of the posterior input; STATEMENT.md:54 hints one exists.
- Constant-multiple beta-min (θ²_min ≳ σ² log p/n) is the standard level in Yang–Wainwright–Jordan, Narisetty–He and Castillo–Schmidt-Hieber–van der Vaart. The order log n/n itself is necessary for exact recovery (Wainwright-type lower bounds), so only the diverging factor is slack.
- The constants are crude: the 1/(2C_R) = 1/(6(M+σ²)) loss at L1_lemmaE.md:95 should be 1/(2v_T). Sharp constants, matching the sharp NL_η threshold (√(2A+1) − √(2A))², would be new work.

**2. SEP (H1b, STATEMENT.md:58) is stated against the worst-case floor (n−1)φ₀⁴θ*²_min; the proof needs only a state-wise relative version.**
- In L3.2(b1)/(b2) the comparison is ζ_n ≤ c_S·E₂, with E₂ the actual decrement of the designated inside addition (L3_descent.md:66-77). In L3.3(c3) it is ζ ≤ c_S·Λ_{T′} (L3_descent.md:102-103).
- So it suffices that, for each S ⊆ 𝒱 missing a truth (and each S′ = S∖{i}), the best outside decrement is at most c_S times the best inside-𝒱 decrement at S.
- The floor loses a factor φ₀⁻² to φ₀⁻⁴. With φ₀² = 0.05 as in L6(d) that is a factor of about 400 in the allowed outside signal.
- c_S may also tend to 1, provided (1−c_S)²L_n/log n → ∞ (from ε = (1−c_S)/4, L3_descent.md:62, 68-71).

**3. (H2) is used only to place the start mode inside 2^𝒱.**
- (C2) holds simultaneously for every centre C ⊆ 𝒱 (STATEMENT.md:99-102). The assembly uses only S* ⊆ 𝒱 (L5_assembly.md:31-32).
- Hence D_n ≤ P(S̃₀ ⊄ 𝒱_n) + P(Ω_nᶜ) with no change to L3–L5. A deterministic single reference with exact identification (VIS for every reference variable, NL for every in-𝒱 non-reference) is more than needed.
- Borderline block signals inside 𝒱 are harmless for the descent. These are the cases between C_L log n and M_n log n, currently excluded by the dichotomy at STATEMENT.md:91-94.
- Proving "S̃₀ ⊆ 𝒱 w.p. → 1" without identification needs a localisation version of T22(i)/(iii), which is new work. A finite family of candidate references is an immediate corollary.

**4. The fixed Gram floors in (H1a)/(H2a) are used quantitatively in fewer places than stated.**
- L1 needs only (n−1)λ_min ≥ max(J, 100) (L1_lemmaE.md:57-58); c_E and κ_E do not involve φ₀.
- In L3 the fixed floor enters L_n via L3.1 (L3_descent.md:47-52), on subsets of 𝒱 and on S₀ ∪ {l}. The floor on 𝒱 ∪ {l} is otherwise used only for L1 and for non-degenerate directions (L3_descent.md:114-116).
- So (H1a) could require the fixed floor on 𝒱 and S₀ ∪ {l} only, with (n−1)λ_min(R̂_{𝒱∪{l}}) ≥ max(J, 100).
- For (H2a) (STATEMENT.md:66), the visible steps need only rank plus the L1 requirement. Whether [P]'s residual and quad lemmas need a fixed floor cannot be seen from the files. This matters because blocks are built from highly correlated columns.

**5. SE_λ(j₀) and SE_w are not needed for the first conclusion.**
- D_n ≤ P(S̃₀ ≠ S*) + P(Ω_nᶜ) uses only G, R, BM, (H1), (H2). The sparse-eigenvalue conditions enter only through T03♯ (L5_assembly.md:37-38).

**6. The size bounds |𝒱| ≤ v̄ and s* ≤ v̄ are not shown necessary.**
- (C6) breaks only growth d_n = 16(2ℓ_n+5) (L7_counterexample.md:16) and only for the LS0 route. Growth o(log n), or c·log n with small c, is open (STATEMENT.md:154).
- No necessity is shown for SEP or the centre certificate either (STATEMENT.md:160).

### (b) Conclusion

**Rate.** P(Ω_nᶜ) = O(1/log n) (STATEMENT.md:110; L5_assembly.md:26-28) is an artefact of the thresholds chosen for 𝒟₅ and 𝒟₄.
- 𝒟₅: replace "+2 log log n" (L3_descent.md:26-28) by "+2r_n" with r_n = ½ log n − C. L3.3(a) still gives a negative increment (L3_descent.md:90-96), and P(𝒟₅ᶜ) ≤ 2e^{−r_n} = O(n^{−1/2}).
- 𝒟₄: take z_n² = log n − C. L3.3(b) has slack ½ log(n+1) against z_n²/2 (L3_descent.md:97-99 bounds by z_n², a factor 2 loose), so P(𝒟₄ᶜ) = O(n^{−1/2}).
- 𝒟₁–𝒟₃ are already O(n^{−1}). So P(Ω_nᶜ) = O(n^{−1/2}).
- That is the natural order: the probability that some null addition beats S₀ is about n^{−1/2}/√log n, consistent with T03♯'s √(log n/n).
- The total D_n rate is then set by P(S̃₀ ≠ S*), for which no rate is stated. A polynomial rate n^{−min(κ, ε₀)} looks extractable from L2 steps 4–8 (L2_centre.md:41-56).

**Onset and move count.** τ ≤ 2^{v̄} − 1 (L4_search.md:33-34, admitted crude at :44) makes the onset ℓ_n ≥ 2^{v̄} − 2 doubly exponential in v̄. On LH, v̄ = g(2c+v) ≥ 18. A potential argument from the existing L3 inequalities gives a linear count:
- From a superset T ⊋ S₀ the best neighbour is always an extra-deletion. L3.3(a) applied to T∖{e} beats every swap of an extra; truth deletions and swaps lose order L_n. So that phase takes exactly |T∖S₀| ≤ v̄ − s₀ moves.
- Each move from a state missing a truth raises F by at least L_n/(8C_R) − W_n (L3.2(a)). The total rise is at most ν log(RSS_C/RSS_{S₀}) + O(v̄ log n), which is about ‖μ‖²/(2σ²).
- Hence τ ≤ v̄ − s₀ + C(M, σ²)·‖μ‖²/L_n. This is O(v̄) when θ*_min is comparable to the signal scale, as in LH, W and the study.

**Scaling ranges versus the literature.**
- Fixed s₀, fixed v̄ and p ≤ n^A are narrower than posterior-consistency results. I am confident that Narisetty–He and Castillo–Schmidt-Hieber–van der Vaart allow log p = o(n)-type growth, and the latter growing s₀. Johnson–Rossell is p ≤ n. I am unsure of the exact s₀ growth allowed in Narisetty–He and of the precise form of Song–Liang's conditions.
- On the algorithmic side, Yang–Wainwright–Jordan (rapid mixing) and Zhou–Yang–Chang–Liang (informed MH) work under global sparse-eigenvalue conditions with growing s₀ and constant-level beta-min. Those conditions hold for fixed-ρ AR(1) designs, which this theorem excludes (S2, L6_instances.md:186-191).
- They concern a different object: MH chains with stronger dimension penalties, not LS0 from a block start with g = n and BB(1,p). They show the class is not at the frontier but do not imply the result.

**Mode.** The conclusion is intersected with 𝒪 and no delivery claim is made (P(U_n) uncontrolled). I cannot judge from these files whether that is improvable.

### (c) Cheapest routes

1. **Easy.** Restate with fixed constants M̄ and C₀ for the D_n bound; relative SEP; D_n ≤ P(S̃₀ ⊄ 𝒱) + P(Ω_nᶜ); P(Ω_nᶜ) = O(n^{−1/2}); hypotheses split per conclusion. All are re-tunings of L3.0, L3.2, L3.3 and L5.1 with no new idea.
2. **Easy to moderate.** Replace 2^{v̄} − 1 by the linear move count above. This needs one added lemma in L4 using only L3.2(a) and L3.3(a),(b).
3. **Hard.** Growing relevance set |𝒱_n| ≤ c log n with s₀ fixed. With route 2, the remaining obstacles are:
   - 𝒟₄ over v̄2^{v̄} normals needs z_n² ≈ 2v̄ log 2, to be kept below log n + 2 log p;
   - c_v = v̄ log 2 + … in L3.3(a) must stay below ½ log n;
   - L1 with J ≍ log n;
   - the centre law with s* growing (L2 step 8 tolerates s*·a_n → 0).

   This would nearly meet (C6) and is the frontier named in REPORT.md:88-91; S2 is the natural test.
4. **Hard.** Constant-level or sharp-constant beta-min for the full theorem (needs T03♯ under BM(c)); growing s₀; super-polynomial p (the tolerance η ≈ 1/(8A) shrinks); a Yang–Wainwright–Jordan-type global sparse-eigenvalue class replacing bounded 𝒱, which needs control of noise-chosen extras under the shipped prior.
