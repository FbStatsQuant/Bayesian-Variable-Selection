## W2-FAC-X:Thm-FAC-CS-iid (STATEMENT.md:76-95; premises :36-44, :48-56, :60-72)

The theorem is not sharp. One hypothesis (the fixed coefficient vector) and one clause (the range of (C1)) can be strengthened with modest edits. The headline exponent ½ is far from the natural target, but improving it needs new work.

### (a) Hypotheses

**(θ), fixed nonzero ϑ (STATEMENT.md:50-52) — stronger than the proof needs; easy to weaken.** A lower bound on the coefficients is used in only three places:

- **Truth moves (X4_path.md:283-294).** The step needs (|ϑ|_min·√((1−ρ)N/2) − C√p·H̄)² to exceed CpH̄² plus a multiple of log(2p) + log n. Line 286 rounds this up to "≥ c_θ n". So |ϑ_n|_min ≥ C√(p log n/n) suffices.
- **Positive pairs in the start mode (X5_start.md:54-55).** The step needs n·c_FS² ≥ C log n and c_FS ≫ δ′_n‖ϑ‖ (X5_start.md:35-38). So a margin c_FS,n ≫ √(log n/n) suffices.
- **BM-∞ (X6_assembly.md:28).** The condition is already of the form θ²_min·n/log n → ∞, and line 32-33 notes that T03♯ accepts a deterministic coefficient sequence.

Everything about null moves is free of β: RSS_T = ‖e‖²/(1+q_T) for T ⊇ S₀ (X1_representation.md:51), and s² only needs ‖ϑ‖ bounded (X2_perturbation.md:55-56). One caveat: X4 imports FAC-B's L3/L4 "verbatim" and I cannot see those files; I assume, from this β-free structure, that they do not use a coefficient lower bound.

So the theorem holds for arrays ϑ_n with ‖ϑ_n‖ bounded, |ϑ_n|_min ≥ C√(p log n/n), and (FS) stated with a margin ≫ √(log n/n). Under (P-½) this lets β_min → 0 polynomially.

For comparison, the information-theoretic level for exact support recovery is of order σ√(log p/n) (Wainwright). Narisetty–He and Yang–Wainwright–Jordan work at that order. I am confident of the order, not of their constants. A constant beta-min is therefore weaker than standard by a polynomial factor, and the proof does not need it.

**(P-½), limsup log p/log n < ½ (STATEMENT.md:49).** The picture differs by clause:

- **(C1) does not need ½.** X5 uses only block-local objects: sets of size ≤ 16 + s₀, Lemma CR with A := 1, valid for p ≤ n (X5_start.md:15-18), and conditional Gaussian noise (X5_start.md:39-45). The global floor λ_min(R̂) ≥ c₀ in 𝒜_n can be replaced on blocks by λ_min(Σ_V) − |V|δ′_n. The only real constraint is K = p, i.e. p ≤ n/2 (X5_start.md:70). So (C1) holds on FAC-B's range.
- **(C2) in its simultaneous-over-𝔫 form is exponent-sharp as formulated.** Re-adding the largest excluded ξ gains about log p − ½log n − ½log log p (STATEMENT.md:99-101), which is positive once p ≫ √(n log n). But the uniformity is a proof device, not a property the theorem needs. The realised N₀ is Z-measurable, and given Z the coordinates g_jᵀe₁ for j ∈ N₀ are at most 15 approximately N(0,1) variables. This is the same conditioning already used for E^Z_2 (X5_start.md:42-45). So max over N₀ of |ξ_j| ≤ C√L_n with probability → 1, and the family of 𝔫 can be restricted to non-extreme sets. That removes obstruction (c) (REPORT.md:27-28) and the degeneration of the margin c = 0.6(½ − A₁) (X4_path.md:88). It does not by itself raise the exponent.
- **The band term (a) is the binding obstruction.** The requirement pH̄√(log n/n) → 0 (X4_path.md:74-76; X2_perturbation.md:118-122) is a fixed-point constraint. It does not depend on the size of ϖ, so enlarging ϖ cannot help. A union bound over band subsets gains nothing either: the supremum over subsets is the ℓ¹ norm of the Wishart row.
- **The second-order term (b) is softer.** The bound p^{3/2}/n ≤ √(p/n) (X2_perturbation.md:87-89) uses crude operator norms. Its dominant part is a common multiplicative factor, since E[(W/N − I)²] ≈ (p/N)·I, so that part is harmless for ordering. I conjecture, but have not verified, that changing the reference from W → N·I to W_T → (N − k − 1)·I removes this term up to p ≪ n^{2/3}.
- **No necessity is shown, and the evidence points the other way.** FAC-B reaches p ≤ n/2 for exact Gram (REPORT.md:9). N1X is clean at exponent 0.75 (REPORT.md:23). The W2-SIM pilot at 0.75 found non-[p] start modes and swap paths that still ended at S₀ (REPORT.md:244-246). So beyond ½, (C2) may fail in form at finite n while (C4) survives.

**(FS) (STATEMENT.md:53-56).** It is used only for (C1) (X4_path.md:9). For fixed ϑ and ρ it excludes a finite union of hyperplanes, so it costs little. It is necessary for (C1) in the stated form: if ϑ(V) = 0 there are extra exact population zeros. It is probably not necessary for (C4). Dropping it needs a path lemma from starts that miss truths or many nulls, and the exceptional-set device (|E| ≤ 17, X2_perturbation.md:63-65) does not give that. FAC-B's (FS′) variant is excluded here only because the partition is random (STATEMENT.md:54-56). This is hard to weaken.

**Fixed s₀ (STATEMENT.md:50).** The proof steps visible here need only s₀·e^{−L_n} → 0 (X2_perturbation.md:37) and s₀ ≪ m₁. However (FS) becomes 2^{s₀} conditions, and the energy bound M = 2s₀‖ϑ‖² (X6_assembly.md:27) feeds T03♯, whose exact hypotheses I cannot see. Slow growth is plausible but I am unsure. Castillo–Schmidt-Hieber–van der Vaart and Yang–Wainwright–Jordan allow s₀ growing nearly up to n/log p under their own conditions, so fixed s₀ is well short of the literature.

**Fixed ρ ∈ (0,1) (STATEMENT.md:48).** All constants (C_X, c_D, c₁, κ_∞) are continuous in ρ. So ρ_n in a compact subset of (0,1) with a uniform (FS) margin should follow with bookkeeping only, as FAC-GL already does for loadings.

**Gaussian rows and Gaussian noise (STATEMENT.md:36-37).** These are essential to the technique: the exact rotation and ξ ⊥ W (X1_representation.md:22-35), and the conditioning in D5. I know of no counterexample showing necessity. Fixed-design results in the literature typically allow sub-Gaussian noise. This is hard to relax.

### (b) Conclusion

- **Scaling range.** The exponent ½ sits against FAC-B's p ≤ n/2 for the same method, and against posterior-consistency results covering p ≫ n (Narisetty–He, p = e^{o(n)}; Castillo et al.; Yang–Wainwright–Jordan, which also gives rapid MCMC mixing). Those concern the posterior or a different sampler. The bottleneck here is the search path from a near-full start, which needs p ≤ n/2 anyway. The fair target is therefore exponent < 1, and ½ is not sharp.
- **Beta-min.** A constant level is not sharp (see (a)).
- **Band scale ϖ_n (STATEMENT.md:70-72).** The term H̄√(p log n/n) matches the measured perturbation in order: R20 finds max|δ|/(H̄√(p log n/n)) ∈ [0.16, 0.28], flat in p and n (REPORT.md:109). That part is sharp up to constants. Two pieces are slack but cosmetic:
  - The n^{−1/3} floor is an artefact of D3 (X2_perturbation.md:19). Any floor ≫ n^{−1/2}·polylog works, and it matters only for p ≲ n^{1/3}.
  - p could be replaced by the current k along the path.
- **Mode of convergence.** The result is "→ 1" with no rate. The events as built fail with probability of order 1/log n (D3, D4, E^Z_1: X2_perturbation.md:36-37; X5_start.md:41) or (log n)^{−c} (E6, D8). A polynomial rate looks reachable by taking L_n ≍ ε log n, at a cost of ε in the margins. D8 and E6 would need retuning; I have not checked this. Yang–Wainwright–Jordan, as I recall, state polynomial probability bounds.
- **Posterior statement.** The proof already shows E[1 − π(S₀|y)] → 0 under the joint law (X6_assembly.md:34), but (C3) does not state it. Adding it costs nothing.

### (c) Cheapest routes

1. **Easy — beta-min.** Replace "fixed ϑ" by ϑ_n with ‖ϑ_n‖ = O(1), |ϑ_n|_min ≥ C√(p log n/n), and (FS) with margin ≫ √(log n/n). Edits: X4_path.md:286-293, X5_start.md:37-38 and :54-55, X6_assembly.md:28.
   - A further step to the optimal order √(log n/n) looks moderate. Apply Proposition X2 to truth coordinates on path sets; its proof does not use nullness of j (X2_perturbation.md:73-111). The noise projection is then ≈ σ(ξ_j − x_T) = O(H̄), replacing the uniform √p·H̄ bound.
2. **Easy — (C1) range.** State (C1) for p ≤ n/2 by making the eigenvalue floor in X5.1 block-local.
3. **Easy — restrict 𝔫.** Restrict the family to max|ξ_j| ≤ C√L_n and show N₀ belongs to it by conditioning on Z. This gives a non-degenerate addition margin and is needed for any extension past ½.
4. **Easy — bookkeeping.** State E[1 − π(S₀)] → 0 explicitly; allow compact-ρ uniformity.
5. **Hard — exponent beyond ½.** Needs a leave-one-out decoupling of band membership from W_{j·} (STATEMENT.md:106-108), plus the centred reference for the second-order term, plus route 3. A full extension to p ≤ n/2 additionally needs FAC-B's finer bulk analysis under perturbation (REPORT.md:247).
6. **Hard — non-Gaussian rows or noise; dropping (FS).**
