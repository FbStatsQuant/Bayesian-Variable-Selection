## W1-PXN:Thm-PXN — headline at STATEMENT.md:70-71 (premises :13-20, :45-48, :52-53)

The theorem has an easy quantitative strengthening on the search side, and several hypotheses sit well above the levels known in the literature, though relaxing those needs new work.

### (a) Hypotheses

- **Fixed signals β (STATEMENT.md:14) — stronger than the proof's own onsets need.** Every explicit onset involving the margin c₂ ∝ β² is at the log n/n level:
  - the (D_S) margin needs n ≳ (4A+2)(4(σ²+M)/c₂) log n (STATEMENT.md:82; FINAL_PROOF.md:60);
  - Lemma Λ needs 8M(A(s₀+2)+1) log n/n ≤ c₂/8 (STATEMENT.md:83);
  - T03♯ already has a constant-level beta-min form BM(c) with c₀(s₀, A), which the ticket leaves unused because BM-∞ holds trivially (P8_T03sharp.md:18, :49, :59).

  So β²_min ≥ C·log n/n with C large is the natural sufficient condition. That matches the necessary order σ²·log p/n for exact support recovery (Wainwright 2009) and the level in Narisetty–He, Yang–Wainwright–Jordan and Castillo–Schmidt-Hieber–van der Vaart. What I cannot check from these files is the start side (P3′ constants C_q, κ_E and the hub-versus-proxy margin inside a signal block; FINAL_PROOF.md:45-48). The SLLN step for b_min (P8_T03sharp.md:39, :43) would also need a ratio form.

- **SIZE, g ≤ B_n < 2g, so 9 ≤ g ≤ 16 (STATEMENT.md:20) — the upper half looks stronger than needed.**
  - The strict-margin argument makes each group a dendrogram node without it (R_branch_repair.md:22-24). B < 2g is used only to make blocks equal groups exactly (:24-25).
  - T22(i-a) holds "for every partition" (FINAL_PROOF.md:46-47). So "blocks = unions of whole groups" (only g ≤ B) should suffice for (C).
  - The price is a partition-dependent reference S° with Φ(S°) ≤ c, and a larger Lemma E / Tr-J family (unions of ≤ ⌊16/g⌋ groups, D = A⌊16/g⌋ instead of D = A; FINAL_PROOF.md:31-32).
  - This would cover the study's groups of 5, which are currently excluded (STATEMENT.md:72-73). It is new work with existing tools.
  - The lower half g ≤ B looks necessary for this route, since a split group breaks the block-table start.

- **p ≤ n^A (STATEMENT.md:15) — the binding step is the cited T03♯.**
  - The ticket's own lemmas need less: [CONC′] with L_nδ_n → 0 needs (log p)^{3/2} = o(√n) (CR_corr_tails.md:38), and the (L) remainder needs log p = o(n^{1/3}) (L_strict_localmax.md:19-20).
  - T03♯ is stated with p ≤ n^A and j₀ = ⌈(2A+1)s₀⌉+1 (P8_T03sharp.md:16-17).
  - Narisetty–He reach log p = o(n), and Yang–Wainwright–Jordan reach s₀ log p ≲ n, but both use p-dependent slab or g hyperparameters. Here g = n and the size prior is BB(1,p), so I am unsure the full range is attainable; a stretched-exponential range is plausible.
  - The absence of a lower growth bound on p is already as weak as possible.

- **Fixed s₀ = 2c+v (STATEMENT.md:13, :19).**
  - The literature allows s₀ → ∞ with s₀ log p = o(n).
  - Here s₀ sits in exponents: D = A(s₀+2) (FINAL_PROOF.md:32), j₀, and T03♯ R1.
  - The proof route has a structural cap: LS0 adds one hub per move and scores S₀ iff ℓ ≥ c − 1 (LS_P6_repair.md:17-19). So this route cannot handle c > ⌈log₂(n+p+1)⌉ + 1. Growth of v is not capped this way.

- **|ψ_i| < ρ (STATEMENT.md:13).** Necessary for the partition step: (R-a) needs a nonempty (ψ̄, ρ), and I* → 0 as ψ̄ ↑ ρ (STATEMENT.md:56-57). No slack that I can see.

- **Gaussian rows and noise, equicorrelation.** These are used through exact laws (Hotelling density, Haar/Beta laws, Lemma CR). Gaussian noise is standard in all the comparison papers. Sub-Gaussian design is plausible but would replace exact tools.

- **(OP) with LS0 (STATEMENT.md:45-48).** D_n is controlled only through LS0. Removing LS0 would need an SMC analogue of the Yang–Wainwright–Jordan rapid-mixing result. I am not confident of the exact Zhou et al. reference or its conditions.

### (b) Conclusion

- **No rate is stated, and the written bound is looser than the proof supports.**
  1. FINAL_PROOF.md:78-79 bounds D_n by P(𝒯_nᶜ). The event 𝒯_n includes 𝒵_n, whose failure bound 2/(√(2π)u_n log n) (FINAL_PROOF.md:60-61) is the slowest term by far. Scoring S₀ needs neither 𝒵_n nor 𝒜_n: it "needs only Ω_n-part (D_S) and ℓ_n ≥ c − 1" (LS_P6_repair.md:31-32, Lemma LS(ii) :18-19). So D_n ≤ P(Ω_nᶜ) + P(S̃₀ ≠ S°).
  2. The term 2E₀δ₁ (Markov, FINAL_PROOF.md:74) can be replaced by P(δ₁ ≥ ½), or by P(S₀ is not the unique F-maximiser on Ω). Either is smaller and follows directly from (P) without bounded convergence.

- **Rate available for D_n: O(√(log n/n)).** The pieces are:
  - P(q > x) ≤ C_q√(log n/n)/x + P(𝒢_nᶜ) (STATEMENT.md:59), used with FINAL_PROOF.md:46-53;
  - Tr-J and Lemma E failures ≤ Cn^{−1}e^{O(√log n)} (STATEMENT.md:80-81);
  - [CONC′] failure 3n^{−2} (CR_corr_tails.md:35).

  Three steps currently carry no rate. The SLLN step in ℰ♯(b),(c) (P8_T03sharp.md:43) can use χ² tails. Lemma Λ is stated only "in probability" (FINAL_PROOF.md:56). And ẽ is stated only as O_P (FINAL_PROOF.md:46).

- **Lower bound on any rate (heuristic).** When LS0 centres at S₀, its final scan scores every S₀+l. So any failure of (L) forces Ŝ⁺ ≠ S₀. The ticket's first-order heuristic for that is n^{−1/2}(log n)^{−1/2} (ERRATA.md:21, :28). The headline rate therefore cannot beat that order, times liminf P(𝒪), for this g = n local-prior posterior. Non-local priors (Johnson–Rossell, for p ≤ n) or p-dependent slabs (Narisetty–He) give faster decay, but that is a property of the target, not of the theorem. The gap to the provable √(log n/n) is one log factor.

- **Full headline rate.** This needs a tail or truncated-moment form of T03♯. Only O_P is cited (P8_T03sharp.md:19), so I cannot judge it from the material.

- **Sub-clauses.**
  - Certification in (T): replacing the 𝒵_n threshold 2 log p + 2 log log n by 2 log p + 2a log n with a < ½ still gives F(S₀+l) − F(S₀) ≤ −(½−a) log n + O(1) (L_strict_localmax.md:22-24). Failure improves from about 1/(log n·√log p) to O(n^{−a}).
  - (P)/(S) rate √(log n/n): for slowly growing p, √(log p/n) is attainable by the ticket's own admission (ERRATA.md:26).

### (c) Cheapest route

1. **Easy (edits only).** Restate (C) for n ≥ n₀ as
   P(𝒪 ∩ {Ŝ⁺ ≠ S₀}) ≤ P(π(S₀) ≤ ½) + P(Ω_nᶜ) + P(S̃₀ ≠ S°),
   with the last two terms O(√(log n/n)). This needs:
   - dropping 𝒵_n at FINAL_PROOF.md:79;
   - χ² tails in place of the SLLN at P8_T03sharp.md:43;
   - writing out the Lemma Λ and ẽ tails.

   Also change the 𝒵_n threshold to get the n^{−a} certification rate.
2. **Unknown.** A truncated-moment version of T03♯ (in the style of P3′) would give the full headline O(√(log n/n)).
3. **Moderate.** Beta-min at C√(log n/n): re-track c₂-dependent onsets and the start-side constants.
4. **Hard.** SIZE relaxed to g ≤ B; superpolynomial p; growing s₀; no LS0.
