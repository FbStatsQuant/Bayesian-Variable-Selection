## W3-SAMP:C — Theorem C (concentrated bridge), STATEMENT.md:149-163

Short version: the T-free bound is the right shape, but the proof is centred at S₀ and that costs both a hypothesis and a constant. Re-centring the same telescoping argument gives E|W_r − 1| ≤ 2·TV(π, π₀), which needs no truth model and is attained. Several sub-clauses (C-ii, C-iv, the HPM clause of C-v) also have cheap stronger forms. I derived the improvements below by hand from the ticket's own steps; they are not machine-checked.

### (a) Hypotheses

**Posterior concentration is not needed for the evidence clauses (C-iii and the Ẑ/Z, log Ẑ and NC_r parts of C-v).**
- Concentration enters only at C_theorem.md:50-52, where β is relaxed to 2max{δ, (1−δ)o₀} ≤ 2δ̄.
- The telescoping at C_theorem.md:34-44 never uses a(S₀) = 0. It uses only V₀ = 1 and identity (2).
- Re-centre at d* := log(Z̄₁/Z̄₀) instead of d(S₀). Then e^{a*} = π/π₀ =: w, c* = 1, V_T = W, and (3) reads E(1 − W)⁺ ≤ Σπ₀(1 − w)⁺ = TV(π, π₀).
- Hence **E[|W_r − 1| | 𝓒] ≤ 2·TV(π, π₀)**, with no S₀ in the statement.
- This is never worse than the stated β: β = Σπ₀|w − 1/c| + |1 − 1/c| ≥ Σπ₀|w − 1| = 2TV by the triangle inequality, and the same inequality shows d* is the best centring.
- Also TV(π, π₀) ≤ max(δ, δ₀) ≤ max{δ, (1−δ)o₀}, so the displayed chain improves too.
- So the evidence conclusions need only 1_𝒜·TV(π, π₀) → 0. That covers non-identified or multi-model posteriors, where 2δ̄ does not vanish.
- The bias bound |E p̂_r(j) − PIP_j| (STATEMENT.md:157) likewise becomes ≤ TV(π, π₀) with no concentration.

**The weakened condition is necessary for the stated parameter class ("any T ≥ 1, any N_e ≥ 1", STATEMENT.md:150).**
- At N_e = 1, T = 1, W = w(X) with X ~ π₀, so E|W − 1| = 2·TV(π, π₀) exactly.
- For W ≥ 0 with EW = 1, W → 1 in probability forces E|W − 1| → 0.
- Under P-CONC, TV → 0 is equivalent to δ₀ → 0. So P-START with no rate is sharp for the evidence clause once concentration is granted.
- This closes the registry line "Linear δ̄ in C: no lower-bound family proved" (ASSUMPTION_MAP.md:22, C_theorem.md:85-86).
- The bound is also tight uniformly in N_e at T = 1 for abstract targets. Take a state S′ with π(S′) ≈ δ and π₀(S′) = ε with N_eε → 0; then E|W − 1| ≥ 2δ(1 − N_eε)(1 + o(1)).
- I have not checked whether a coded regression target can realise this hidden-mass configuration.

**P-START is not necessary for the PIP, MPM and HPM clauses or for production budgets.**
- TR and D already give alternative routes (ASSUMPTION_MAP.md:18, 21).
- The HPM clause of C-v (C_theorem.md:74-75) needs only P-MAP and P-OVL, which is Theorem D-iv. Here δ̄ → 0 is far stronger than required.
- o₁ → 0 is used for the PIP clauses only through g ≤ 21o₁ (C_theorem.md:59). The κ₀ term is attained, so some concentration-type condition is needed for a sup-norm target of 1_{S₀}.
- Whether full δ → 0 (rather than max_j|PIP_j − 1{j∈S₀}| → 0, which is weaker when p is large) is needed is not settled; the registry concedes this at ASSUMPTION_MAP.md:16.

**Inherited premises.** KERNEL invariance at each stage and unbiasedness (MR-a) are genuinely used at C_theorem.md:37-39. I see nothing removable there.

### (b) Conclusion

**C-iii constant and form.** 2·TV(π, π₀) replaces β and is attained exactly at N_e = T = 1. In the concentrated regime the gain over β is at most a factor 1 + 1/π₀(S₀); for spread posteriors it is unbounded.

**Log-evidence tail (STATEMENT.md:155).** Splitting the two tails gives (β/2)·coth(u/2) instead of β/(1 − e^{−u}). This is a factor (1 + e^{−u})/2 better, which is cosmetic.

**No dependence on N_e.** This is the main slack for production, where N_e ≥ max(2500, n, p) (D_theorem.md:53).
- At T = 1, Var(W) = χ²(π‖π₀)/N_e ≤ 2M·TV/N_e, so E|W − 1| ≤ min{2TV, √(2M·TV/N_e)}.
- Under TR's premise M = o(log n), this beats O(δ̄) whenever δ̄ ≫ log n / max(n, p).
- The hidden-mass example shows that no N_e gain is possible without an overlap condition (χ² or M).
- For general T this needs non-asymptotic SMC variance theory. I believe the relevant work is Cérou–Del Moral–Guyader on normalising-constant variance and Marion–Mathews–Schmidler on SMC sample complexity, but I am not certain their conditions fit this bridge.

**C-ii (STATEMENT.md:152).** The truncation and the constant 4 are both unnecessary.
- E x_t = E[W_t x_t] + E[(1 − W_t)x_t] ≤ (1 − q_t(S₀)) + TV(q_t, π₀), using the re-centred telescoping on the sub-grid φ₀..φ_t.
- Each term is at most δ̄/(1 + δ̄), so **E[U_r | 𝓒] ≤ 2(T+1)δ̄/(1+δ̄)**, untruncated.
- Linear growth in T is exact: when π₀ = π, E U = (T+1)δ.

**C-iv PIP bound (STATEMENT.md:156).** The burn-in L is unused, and C and TR can be merged into one bound.
- MR-b at s = 0 gives ‖E q̂_T − π‖_TV ≤ E(1 − W)⁺ ≤ TV(π, π₀).
- The Doeblin contraction at TR_theorem.md:33-34 then gives **E‖p̂_r − 1_{S₀}‖_∞ ≤ min{1, κ₀ + δ + (1−δ)λ₀ + e_L·TV(π, π₀)}**.
- This dominates both C-iv (g + β/2) and TR-b (g + e_L).
- The asymptotic condition becomes o₁ → 0 and e^{−L/M}·δ₀ → 0 on 𝒜, which is implied by either P-START or P-OVL∞.

**C-iv MPM bound (STATEMENT.md:157).** The linear rate 44δ̄ comes from a single Markov step.
- Under IND-T, {MPM ≠ S₀} requires at least two islands with E_r ≥ 1/3.
- That gives P(MPM ≠ S₀ | 𝓒) ≤ 54(g + β/2)² = O(δ̄²).
- Without IND-T, linear order is plausible at N_e = 1. For large N_e it is probably very loose, but resampling dependence makes that hard to exploit.

**C-iv discovery bound (STATEMENT.md:157).** The proof at C_theorem.md:64 already has δ₀^{N_e}, and D-i gives δ₀^{D_r}. The stated "≤ δ̄" throws away the exponent.

**Mode of convergence (C_theorem.md:77-78).** L¹ convergence under the joint law is free: E[1_𝒜|Ẑ/Z − 1|] ≤ E[1_𝒜 min(2, 2TV)] → 0 by bounded convergence, and likewise for the PIP error. Only L¹ rates need moment control of δ̄.

**Rate against the indicator.** The O(o₁) part cannot be improved in order: the exact PIPs themselves can differ from 1_{S₀} by about δ, and κ₀ is attained. The constants 19 and 21 (from B + 3, B + 5) are loose but harmless.

**Scaling range and literature.** Theorem C is design-agnostic. All of p versus n, beta-min and sparsity sit in P-CONC and P-START, so there is nothing to sharpen inside C.
- The transfer is linear, so no rate is lost from W3-POST.
- The real gap to the literature is the regime label at STATEMENT.md:163. Yang–Wainwright–Jordan (2016) obtain polynomial mixing for variable-selection MH from arbitrary starts, and Zhou–Yang–Vats–Roberts–Rosenthal obtain dimension-free mixing for informed MH. Theorem C instead assumes the start is already selection-consistent.
- Those mixing times are polynomial in (n, p, s₀) single updates, as I recall. That is well above L = ⌈log₂(n+1)⌉ rounds, so removing P-START for the PIPs this way would need the SMC bridge or independence proposals to do real work.
- I know of no published result giving π₀(S₀) → 1 for a block-product start, so I cannot judge P-START against a benchmark.

### (c) Cheapest routes

1. **Easy, about five lines.** Replace d(s) by log(Z̄₁/Z̄₀) in Step 4 (C_theorem.md:34-52). State C-iii as E|W_r − 1| ≤ 2TV(π, π₀) ≤ 2max(δ, δ₀). State the evidence part of C-v under 1_𝒜TV(π, π₀) → 0 alone. Record exact attainment at N_e = T = 1.
2. **Easy.**
   - C-ii as the untruncated E U ≤ 2(T+1)δ̄.
   - C-iv as g + e_L·TV(π, π₀), nesting TR-b.
   - Discovery as δ₀^{D_r}.
   - MPM as O(δ̄²) under IND-T.
   - L¹ mode of convergence.
   - The coth log-tail constant.
3. **Moderate.** A T = 1 addendum, min{2TV, √(2M·TV/N_e)}, to show the particle budget matters.
4. **Hard.** An N_e-dependent bound for general T under overlap or mixing conditions, and removing P-START for PIPs through bridge transport or YWJ-type mixing.
