## W3-SAMP:LT# — Theorem LT♯ (STATEMENT.md:126-140; proof `lemmas/LT_theorem.md`)

**Summary.** The finite-sample inequalities are Markov-tight in form and the rate o₁ cannot be beaten. But the asymptotic clause LT-e throws away a rate the proof already gives, three hypotheses are stronger than the steps that use them, and the "open" status of NC_r can be partly closed with material already in the ticket. Verdict: IMPROVABLE-EASY.

### (a) Hypotheses

1. **"NC_r for every r ≤ 4" (STATEMENT.md:136, 139) is redundant or too strong, depending on the conclusion.**
   - Each island is the same map Φ(𝓒, U_r) of an iid stream independent of 𝓒 (MR_identities.md:64-66; D_theorem.md:35-36). So P(𝒜 ∩ {W_r < η}) should not depend on r, and NC_1 ⇔ NC_r for all r. I infer this from how the proofs use Φ; I have not seen the code pass r into anything but the stream choice. If so, LT-e′'s island-specific thresholds η_{n,r} (LT_theorem.md:77-78) are also unnecessary.
   - The HPM conclusion uses one island only (LT_theorem.md:72-73, 82-83).
   - The MPM conclusion tolerates one fully collapsed island. MPM ≠ S₀ forces Σ_r E_r ≥ 2 (Step 4, LT_theorem.md:56-58). If three islands have E_r < 1/3 the sum is < 2, so {MPM ≠ S₀} ⊆ {at least two islands have E_r ≥ 1/3}. This gives P(MPM ≠ S₀ | 𝓒) ≤ 2ρ without independence and ≤ 6ρ² under IND-T, with ρ := 3g/η + P(W_r < η | 𝓒).
   - Only the sup-norm PIP conclusion needs all four islands.

2. **Global P-CONC is stronger than Step 5 uses.** The terms P(o₁ > ε/3) and P(δ ≥ ½) (LT_theorem.md:66-68, 72) can be intersected with 𝒜. Localised 1_𝒜o₁ → 0 suffices, as LT-e′ (STATEMENT.md:140) and C-v (C_theorem.md:70-71) already have it.

3. **"o₁ ≤ ¼" in LT-d (STATEMENT.md:134) is an artefact of routing through LT-b with its κ₀ offset.** LT-b′ gives P(E_r ≥ ½ | 𝓒) ≤ 2g/η + P(W_r < η | 𝓒) with no side condition.

4. **LT-e′'s premise has a simpler equivalent form.** The existence of measurable η_{n,r} with 1_𝒜o₁/η_{n,r} → 0 and P(𝒜 ∩ {W_r < η_{n,r}}) → 0 is equivalent to 1_𝒜·o₁/W_r → 0 in probability. For the converse take η_n = o₁/a_n with deterministic a_n ↓ 0 slowly. This drops the auxiliary thresholds.

5. **The trade-off o₁/η → 0 is necessary in form.** In a pure importance-sampling configuration (N_e = 1, T = 1, identity-like π-invariant mutation), put π₀(S) = π(S)/η for S ≠ S₀. Then W ≥ η always and P(X ≠ S₀) = δ/η exactly. So Markov's δ/(εη) in LT-a/LT-c is attained and LT-e′ fails if o₁/η stays bounded away from 0. This is an abstract witness at the level "any π-invariant kernel"; the coded Gibbs moves may do better, as TR shows.

6. **Whether NC_r is necessary is recorded as open (STATEMENT.md:141-143; ASSUMPTION_MAP.md:20; LT_theorem.md:87-93), but at the abstract-target level both halves can be settled.**
   - *Not necessary.* Take TR's own converse example (TR_theorem.md:69-71) with M = 2 fixed, L → ∞, N_e = 1, T = 1. Then W_r = w(X₀) equals 2δ/(1+δ) with probability (1+δ)/2 → ½, so NC_r fails. TR-d (STATEMENT.md:170-171) still delivers every conclusion of LT-e.
   - *Cannot simply be dropped.* Make the "trap" a sequence: π₀ nearly a point mass at S′ with π(S′) = δ/2, all flip and swap neighbours of S′ of relative mass e^{−n}, and π₀(S₀) = e^{−n}. With polynomial budgets the particles never leave S′, P-CONC holds, and the outputs are wrong.
   - Both are abstract triangular arrays. Whether a regression target with the coded block start realises either, and whether collapse can happen at production N_e ≥ max(2500, n, p), remains genuinely open.

7. **The inherited premises are not excessive.** KERNEL invariance is needed for MR. No assumption is made on p, s₀, N_e, T or L, so the p ≤ n^A range is inherited entirely from P-CONC.

### (b) Conclusion

1. **Rate is missing but free.** LT-e claims consistency only (STATEMENT.md:136-138). LT-b′ (STATEMENT.md:131-132) gives immediately: if 1_𝒜o₁ = O_P(r_n) and NC_r holds, then 1_𝒜‖pip_primary − 1_{S₀}‖_∞ = O_P(r_n) and 1_𝒜(1 − A_{r,q}) = O_P(r_n).
   - Proof: P(𝒜 ∩ {E_r ≥ Kr_n}) ≤ E[1_𝒜 min(1, 21o₁/(Kr_nη))] + P(𝒜 ∩ {W_r < η}); pick η by NC_r, then K.
   - At present the rate is stated only under P-START in C-v (STATEMENT.md:160-161).
   - So the transfer preserves rates. Any rate from the selection-consistency literature for π(S₀ | y) passes through unchanged, for example Narisetty–He, Castillo–Schmidt-Hieber–van der Vaart under beta-min, or Yang–Wainwright–Jordan.

2. **The rate o₁ is unimprovable.** MR-c (STATEMENT.md:112-113) and p̂_r(j) ∈ [0,1] give an exact identity not in the ticket:
   - E[W_r·|p̂_r(j) − 1{j∈S₀}| | 𝓒] = |PIP_j − 1{j∈S₀}| for every j.
   - Hence (1−δ)κ₀ ≤ max_j |PIP_j − 1{j∈S₀}| ≤ E[W_rE_r | 𝓒] ≤ g ≤ 21o₁.
   - When the off-S₀ mass sits on one neighbour, κ₀ ≈ o₁, so LT-b′ is sharp up to the constant (at most 21). This is a cleaner sharpness statement than the pathwise κ₀ remark (LT_theorem.md:108-109).

3. **The same identity gives strictly better companion bounds.**
   - Per coordinate: P(|p̂_r(j) − 1{j∈S₀}| ≥ u | 𝓒) ≤ |PIP_j − 1{j∈S₀}|/(uη) + P(W_r < η | 𝓒), with no κ₀ and no λ₀. It needs only marginal concentration of PIP_j, not π(S₀) → 1.
   - ℓ¹ form: E[W_r‖p̂_r − 1_{S₀}‖₁ | 𝓒] = E_π|S △ S₀| exactly.
   - Sup-norm: E[W_rE_r | 𝓒] ≤ min{g, E_π|S △ S₀|}. This is ≈ δ rather than 21o₁ when the residual mass is at Hamming distance 1–2, the typical superset regime.

4. **Constants in LT-d are loose by a factor 4.** Pool before applying Markov. On ∩_r{W_r ≥ η}, Σ_rW_rB̄_r ≥ ηΣ_rB̄_r, with E Σ_rW_rB̄_r = 4δ and E Σ_rΛ̄_r ≤ 4λ₀. Under o₁ ≤ ¼ this gives
   - P(MPM ≠ S₀ | 𝓒) ≤ 8δ/η + 152o₁ + Σ_rP(W_r < η | 𝓒), against Σ_r[8δ/η + 152o₁ + …] in the statement;
   - or unconditionally ≤ 2g/η + Σ_rP(W_r < η | 𝓒).
   - In LT-b the equal split 2ε can be replaced by ε₁ + ε₂ (constant gain of at most 2).
   - The constant 19 = B + 3 is loose but only a constant.

5. **Mode and scope.** The theorem covers only the strong-selection regime π(S₀) → 1, and is conditional on a particle-system premise.
   - The stronger computational benchmark is Yang–Wainwright–Jordan, who derive rapid mixing from primitive design and beta-min conditions. Their polynomial mixing bound is, as far as I recall, far larger than the O(p log n) terminal elementary steps coded here, so it does not discharge NC_r directly.
   - Consistency of p̂_r(j) toward the exact PIP_j without posterior concentration is not addressed. It would need N_e → ∞ and mixing.
   - I am not confident of the precise statements of Song–Liang or Zhou et al. relevant here and do not rely on them.
