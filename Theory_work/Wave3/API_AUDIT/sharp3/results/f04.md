## W3-SCR:A1 — Corollary A1 (STATEMENT.md:158-163)

**Summary.** A1 is correct in shape but loose in four places that the ticket's own lemmas already repair. The dependence of c₁ on κ²b²/σ², the linear cap in log p and the ∝ 1/s₀ ceiling are necessary for the statement about locally optimal (LO) models at q = 0. The "fixed" qualifier, the ℓ₁ bound, the M² in c₁, the constant 200 and the mode "→ 0" are not.

### (a) Hypotheses

1. **"κ, b, M, σ fixed" (STATEMENT.md:159-160) is stronger than the proof needs.**
   - Fixedness is used once, at L3_screening.md:71 ("let n be so large that √(κ(n−1))·b ≥ 2M/√(n+1)"). That is the explicit condition κb²(n²−1) ≥ 4M².
   - Every later step (L3:72-75) is an inequality uniform in n, and gives ρ² ≥ 200/64 whatever the sequences κ_n, b_n, M_n, σ_n are.
   - (A∞) (L3:27-35) needs only liminf ρ > 1.
   - So A1 holds for n-dependent constants once κb²(n²−1) ≥ 4M² is added. The growth condition then reads as a beta-min: b_n ≥ √200·κ⁻¹·√((M_n²+σ²)𝐋_n/n), where 𝐋_n = (q_n+1) log p + log n.
   - For M_n = O(σ) this is the rate σκ⁻¹√(𝐋_n/n), i.e. A1°'s rate (STATEMENT.md:165-169), from A1's own proof.
   - As stated, A1 requires fixed coefficients. That is far above the necessary level θ_min ≍ σ√(log p/n) (Wainwright 2009, information-theoretic limits) and above the beta-min of order σ√(log p/n) used by Yang–Wainwright–Jordan 2016. I am unsure of the exact power of the eigenvalue constant in their condition.

2. **‖θ*‖₁ ≤ M is stronger than needed, and silently forces s₀ ≤ M/b = O(1).**
   - This sits oddly with "no bound on s₀" at STATEMENT.md:89-90.
   - The ℓ₁ norm enters only at L3:70, in two places: τ_n² ≤ M²+σ² and c_θ ≤ M/√(n+1).
   - The bridge B2/B2d consumes only ‖θ*‖₂ (L4_bridges.md:24-33; the ℓ₁→ℓ₂ step is internal).
   - It suffices to assume ‖Xθ*‖² ≤ M²(n−1) (bounded total scale) and ‖θ*‖₂ ≤ ½√(κ(n²−1))·b. Then s₀ may grow, with c₁ ∝ 1/s₀ automatically, because DF at S = ∅ gives ‖μ‖² ≥ κ(n−1)s₀b².

3. **The M² in c₁ = κ²b²/(200(M²+σ²)) is an artefact of routing through (A∞) and the total scale τ_n (L3:70, 74).**
   - Example: S₀ = {1,2}, orthogonal columns, θ* = (b, A), A → ∞. A1's c₁ → 0.
   - Yet any S missing truth 2 has the witness gain ≈ A²n, and any S ∋ 2 missing truth 1 has σ_S² ≈ σ² + A²/n² (ASSUMPTION_MAP.md:17). So screening holds for 𝐋_n ≲ nκ²b²/σ² regardless of A, up to ‖μ‖ = o(nσ).
   - The proved B2° (L4:35-47) with SCR-A° gives Ψ_n ≥ n·min{κ²b²/(32σ²), κ/(72s₀)} (L3:84). So a sufficient condition is 𝐋_n ≤ c·n·min{κ²b²/σ², κ/s₀}, with no M.
   - With the written constants this beats A1 once M/(κb) exceeds a small constant; it is not uniformly better, so the best statement takes either condition.
   - In the proportional regime 𝐋_n ≍ n, (A°∞) does not apply as written, since it needs q_n log p = o(n) (L3:59-62). MC° must be used non-asymptotically with ϑ°_n ≤ √(2c)+o(1), which only changes the numerical constants (my estimate: about 384 and 864 in place of 32·8 and 72·8).

4. **What is necessary** (for the statement "every LO model of size ≤ q contains S₀"), from SCR-N (STATEMENT.md:201-207; L6_sharp.md:12-28). The first two items are my own applications of SCR-N to specific designs, not results stated in the ticket.
   - **Linear cap in log p.** Take s₀ = 1, x₁ orthogonal to the rest, b and σ fixed. Then (★) at L6:18 makes ∅ LO with probability → 1 once 2 log p + log n ≥ (1+δ)·n·log(1+b²/σ²). So "log p ≤ c·n with c depending on b²/σ²" cannot be relaxed, and p ≤ n^A is correctly absent (ASSUMPTION_MAP.md:24).
   - **κ²b²/σ² dependence.** Take the pair design of ASSUMPTION_MAP.md:20, where φ^d(0) = κ, Γ_n(0) ≍ κ²b²n and τ_n² = σ²+2κb². SCR-N makes ∅ LO when κ²b²n ≤ (1−δ)²τ_n²(2 log p + log n). So κ² (not κ) and b²/σ² are the right dependence for LO models.
   - **s₀𝐋_n ≲ n.** With s₀ equal strong orthogonal truths, γ(t|∅)/τ_n² ≈ n/s₀, so ∅ is LO unless s₀(2 log p + log n) ≲ n. The ∝ 1/s₀ ceiling is necessary too.
   - **Not shown necessary.** DF itself is only a sufficient bridge (ASSUMPTION_MAP.md:18-20). The entropy part q_n log p has no lower bound for q ≥ 1 (ASSUMPTION_MAP.md:16). The size restriction |Ŝ| ≤ q_n is needed by the addition argument only (ASSUMPTION_MAP.md:22).

5. **Design hypothesis compared with the literature.** DF(q_n) is weaker than what Yang–Wainwright–Jordan assume (sparse eigenvalue floor on all sets up to a size bound, plus a sparse-projection condition). Non-true columns may be arbitrarily collinear here. I see no weaker primitive condition to recommend.

6. **Gaussian errors** (L2_noise.md:3-6) could be sub-Gaussian with changed constants (ASSUMPTION_MAP.md:28). This is not free: the χ² steps need Hanson–Wright.

### (b) Conclusion

1. **Mode and rate.** A1 states only "→ 0"; the proof gives 3e^{−√log n} (L3:28, 35). The slack 200/64 allows applying SCR-A (STATEMENT.md:80-85) with x = c₃n instead.
   - I checked that MC(q_n, c₃n) holds for large n with c₃ = κ²b²/(400(M²+σ²)): the extra terms 2a_xτ_*√T_n + τ_*√(2x) ≤ 0.081·κb√n fit in the slack 0.108·κb√n.
   - Hence P(some LO model of size ≤ q_n misses a truth) ≤ 3·exp(−c₃n). This is exponential, and it covers all LO models at once rather than only the reported one.

2. **Constant 200.**
   - With the written proof, any c₁ < κ²b²/(64(M²+σ²)) works, since only ρ > 1 is needed and not ρ ≥ √2 (L3:68, 75).
   - The 16 at L3:72 is avoidable: c_θ = O(n^{−1/2}) and (n−1)²/n ~ n give Γ_n ≥ κ²b²n(1−o(1)). Then any c₁ < κ²b²/(4(M²+σ²)) works, a factor of 50.
   - At q = 0 the necessary threshold above is 2 log p + log n < n·log(1+b²/σ²) (s₀ = 1, κ = 1). L1.5 (L1_score.md:53-56) linearises log(1/(1−ρ)) ≥ ρ, so A1 can reach only n·b²/(b²+σ²). Using SCR-M's exact gap −ν log(1−u_S) (L7_master.md:27, 55-58) recovers the exact threshold at q = 0.

3. **Scaling range.**
   - q_n up to order n/log(p∨n) and log p up to order n are rate-right at q = 0, by item (a)4.
   - Beyond q = 0, the term σ√(2q log p) is a union bound (L2:15-20). For incoherent designs the Gaussian width in SCR-M (L7:16-20) is plausibly much smaller, by my heuristic of order 1 + q log p/√n for i.i.d. designs; this is not proved here or in the ticket. Under fixed κ and the linear cap this would change only constants.
   - Extending to sizes up to K−1 or K needs swap or deletion arguments (REPORT.md:129-131). That is open and hard.

4. **Screening only.** "⊇ S₀" cannot become "= S₀" without extra conditions: LO strict supersets exist (ASSUMPTION_MAP.md:27). No complaint here.

### (c) Cheapest route to a stronger A1

All steps reuse proved lemmas; no new mathematics is needed.

1. Delete "fixed"; add κb²(n²−1) ≥ 4‖θ*‖₂² (L3:71). Replace ‖θ*‖₁ ≤ M by ‖Xθ*‖² ≤ M²(n−1).
2. State the bound as 3·exp(−c₃n) through SCR-A with x ∝ n.
3. Replace 200 by any constant above 4 (asymptotic) or above 64 (proof as written).
4. Add the alternative growth condition 𝐋_n ≤ c·n·min{κ²b²/σ², κ/s₀} via B2° and MC°, which removes M².

Steps 1-3 are line edits to L3:66-76. Step 4 is a half-page non-asymptotic rerun of L3:79-86.

Harder and optional: exact constants in the proportional regime via the SCR-M gap; a lower bound for the entropy term at q ≥ 1; sizes up to K.
