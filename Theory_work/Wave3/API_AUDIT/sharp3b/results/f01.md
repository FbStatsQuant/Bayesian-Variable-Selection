### (a) Hypotheses

**1. Margin constant c > c₁(s₀, A) (STATEMENT.md:130; E_visibility.md:11) — far stronger than Lemma E1's assembly needs.**

The log n level of the margin is necessary in order; only the constant and its uniformity are loose.
- For p = n^A, exact recovery in an orthogonal design needs θ²_min·n ≳ 2Aσ² log n (Gaussian maxima; Wainwright 2009 gives the same order information-theoretically).
- For this prior, a single deletion gains about p√n, which gives the 2A+1 (consistency) and 2A+2 (rate r_n) obstruction the ticket records (REPORT.md:159; E_visibility.md:83-84).

Against that, c₁ ≈ 161 at s₀ = A = 1, and c₁ grows like s₀A². Four steps create the slack:

- **Target n⁻² is not needed.** In E2 (E_visibility.md:62-65) the bound is π(𝒰) ≤ 2^{s₀}κ̄(1 + e^{ρ_n}Υ′_n) + …, and Υ′_n is already O_P(r_n). So pure deletions S ⊊ S₀ (the "1") need only κ̄ = O(r_n), and underfits with J′ ≠ ∅ need only κ̄ = O(1). The "+2" at E_visibility.md:49-54 can become "+½" and "+0" respectively.
- **The factor 2/log 2 is avoidable.** It comes from log(1+u) ≥ (log 2)·min(u,1) (E_visibility.md:48). At margins of order log n, D/B′ → 0, so log(1+u) ≥ (1−ε)u on u ≤ u_ε gives 2(1+ε); the case u > u_ε is trivial because ν·log(1+u_ε) ≫ log n.
- **The prefactor is over-counted.** The proof uses n^{s₀(A+1)} (E_visibility.md:37-38); the actual factor is (2p)^{|J|}(n+1)^{|J|/2} with |J| = |S₀∖S|, i.e. about n^{|J|(A+½)}.
- **Union and χ² costs are charged at the worst size j₀.** These are x_n ≈ Aj₀ log n (line 24) and χ²_{J′} ≤ C_A j₀ log n with C_A = 8+3A (line 46). Both can be taken per size |J′|, and C_A·j′ can be about 2(Aj′+1).

Pure deletions separate cleanly. There are at most 2^{s₀} of them, so no p-union is needed, and χ²_∅ = 0. With the cross term O_P(√L_S), the proof of E1 then gives the in-probability conclusion under L_S ≥ (2A|J| + |J| + 1 + ε)σ² log n. At |J| = 1 this is 2A+2+ε, which matches the recorded obstruction. Keeping the 1/n-probability event that Claim C needs gives about (√2 + √(2A+4))², roughly 15 at A = 1.

The uniform constant over 𝔉 is also structurally conservative. Under per-level Occam decay (DG/ADG), a multiplicity count suggests the required margin should decrease with the excess |S∖S₀| − |S₀∖S| and become vacuous near j₀. In the proof c₁ instead increases with j₀. The cause is that χ²_{J′} is charged twice: once in D through Q_{S′} ≤ Q_{S₀} (E_visibility.md:39, 44-47) and again inside ℓ(S′)/ℓ(S₀).

For comparison, Narisetty–He (2014) Condition 4.4 uses, as I recall, a threshold of 5σ²|t|(1+δ) log(√n ∨ p); I am not certain of the exact form. Yang–Wainwright–Jordan (2016) have C·σ² log p/n with an unspecified constant.

### (b) Conclusion

- **In-probability rate.** O_P(r_n) is sharp for genuinely polynomial p. With about p independent nulls the superset sum behaves like n^{-1/2}·p⁻¹Σ_j e^{χ²_j/2}, and the summands have tail index 1, so the sum is ≍ √(log p/n). This is my heuristic, not a proof. For fixed or sub-polynomial p it is improvable to O_P(√((1+log p)/n)), which meets the deterministic lower bound of order n^{-1/2}.
- **Expectation rate (Claim C, invoked at STATEMENT.md:132).** I believe the √log n is real even at fixed p. For one orthogonal null, E[n^{-1/2}e^{χ²₁/2}; χ²₁ ≤ log n] = √(2 log n/(πn)), so E δ_n ≳ √(log n/n). This short calculation would answer the open question at REPORT.md:190 and should be checked.
- **Mode.** O_P plus an expectation bound is already strong. P(HPM ≠ S₀) = O(r_n) is loose by at most a log factor.

### (c) Cheapest routes

1. **Retune E1 (easy).** Use a graded margin: about 2|J|(A+½)+1 on pure deletions, and a per-size constant with target O(1) on swaps. Add a two-model converse δ_n ≥ π(S)/(π(S)+π(S₀)) for S = S₀∖{j}, to pin the deletion constant.
2. **Vanishing floor (easy to medium).** Replace φ₀² by C₀/N after checking η_n in (B3).
3. **Rate √((1+log p)/n) (easy under (DG)).** Truncate the level-1 χ² at 2 log(pM) in (C5′). Under (OS) the weights a_J must be restated at the log p scale.
4. **Sharp swap constant, growing s₀, p = e^{o(n)} (hard).** The swap constant needs the χ²_{J′} double charge removed, which the floor-free comparison currently forces.
