## W2-FAC-PKA:L4 — table discovery and posterior-to-reported-model transfer

**What I checked.** The statement (L4_table_discovery.md:7-14) against the proof (L4_table_discovery.md:16-20) and the definitions it draws on in STATEMENT.md:15-17 (score F = log w + log m, D_n = P(potential output and S0 not scored), GUARD-W).

**The proof holds as a conditional transfer.**

1. **Inclusion {potential output, S0 not scored} ⊂ A_n^c** (L4:18). On A_n, S0 is a feasible subset of a single block. Under the stated hypothesis that every feasible block subset is scored and offered to the ledger, S0 is scored on every potential-output branch. The exact-enumeration branch scores every feasible model, and feasibility is part of A_n, so that branch is covered too. A_n may be data-dependent through the partition; the inclusion is pointwise, so D_n ≤ P(A_n^c) follows without any independence.

2. **HPM step** (L4:20). If π(S0) > 1/2, every other model has mass below 1/2, so S0 has strictly larger F and no tie convention matters. On A_n ∩ {potential output}, S0 is in the scored set, so the all-scored maximiser is S0. GUARD-W transfers this to the reported record on actual output. Hence {actual output, reported ≠ S0} ⊂ A_n^c ∪ {π(S0) ≤ 1/2}.

3. **Markov** (L4:20). P(1 − π ≥ 1/2) ≤ 2E[1 − π]; the constant 2 is right. The union bound gives the displayed inequality at L4:12.

4. **Consequences** (L4:14). Since 1 − π ∈ [0,1], convergence in probability gives E[1 − π] → 0. Adding P(no actual output) gives the unconditional version. No design, iid, factor or LS0-path input is used.

5. **Application** (L4:24, outside the registered span). For s0 = 1 and K ≥ 1 a singleton lies in its own block under any partition, so A_n holds deterministically and D_n = 0 given the same method premises.

**Findings.**

- **MINOR** (L4:12, L4:20). π(S0|data) is never defined. The step "π(S0) > 1/2 ⇒ S0 has strictly greater F than every other scored model" needs π to be the exact posterior proportional to exp(F), with F the D0 score of STATEMENT.md:15 used by the ledger, normalised over a model space that contains every scored model. That is the natural reading, and the step is valid under it. It would fail if π meant an SMC estimate, a tempered posterior, or a posterior under a different prior or score. The statement should say so.

- **MINOR** (L4:18). Two method-side facts are asserted only by source citation (v3_core.cpp:372, :408), which is not in the material provided. First, that the table entry offered for a block subset U is the full F(U) = log w(|U|) + log m(U), on the same scale as other ledger entries, rather than the block-product start score. Second, that every potential-output branch performs complete ingestion; the proof hedges this with "on other potential-output branches with complete ingestion". The statement hypothesis "complete table-ingestion behavior of the merged method" (L4:7) covers the second fact but only implicitly the first. The HPM step needs score identity, not just ingestion, so it should be a named premise. The file itself flags source fidelity as unreviewed (L4:28). This does not break the lemma as a conditional statement.
