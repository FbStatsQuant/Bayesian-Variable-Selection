## C-DLV:Prop-F

Proposition F has two halves: delivery, P(𝒰_n) = 0 for every n ≥ 3 for the patched calibration, and consistency, P(Ŝ⁺ = S₀) → 1 on LH, LH-iid and GEN (L4_delivery.md:79-84). I take both as correct and assess them separately.

### (a) Hypotheses

**Delivery half: model G and the class restrictions are stronger than the proof uses.**
- The proof is "Theorem D verbatim with 𝒮ℒ_n = ∅" (L4_delivery.md:85-88). In that walk the data law enters only through Lemma S.1, to make A3–A5 null (L2_degenerate.md:9-16; L4_delivery.md:24-26).
- Everything else is deterministic:
  - Lemma R holds "for every valid input, every seed and every realisation of the uniforms" in terms of p_raw (L1_resources.md:70-72, :110-116).
  - S.2 is exact-arithmetic algebra needing only yᵀy > 0 after standardisation (L2_degenerate.md:22-32).
  - S.3 is a call-graph audit (L2_degenerate.md:38-67).
  - The fallback grid is valid by Lemma 3.3 (L3_grid.md:30-40).
- So the proof gives a sure statement: for every finite numeric (X, y) with n ≥ 3, at least one non-constant column and non-constant y, and for every seed and uniform realisation, the patched fit returns estimates.
- Gaussian noise, fixed s₀, p ≤ n^A, the LH/GEN design conditions, and EX-S, SR and the e4 tie rule (L4_delivery.md:7-8) play no role in this half. OP-LS0 enters only through the move cap ℓ. TDP-IMPL enters only through its polynomial cost, and any polynomial cost bound would do with the envelope adjusted.
- "P = 0" is therefore the weak form. The null set is exactly A3 ∪ A4 ∪ A5 and can be named.

**Delivery half: premises that are needed.**
- IDEAL-W: `unsupported_size` fires at any fixed word size for large n (INVENTORY.md:24).
- A growing allocation: a fixed one fails by definition (L1_resources.md:142-143).
- MACH: operational, cannot be derived.
- n ≥ 3: the code rejects n < 3 (INVENTORY.md:20).
- The envelope of Corollary R is sufficient but not shown minimal. The memory order (n+p)² looks tight in the worst case, from X and the Gram matrix (np + p²) and the population term N̄(W+K) with N₀ ≥ n and K up to n/2. The time envelope is driven by the worst-case score cost at k = K (L1_resources.md:32-34, :140-141). Since the claim is exact zero for every realisation, a worst-case envelope is the right kind of hypothesis. A smaller one would at best give P → 0.

**The form of the fallback is over-specified.** The proof uses only that the frozen grid starts at 0, ends at 1, is strictly increasing, has at most T₁ intervals, and is a function of calibration streams only (L4_delivery.md:85-87). Any such grid works; the pilot schedule and "last successful construction" are two instances.

**Whether the patch is necessary is not established.** The ticket shows (α) and (γ) are reachable for constructed candidate sets (L5_insertions.md:87-92). It does not show that 𝒮ℒ_n has positive probability under the data law at any n. No tier-1 escalation occurred in 134 fits (STATEMENT.md:74). So it is unknown whether the unpatched method already has P(𝒰_n) → 0 or even = 0; that is item (O).

**Consistency half: the posterior hypothesis is stronger than needed.** The proof goes through {π(S₀|y) ≤ ½} and Markov, giving 2E[1 − π(S₀|y)] (L4_delivery.md:65-71). The pathwise Claim only needs S₀ to be the unique ≻-maximum among feasible models (L4_delivery.md:68-69). So "P(S₀ is the posterior mode) → 1" suffices in place of [P:POST-CONC]-type strong selection consistency. That is a weaker upstream requirement; the gain is in not needing the total mass of competitors to vanish.

**Consistency half: the class restrictions are inherited, not used.** Fixed s₀, p ≤ n^A and the hypotheses of W1-GEN / W1-PXN enter only through the two inputs P(𝒦_nᶜ) → 0 and posterior concentration (L4_delivery.md:55-64). Prop F adds no restriction of its own.

### (b) Conclusion

**Delivery.** Probability zero cannot be beaten as a number. The mode is improvable to "surely, on non-degenerate inputs, uniformly over seeds and over all (n, p_raw)", as above.

**Consistency: rate.** Only "→ 1" is stated, but the proof yields a non-asymptotic bound once P(𝒰_n) = 0:

P(Ŝ⁺ ≠ S₀) ≤ P(S₀ is not the unique HPM) + P(𝒦_nᶜ) ≤ 2E[1 − π(S₀|y)] + P(𝒦_nᶜ)

(L4_delivery.md:70-72). The upstream rate quoted in the ticket is P(S̃₀ ≠ S*) + O(1/log n) for the 𝒦 term (L4_delivery.md:59). Stating the inequality costs nothing and exposes which upstream term is the bottleneck.

**Consistency: mode.** The event 𝒦_n ∩ {S₀ is the HPM} depends on the data only (L4_delivery.md:53-55, :63-64), and delivery is sure. Hence P_data(for every seed and every uniform realisation, Ŝ⁺ = S₀) → 1. This is stronger than convergence under the joint law of data and algorithm randomness.

**Scaling range against the literature.**
- Fixed s₀ and p ≤ n^A are well inside what posterior-level theory covers. Narisetty–He give strong selection consistency with p = e^{o(n)}. Castillo–Schmidt-Hieber–van der Vaart give selection consistency under beta-min and compatibility conditions with growing sparsity. Yang–Wainwright–Jordan give posterior concentration together with polynomial-time mixing of a Metropolis–Hastings sampler with growing s₀ and p up to roughly s₀ log p ≲ n. I am confident of these regimes but not of exact constants. Johnson–Rossell is restricted to p of order at most n, as I recall.
- Those results concern the posterior or MCMC mixing, not this algorithm's reported model.
- The restriction here comes from the upstream discovery result and the method's budgets, namely the LS0 cap ℓ = ⌈log₂(n+p+1)⌉ and B ≤ 16 (L1_resources.md:8). Widening s₀ or p is upstream work, not something Prop F can supply.

**What the conclusion does not cover.** The fallback grid "need not satisfy the candidate grid's restricted-ESS property" (L4_delivery.md:89-90). Only the reported HPM is controlled, and that goes entirely through deterministic LS0. Nothing is claimed about the accuracy of the SMC estimates (PIPs, evidence) on the fallback event. A statement there would be a genuinely stronger result.

### (c) Cheapest route to a stronger version

**1. Restate as two parts (easy, no new mathematics).**
- F-i, deterministic delivery: under EX-A, IDEAL-W, MACH, CONFIG, TDP-IMPL and the Corollary R allocation in (n, p_raw), for every valid non-degenerate input and every seed, the patched fit returns estimates. Any fallback grid passing `v3_check_grid` with at most T₁ intervals and measurable with respect to calibration streams is allowed. Proof: the existing walk (L4_delivery.md:24-40) with Lemma S.1 replaced by the non-degeneracy assumption.
- F-ii, modular consistency: for any data law with P(degenerate input) = 0, sup over seeds of 1{Ŝ⁺ ≠ S₀} is at most 1{S₀ not the unique HPM} + 1{𝒦_nᶜ} almost surely. The explicit bound follows, and the three classes become corollaries through the cited inputs. Proof: the existing Claim (L4_delivery.md:65-69), with the Markov step dropped.

**2. Estimate quality on the fallback event (hard).** Preferring the "last successful construction" fallback when one exists keeps the restricted-ESS property for that construction's V. The pilot-only case needs the open stage-count and variance question (L5_insertions.md:115-117).

**3. Wider s₀ or p range (hard, upstream).** This needs a new LS0 discovery result. The log-size move cap is the visible obstacle.
