## PL audit and the RM-lattice alternative

The central quantitative idea is plausible, not refuted. `Theory_work/Wave3/W3-SCR/lemmas/L5_exact.md:36–58,90–103` controls the sum of deletion losses. For a strict superset with m extras, let ell=log(p/m), L=2log p+log n, and let phi be its uniform floor. Using the exact deletion-score identity and concavity of log bounds the average likelihood loss by the average deletion gain divided by the residual scale. Up to uniform lower-order terms the best deletion improvement is at least

`1/2 [2ell + log n - (2ell)/phi] >= (epsilon/(2phi)) L`.

To attain the displayed `(1-o(1))` coefficient rather than merely a smaller fixed constant, choose the excess tail parameter x' tending to infinity but o(L), and redo the uniform probability bookkeeping. The existing proof's x'=epsilon log n/8 spends a fixed fraction of the margin; it does not itself establish the sharpened coefficient.

For a **phase-2** entry C0 containing S0, the size-prior/determinant penalty and monotonicity of the residual fit give `F(S0)-F(C0)<=m0 L/2 (1+o(1))` under A1/NF. Thus C=phi/epsilon is a reasonable leading-order upper bound on total moves, conditional on remaining in the sparse family. If C0 was the original missing-truth entry, rename the phase-2 entry and account for its O(1) size and move overhead. M5 does not prove preservation of individual truths before containment.

For this bound, phi>=vartheta+epsilon and phi<=1 give `C-1>=vartheta/epsilon>=2log p/log n` at leading order. For p>=n, ell_n/(C-1) is at most `(1/(2 log 2)+o(1))log n`, approximately **0.72135 log n**, not a literal upper bound 0.72 log n. For p<n the corresponding upper envelope is `(1/(2log2)+o(1))(log n)^2/log p`. The final-search cap lacks the m0 term. These are limits of the bound, not lower bounds on actual path lengths, and the N-chi comparison must retain the m0-versus-Q distinction.

The “on RM's own lattice” sketch (memo 117–121) is a credible separate improvement: replacing binomial entropy over p by entropy over |Q| can give an almost-full deletion penalty with only O(log|Q|/phi0²) average loss. A budget allowing `m0 log|Q|` of order log² n is plausible. It needs a uniform score expansion and explicit constants/slack; in-lattice additions and swaps are “harmless” only in the sense of being charged to a budget, not prohibited. Exit control must include additions from deletion bases, thereby covering swaps leaving Q, and TL must handle truth removal. This may weaken (N); it does not prove (N) removable. The cited R02 review `Theory_work/Wave3/W3-RM/reviews/R02_better_theorem_astra.md:220–238` leaves precisely this work open.

## Is there a missed route?

**Yes, candidate strengthening routes are omitted or prematurely excluded; no completed supersession proof is furnished by the sources inspected.** The useful candidates are (i) size-only/block-relative entry with a deterministic search family rather than a small global Q, followed by a confined potential argument; (ii) the memo's own restricted-lattice potential plus exit control; and (iii) EC' with favourable conditional lengths and dual-direction complexity, retaining more information than omega<=1/phi. L5.2 (`Theory_work/Wave3/W3-SCR/lemmas/L5_exact.md:71–79`) already permits the third route to conditional exactness without NF. It still needs entry and cap control for a reported-model result.

SCR-M with a deterministic special family or low witness width can improve screening/gaps; it cannot by itself exclude LO supersets or force the coded path into that family. SCR-G does cover uncertified capped returns for containment, but is not an exact-recovery or delivery theorem. Final-search discovery remains a possible independent route, and FAC-PK does not refute it. A regime-D numerical trigger cannot use SCR-C as its last step without verifying NF on the actually chosen range or replacing NF by an EC'-based exactness argument (memo 163–165). No method change is needed to formulate these research questions.

**Strongest case for (b):** a named quantitative-deletion/potential lemma and the restricted-lattice variant could save an alternative proof when realised starts are small or permit budgeted in-lattice swaps. **My recommendation is (a), with corrected CERT and explicit open alternatives.** Keep RM as the established a-priori output guarantee and SCR/SCR-C as landscape-based screening/exactness implications for the reported flag.
