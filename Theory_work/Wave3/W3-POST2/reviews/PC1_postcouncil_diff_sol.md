## Mathematical verification and dependency consequences

**E0, line by line.** The inherited definition in W3-POST/STATEMENT.md:36-38 minimizes over m>=s0 and imposes R_m<=n^(-(s0+2)/2). The source [T03R] PROOF.md:22 gives q_(m+1)/w(s0)=C(p,s0) product_{i=s0}^m rho_i. For m<min(B,K), q_(m+1) is present in R_m. Every denominator 2p-1-i is positive. If p>=2B, i<B implies rho_i>=(p-B)/(2p)>=1/4; otherwise rho_i>=1/(2p)>=1/(4B). There are m-s0+1<=B factors, each at least 1/(4B), and C(p,s0)>=1. Thus R_m>=(4B)^(-B), uniformly in p. Choosing n0 strictly above (4B)^(2B/(s0+2)) excludes every candidate m below min(B,K). If K<B this excludes all m<K and the admissible endpoint is K, where R_K=0. This is valid in the ticket's R1 domain s0<=K. E1 uses B=j0; D3 uses B=s0+j0; both are fixed functions of s0,A, so no new n0 dependence leaks into the stated rates. E2 and C case (iii) consume E1 and need no further hypothesis changes.

**Intercept, line by line.** By the truth convention in W3-POST/STATEMENT.md:31, ybar^r=alpha*+mean(X^r beta*)+epsilonbar, while mu=CX^r beta*. Consequently the raw fitted-minus-true mean is epsilonbar 1+(muhat-mu). Both fitted and true centered means lie in 1-perp. Squaring gives exactly n epsilonbar^2+||muhat-mu||^2. Repeating D0:12-17 yields the displayed Hellinger upper bound, and Gaussian noise gives n epsilonbar^2/sigma*^2~chi-square_1. For D(a), n h_int^2 is posterior-tight in sampling probability, hence every diverging M_n threshold works. For D(b), one must use slack rather than simply append a positive term to an exhausted bound: apply the uniform variance result D3:70-72 with M_n=sqrt(log n/2). Its squared contribution is <=(log n)/(2n) outside vanishing posterior mass; epsilonbar^2/(4sigma*^2)=o_P(log n/n), so it fits inside the remaining (log n)/(2n). Thus exactly C_H_prime=1+C_H/(4sigma*^2) still works. STATEMENT and REPORT carry this extension; no coefficient or selection premise changes.
