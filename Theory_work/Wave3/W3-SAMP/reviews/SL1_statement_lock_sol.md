- **ST / LT-b:** Gibbs exit targets S₀ △ {j} are distinct, so their exit probabilities sum to at most o₁. Each of U, I and the B neighbour labels contributes at most o₁ by invariance. Thus the round first-exit probability is at most (B+3)o₁ ≤ 19o₁. For every coordinate, the same pathwise bad-particle indicator controls all recording times: error ≤ κ₀ + B̄ + Λ̄. MR gives E[W B̄ | 𝓒] = δ; first-exit gives E[Λ̄ | 𝓒] ≤ 19o₁. Two Markov bounds prove LT-b without a factor p or posterior-size control. - **C-ii / C-iii:** These stronger-than-source constants are plausible and have a short derivation. Put b_t = 1−A_t and V_{t−1} = Σ_{s<t} b_s. Hölder gives q_t(S₀ᶜ)/q_t(S₀) ≤ δ̄. The stagewise version of C-i implies W_t ≥ [q_t(S₀)/π₀(S₀)]∏_{s<t} A_s. On V_{t−1} ≤ 1/2 this is at least 1/[2(1+δ̄)]. MR then gives E[b_t 1{V_{t−1}≤1/2} | 𝓒] ≤ 2(1+δ̄)(1−q_t(S₀)) ≤ 2δ̄, also for t=0. Pointwise, min(U,1/2) ≤ Σ_t b_t 1{V_{t−1}≤1/2}; hence C-ii follows by min(U,1) ≤ 2min(U,1/2). This uses stagewise weighted identities with a deterministic lower bound, not an unjustified optional-stopping identity.

  Also W ≥ [π(S₀)/π₀(S₀)]∏ A_t ≥ (1−δ)(1−min(U,1)), so (1−W)_+ ≤ δ+min(U,1). Mean one gives E|W−1| = 2E(1−W)_+ ≤ 2δ+8(T+1)δ̄ ≤ 18Tδ̄ for T≥1. Pooling uses convexity, with no island independence. LT-b at η=1/2 and P(W<1/2) ≤ 2E|W−1| give C-iv's 21 and 36. Its discovery bound also follows immediately from missing S₀ in the initial population: δ₀^{N_e} ≤ δ₀ ≤ δ̄.
