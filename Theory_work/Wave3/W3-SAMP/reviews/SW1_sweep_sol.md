1. **MAJOR — STATEMENT.md:85–88; lemmas/PC_completed.md:21–24. The HPM is incorrectly described as a function of the islands' statistical records.** PC(ii) says the reported HPM is a function “of these records,” alongside the PIPs and evidence. The specified statistical record does not contain rejected independence proposals, inherited models, or final local-search/LS0 evaluations. All can affect the HPM. W1-N1's record convention explicitly excludes the ledger (`Theory_work/Wave1/W1-N1/lemmas/L1_bridge.md:14–20`). CC2 correctly identifies the additional ledger dependence, but PC(ii) does not. **Fix:** separate the claims: evidence/PIPs/MPM are functions of island statistical records; HPM is the hpm_better-maximum of the final ledger's offered set, including the additional offers. Alternatively augment the records explicitly. The downstream HPM arguments already use the correct ledger version.

2. **MAJOR — STATEMENT.md:161–163; lemmas/TR_theorem.md:64–65. The advertised non-implication/non-nesting for the coded method is not established by the three-state example.** The example freely assigns posterior and start masses. Here the start is specifically the block product of the same model's Bayes factors, not an arbitrary positive distribution. In fact a three-state coded space has p = 2, K = 1; every feasible model is empty or a singleton, so the block product equals BF on every feasible model, d ≡ 0, and π₀ = π. Thus that example cannot be a coded three-state instance with enormous M. The converse non-implication is asserted without an example as well. **Fix:** label the construction as an abstract positive-target example and restrict its conclusion to that generalization; retain a coded-method non-nesting claim only after providing regression data and the actual block construction realizing it. This does not invalidate TR's sufficient condition or its proof.

5. **MINOR — lemmas/LT_theorem.md:93–95; STATEMENT.md:133–134. “No output certifies W_r ≥ η” is too absolute.** The top store has capacity 40p + 1 (`Scripts/SMC_BVS/core/cpp/v3_ledger.h:72–78`).
