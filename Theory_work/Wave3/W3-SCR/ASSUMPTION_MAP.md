# W3-SCR — ASSUMPTION MAP (sharpness gate, PROTOCOL item 3) — for STATEMENT v3.3 (after the sweep R07)

Proof sketch of SCR-A / SCR-M in one line: *on the noise event of L7, a model S of size ≤ q missing a truth has a
missing truth t(S) whose unstandardised one-step gain g_{y_c}(t(S), S) is at least (√γ − σ b)₊² (positive part), and one
addition strictly improves F once that gain exceeds the exact penalty in the units of the current residual; so S is
not locally optimal — and, since every scanned neighbour reaches the ledger, S cannot be a scanned centre near the
best score either.* Where each assumption enters, and what is known about its necessity (numerics:
`numerics/SUMMARY_N1_N2.md`, `numerics/n2_out/`). "Necessary" is used only where a proof is cited.

## Outcome of the gate (recorded for HQ)
1. Strengthenings adopted (the brief pre-authorised "as strong as possible"; R02's proposals 1-5): visibility instead of
   the sparse floor; directional floor bridge; q_n up to c₁n/log(p ∨ n) and, in SCR-M, any deterministic family with
   positive gaps; all certified endpoints **and uncertified runs** (SCR-G); self-normalised SCR-A° and A1° (no
   fixed-coefficient requirement); geometric noise complexity; excess-set inequality (EC′) and exact recovery SCR-C
   (s₀ bounded, near-orthogonality floor); SCR-R; SCR-N.
2. Proved sharp: the empty-model visibility threshold (constant 1 at q = 0, for the statement about LO models; SCR-N).
   Everything else in the table is a sufficient condition. Exact recovery in general requires additional control of
   possible LO strict supersets; NF is one sufficient condition.
3. Open: a lower bound for the entropy term when q ≥ 1; the case of size K and truths masked by a proxy (R02
   proposal 6: quadratic contrasts over all legal moves — not adopted, no primitive corollary yet); an asymptotic
   exact-recovery theorem in terms of (α, ω); an information-theoretic necessity statement (in terms of Δ/σ² over a
   parameter class) is not attempted.
4. "Better theorem" review: `reviews/R02_better_theorem_astra.md`; disposition `reviews/R02_R03_R04_disposition.md`.
