The arbitrary-witness master theorem never asserted positivity for that choice. With the required witness, γ(1|∅) is of order n, the noise threshold is bounded, the crude denominator is of order n, and the resulting gap is positive eventually (a positive order-n term dominates the logarithmic penalty). The counterexample therefore no longer contradicts the statement. L2 also explicitly notes that maximising γ maximises γ/σ_S², since σ_S does not depend on t; the repair covers both margin conditions.
