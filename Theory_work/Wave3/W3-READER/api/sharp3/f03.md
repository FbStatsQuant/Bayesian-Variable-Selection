## W3-SAMP:TR — Theorem TR (terminal repair), `STATEMENT.md:165-177`; proof `lemmas/TR_theorem.md:26-59`

### (a) Hypotheses

**The minorisation constant 1/M is sharp for the proof's mechanism, but not for the coded kernel.**
- The proof (`TR_theorem.md:26-31`) uses only the single independence step per round and treats every other step as a π-invariant pass-through.
- For a pure independence Metropolis kernel the bound cannot be improved. From x* = argmax w, K_I(x*, y) = π(y)/M for y ≠ x*, so the holding probability is 1 − (1 − π(x*))/M ≥ 1 − 1/M. This is the classical result of Mengersen–Tweedie (1996) and Liu (1996): uniform ergodicity iff sup π/π₀ < ∞, exact rate 1 − 1/M.
- So "any bridge outcome" plus "independence step only" forces M, and e_L is attained up to the factor 1 − π(x*).
- The coded round also has p Gibbs steps and B + 1 swaps, which the proof ignores. The ticket's own "near" target shows repair with M = 1.3e6 (`ASSUMPTION_MAP.md:21`, `TR_theorem.md:79-80`). P-OVL∞ is therefore far from necessary for the method.

**P-OVL∞ is very demanding in production.**
- With L = ⌈log₂(n+1)⌉ it reads M = o_P(log n), where M is a supremum over all of Ω (`STATEMENT.md:79-81, 171-173`).
- It forces π₀(S₀)·log n → ∞ (`TR_theorem.md:65`). Theorem D needs only max(n, p)·log n·π₀(S₀) → ∞ (`STATEMENT.md:104-105`), a factor N_e ≥ max(2500, n, p) weaker.
- A global likelihood-ratio bound of order o(log n) over a model space of size about p^K, for a block-product start, looks hard to obtain when p ≫ n. I cannot prove it fails; the ticket lists primitive conditions as open (`REPORT.md:172-173`).

**P-CONC is stronger than the proof needs, in three places.**
- *Localisation.* Only 1_𝒜·o₁ → 0 is used for the PIP and MPM clauses (`TR_theorem.md:57`). Theorem C-v already states this localised form (`STATEMENT.md:158`); TR-d does not.
- *HPM clause.* It needs only P-MAP plus discovery. Under P-OVL∞, π₀(S₀) ≥ π(S₀)/M, so D_r·π₀(S₀) ≥ N_e(L+6)(1−δ)/M → ∞. Theorem D-iv then gives the HPM clause with miss probability at most exp(−N_e(L+6)(1−δ)/M). That is far smaller than TR-c's δ + e_L (`STATEMENT.md:169`) under production budgets. The HPM and discovery parts of TR-c and TR-d are dominated by D and could be dropped or cited from it.
- *PIP tracking.* The TV bound of TR-a gives |E[p̂_r(j) | 𝓒] − PIP_j| ≤ e_L for every j with no concentration at all. The reason is that o_j ∈ [0,1], π(o_j) = PIP_j, and the pre-step law is within e_{L+q−1} of π. Theorem C states the analogous bias bound (`STATEMENT.md:157`); TR omits it.

**Other premises.** Only the Metropolis form of the independence step and π-invariance of the steps after it are used. KERNEL is not excessive. The order law, N_e, grid, Hm and R′ are free.

### (b) Conclusion

**The factor ‖μ − π‖_TV is thrown away.**
- `TR_theorem.md:33-34` proves ‖μK₁^L − π‖_TV ≤ (1 − 1/M)^L·‖μ − π‖_TV and then bounds the last factor by 1.
- For a particle that ends the bridge at S₀, ‖δ_{S₀} − π‖_TV = δ. The burn-in step (`TR_theorem.md:47-49`) therefore gives, conditionally on 𝓗, a mean bad fraction of at most δ + (1−δ)λ₀ + e_L·[δ·A^{br}_{r,T} + (1 − A^{br}_{r,T})].
- Hence E[‖p̂_r − 1_{S₀}‖_∞ | 𝓒] ≤ g + e_L·(δ + E[1 − A^{br}_{r,T} | 𝓒]).
- This strictly improves TR-b and unifies the three regimes:
  - under NC_r, MR-b at s = 0 gives E[W(1 − A^{br}_T)] = δ, so 1 − A^{br}_T → 0;
  - under C, E[1 − A^{br}_T] ≤ δ + β/2, so the bound is g + e_L(2δ + β/2). The start-odds term β/2 in C-iv (`STATEMENT.md:156`) is damped by e_L;
  - under P-OVL∞, e_L → 0 as now.
- The asymptotic premise becomes 1_𝒜·e_L·(1 − A^{br}_{r,T}) → 0 in probability. It is implied by each of NC_r + P-CONC, P-START + P-CONC, and P-OVL∞, and it also covers event-wise mixtures of them.

**MPM: Markov where a concentration bound is likely available.**
- TR-c uses Markov at level ½ (`TR_theorem.md:54-55`), so MPM consistency needs e_L → 0.
- No resampling occurs at φ = 1. If (R3) gives a product law across particles given 𝓗, then Hoeffding gives, per island, a tail exp(−2N_e·t²) for the bad fraction. I believe this follows from the chain rule over execution order, but it must be checked against T12 (R3); the proof says independence is "not used", not that it fails.
- Then P(MPM ≠ S₀ | 𝓒) ≤ 4·exp(−2N_e(½ − g − e_L)₊²).
- With N_e ≥ max(2500, n, p), the MPM premise weakens from L/M → ∞ to L/M ≥ ln 2 + ε, that is M ≤ c·L with c < 1/ln 2. The failure probability becomes exponentially small in N_e.
- The same route gives ‖pip_primary − PIP‖_∞ ≤ e_L + O_P(√(log p / N_e)) without P-CONC. This appears to be the BLIND_astra2 Thm 5 form that the remark at `TR_theorem.md:74` set aside. It would give MPM consistency when the exact MPM equals S₀ with a margin, which is weaker than π(S₀) → 1.

**No rate is stated.** TR-d could say, as C-v does: if 1_𝒜(o₁ + e^{−L/M}) = O_P(r_n), the PIP error is O_P(r_n). In production e^{−L/M} ≤ n^{−1/(M ln 2)}, so bounded M gives a polynomial repair rate.

**What is already tight.** The g term is sharp in form: κ₀ is attained (`LT_theorem.md:108-109`) and δ is the stationary off-truth mass. The constant B + 3 is loose but harmless.

**Literature.**
- Yang–Wainwright–Jordan (2016) prove worst-case mixing of add/delete/swap Metropolis that is polynomial in (n, p, s₀). As I recall the order is p·n·s₀²·log p iterations, and it needs a beta-min condition, restricted eigenvalues and a size cap. Converted to rounds this far exceeds L = log₂ n, so it does not directly give terminal repair with the coded burn-in.
- Zhou, Yang, Vats, Roberts and Rosenthal (2022) give warm-start, drift-based bounds; that is the more relevant template.
- I am not sure which paper "Zhou–Yang–Chang–Liang" refers to and do not rely on it.
- The posterior-consistency papers (Narisetty–He, Castillo–Schmidt-Hieber–van der Vaart, Johnson–Rossell, Song–Liang) bear on P-CONC, not on M. I know of no result bounding sup π/π₀ for a block-product start.
