1. **Theory_work/Wave1C/C-MIS/STATEMENT.md:26,73–74,82–84; lemmas/L2_gaussian.md:19–20,52–58 — major. Unqualified identification with Song–Liang's target ignores centering/intercept conventions.** The package permits arbitrary row means m and an intercept and correctly derives the population regression slope *with an intercept*, Σ_CC⁻¹cov(x_C,y). On printed p. 957, however, SL first define β₀ by minimising E[(β_sᵀx_s − β*ᵀx)²] in their regression without an intercept. For noncentred variables that minimiser uses second moments, not covariance matrices. Their subsequent covariance formula (19) does not resolve the mismatch between that minimisation and the package's unrestricted nonzero-mean setting. For example, let x,z be independent N(1,1), candidate x, omitted z, y=z+ε. The package's slope is zero, whereas the minimiser without an intercept is E[xz]/E[x²]=1/2. Thus the categorical equality to SL's KL support is not established in the stated generality. **Exact fix:** retain the current MIS theorem, but qualify every identification with SL by population centering and removal of the intercept. Explicitly say that for arbitrary m the package targets the regression slope with an intercept; comparison with SL is made after centering, rather than identifying the two original experiments verbatim.

5. **Theory_work/Wave1C/C-MIS/STATEMENT.md:92–93 — minor. Zero projected support does not imply independence of omitted signal from candidates.** The parenthetical gloss of β^C=0 asserts that every omitted signal is independent of the candidates. Under GR, β^C=β_C+γ=0 can instead be caused by cancellation with γ≠0. For instance, an omitted contribution −x₁+z and retained slope β₁=1 give zero projected support although the omitted contribution depends on x₁. Even a zero aggregate projection does not imply that individual omitted variables are independent of candidates. **Exact fix:** replace the parenthetical by “β_C+γ=0, so the aggregate candidate regression slope vanishes; this may result from cancellation and does not require independence of individual omitted predictors.”
