**Reading.** Every correctly specified Song–Liang selection-level result (Thm 3, ξ_q, the MAP description of
Thm S), and the selection-level counterparts of Thm 1–2, has an analog for the exact posterior that is a one-line
consequence of 1 − π(S₀) → 0 (SL0, deterministic), with the polynomial rate √(log n/n) for p ≤ n^A and with specificity
added. Gaps: (i) Hellinger/predictive contraction and exponential rates (not attempted); (ii) exponential p; (iii) the
method's **shipped** pooled reported PIPs (R′ = 4) have no in-probability sure-screening theorem in the cited cards —
only the islandwise weighted bound, the stacking routes of M1(i), and the store-restricted route SL2; (iv) Theorem S's
hypotheses not inspected; (v) misspecification is C-MIS.
