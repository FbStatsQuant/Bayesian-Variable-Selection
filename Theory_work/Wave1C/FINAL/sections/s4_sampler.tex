After each of the four measured \texttt{population\_round(P,1.0)} calls the population is $(S_{rqi})_{i\le N_e}$, $q=1,\dots,4$ \src{CORE/cpp/v3_engine.h:355-361}; the v3.1 accumulator is $H_r(h)=(4N_e)^{-1}\sum_{q,i}h(S_{rqi})$, which for $h=1\{j\in S\}$ is exactly the coded \texttt{pip\_plain[j]}.

Let $\mathcal A\in\sigma(\mathcal C)$ be the successful-calibration event and $E_r=\mathcal A\cap\{r\le R'\}$. An identity $\EE[Y\mid\mathcal C]=b$ ``almost surely on $E_r$'' means $\EE[1_{E_r}Y\mid\mathcal C]=1_{E_r}b$ almost surely; no island record is asserted outside $E_r$ \srcq{W1/W1-N1/STATEMENT.md:94-99}{localised form}.
\begin{lemma}[N1]\label{lem:N1}
For every island $r$, every $q\in\{1,\dots,4\}$ and every jointly measurable $h(S;\mathcal C)$ with a deterministic bound $|h|\le B_h$, fixed before stream 10, almost surely on $\mathcal A\cap\{r\le R'\}$ (localised form):
\[
\EE\Bigl[\hat{Z}_r\cdot\tfrac1{N_e}\textstyle\sum_ih(S_{rqi})\Bigm|\mathcal C\Bigr]=Z\,\pi(h),\qquad\text{hence}\qquad \EE[\hat{Z}_rH_r(h)\mid\mathcal C]=Z\,\pi(h).
\]
No regime assumption on $X$ is made.
\end{lemma}

\end{corollary}
