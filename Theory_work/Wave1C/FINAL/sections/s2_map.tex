\section{The Song--Liang (2015) map}\label{sec:map}

Song \& Liang (2015, JRSS-B 77(5), 947--972) is the template for the story: posterior consistency, sure screening, thresholded and MAP-model consistency, and a misspecification theorem. The table updates C-PACK's map v2 \srcq{W1C/C-PACK/SONG_LIANG_MAP.md:1-12}{SONG_LIANG_MAP} with C-MIS, C-GROW and C-DLV/A1. Two global differences: (a) their correctly specified asymptotics also assume $P_n/n^\theta\to\infty$ for some $\theta>0$, while ours impose $p_n\le n^A$ (LH-iid: also $p_n\to\infty$) with no positive-power lower growth bound — different domains, not identified \srcq{W1C/C-PACK/SONG_LIANG_MAP.md:10-12}{P_n/n^θ → ∞}; (b) their Theorem~S is in an online supplement that was \textbf{NOT INSPECTED} (publisher access failed); comparison is with the main-text description only \srcq{W1C/C-PACK/REPORT.md:87}{Theorem S} .

\begin{plain}[the map in one paragraph]
Every correctly specified Song--Liang \emph{selection} result (Thm~3, $\xi_q$, MAP) has an exact-posterior analog that is a one-line consequence of $1-\pi(S_0)\to0$ (Lemma SL0, deterministic), with rate $\sqrt{\log n/n}$ for $p\le n^A$ and with specificity added. Beyond Song--Liang, the method's \emph{reported} model is proved consistent jointly with output on a general correlated class (GEN), on growing relevance sets including the study's AR(1) design (GROW), and under misspecification (C-MIS Cor.~D), and — with a one-line calibration fallback — unconditionally (Prop.~F). Gaps: Hellinger/predictive contraction and exponential rates; exponential $p$; shipped pooled $R'=4$ PIPs; Theorem~S's hypotheses not inspected; density-level misspecification.
\end{plain}
