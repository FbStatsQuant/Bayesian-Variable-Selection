\section{Scope and frontier}\label{sec:scope}

\begin{notproved}[what the paper must not claim]
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Selection consistency is asymptotic.} No quantitative finite-$n$ selection-consistency guarantee is proved, in particular none for the study. (Deterministic inequalities such as SL0, M5(0) and Lemma CAP, the sampler identities N1/Corollary E, and the ideal-machine delivery statement of Proposition~\ref{prop:F} keep their stated finite-$n$ scope.) Onsets: the TH09 binding condition first holds near $n\approx2\cdot10^6$ for the exhibited families (an illustration, not a universal onset) \srcq{SoP/04_theory.tex:811}{n\gtrsim2\cdot10^{6}}; the LH-iid onset of $L'_n\delta_n\le\lambda_0/2$ is $\approx10^{10}$ for typical constants (C-PACK council) \srcq{W1C/C-PACK/lemmas/I2_LHiid_unconditional.md:80-81}{≈ 10¹⁰}; PXN's proven margins are vacuous at $n=200$; GEN's visibility margins at the study size are 0.16--0.94 ($<1$); GROW's asymptotic events fail at tested $n$ and S5 needs $\log n\gtrsim96$. \textbf{The study ($n=200$) is not covered} by any asymptotic result; there, M5(0)'s finite-$n$ composition bracket (with the posterior-miss probability measured, not bounded) is the account.
\item \textbf{Polynomial dimension only}: $p\le n^A$ for fixed $A$; no uniformity in growing $A$; exponential $p$ (Song--Liang Cor.~1 regime) is open (CJ18).
\item \textbf{Growing relevance sets of factor type}: S5 / common-factor designs with growing $p$ and random-design S5 are not proved (GROW (C6)); obstacles: the sandwich needs $|Q|=O(\log n)$ and NA needs a factor-aware direction count \srcq{W1C/C-GROW/REPORT.md:86-90}{S5 / factor designs with growing p}. Growing $s_0$ is not covered anywhere.
\item \textbf{Delivery for the shipped calibration} is open: event (O) of Theorem~\ref{thm:D}. Delivery is proved only for the fallback calibration of Proposition~\ref{prop:F}, under IDEAL-W, MACH, CONFIG and the amended allocation, on LH, LH-iid and the GEN class (Proposition~\ref{prop:F}) and on the GROW class (Proposition~\ref{prop:FG}, \st{PROVED (A2), checked by A3, not council-passed}); not for the C-MIS rerun setting, random-row S5 or factor designs.
\item \textbf{Reported pooled PIPs}: no in-probability sure-screening theorem for the shipped $R'=4$ pooled PIPs; only the islandwise bound, M1(i) under stacking (\st{(h), (i): PROVED (by this seat, unchecked)}) and the store-restricted SL2 (§\ref{sec:pips}).
\item \textbf{Misspecification}: exact-model concentration is proved under GR (fixed $\tau^2$, fixed projected sparsity, T03♯ hypotheses on the projected truth), and for fixed candidate designs under EP with the hypotheses of Theorem~\ref{thm:F}, including its size-tail condition or the stated bounded-dimension/TL alternatives; Lemma Z also covers the stated bounded-dimension null-projection cases. EP with growing $p_{\mathcal C}$ and small residual without a proved size tail, approximate projection, non-Gaussian/heteroscedastic random omitted parts outside GR, random-design EP and $n$-dependent $\tau_n^2$ are open; no density-contraction analog of Song--Liang Thm~4.
\item \textbf{Hellinger/predictive contraction and exponential rates} (Song--Liang Thms 1--2): not attempted (CJ20).
\item \textbf{Memory.} The memory envelope proved sufficient for delivery is $O((n+p)^2+\mathrm{Cert}_M)$ words (C-DLV Lemma R; with MR1 (C-GROW), A1's $M_G$, same order); the shipped scorer is Gram-based (a Gram-free scorer is a declared, unshipped v3.2 item \srcq{SoP/02_method.tex:414-415}{C7 Gram-free scorer}), so quadratic memory in $p$ is a practical limit for $p\gg n$; no sub-quadratic bound is proved.
\item \textbf{Floating point.} Every theorem assumes EX-A (exact arithmetic, an idealisation). The only binary64 statement is Lemma UM-FP for the start-mode identification step, under FP-TAB, untested for the coded scorer; delivery also assumes IDEAL-W (the checked-overflow status \texttt{unsupported\_size} fires for any fixed word size at large enough $(n,p)$).
\item \textbf{Implementation premises} remain premises: GUARD-W audit, integrated-LS0 replay, worker equivalence, IND-T extension ($R'>4$), N9 START/KERNEL (candidate), e4 for the integrated LS0 centre (W1-N3: OPEN; used by the delivery results through TDP-IMPL, while the consistency results need only e4′ or `some maximiser'). LS0, TDP-E, MR1 (C-GROW) and the fallback are implemented on the unmerged C-ENG branch (head \texttt{8a27652}), not in the frozen core or on main; the branch preflight formula does not count the MR1 (C-GROW) LS0 term. Song--Liang's Theorem~S hypotheses were not inspected.
\end{enumerate}
\end{notproved}
