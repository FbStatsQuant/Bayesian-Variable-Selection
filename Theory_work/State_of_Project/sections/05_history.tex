\section{Work done: the campaign, reversals and lessons}\label{sec:history}

This section is the memory of the project. It records how the theory reached its present state, which claims were reversed or refuted and why, which routes are known dead ends, how results were checked, and how the work sits against the literature. Every reversal below was paid for with real compute and reviewer time; the loop must not pay for it again. Statuses of campaign-history IDs (three-digit IDs, ``Part B'' of the archive registry) are copied verbatim into the registry and rendered in Appendix~\ref{app:registry}; where a status is quoted here it is inside \verb|\status{}| and copied from \src{NBH/ID_REGISTRY.md}.

\begin{agentnote}[how to use this section]
Before you attack any target, (1) find it in the reversal entries (\S\ref{sec:history:reversals}) and in the do-not-retry table (\S\ref{sec:history:dead}); (2) check whether a campaign-era result already covers part of it (its three-digit ID, Appendix~\ref{app:registry}); (3) note which check level its inputs carry (\S\ref{sec:history:verif}). A route listed as refuted may be revisited only with a new idea that addresses the recorded reason for failure, and you must say so explicitly in your output. The old repository documents (\S\ref{sec:history:olddocs}) are \emph{not} current; never quote their statuses or targets as the present state.
\end{agentnote}

\subsection{Verification infrastructure and what each check level means}\label{sec:history:verif}

\paragraph{Status words of authors.} The campaign protocol allowed only: ``PROVED (a complete proof you checked line by line), PROOF SKETCH (the crux is identified and the steps are plausible, but not every step is verified), COUNTEREXAMPLE (with a reproducible script or explicit construction), CONJECTURE, and OPEN. Never upgrade a status. A proof here is a \textbf{candidate}: it is not reviewed and not adopted. Review happens later, by other model families'' \src{NBH/branch_snapshots/campaign/PROTOCOL.md:19}. In the theorem meeting a seat's own argument is ``PROVED (by this seat, unchecked)'' until a check is filed \src{NBH/branch_snapshots/MEET-THM/DOSSIER.md:104}.
