\section{Glossary of status words and review vocabulary}\label{app:glossary}

Meanings are as the sources define them; the source of each definition is cited. Status words are always copied verbatim and never upgraded. Mathematical symbols and design families are defined in Appendix~\ref{app:notation}.

COUNTEREXAMPLE & ``with a reproducible script or explicit construction'' \src{NBH/02b_theory_campaign_history.md:46}; ``(numerical, reproducible)'' marks a computed one. \\
REFUTED, RETIRED, CORRECTED, RETRACTED, STRUCK, QUALIFIED, SUPERSEDED, withdrawn & Registry Part B dispositions of claims that are false, obsolete, numerically corrected, withdrawn by their author, removed from a statement, or weakened; the exact word and its reason are in each row, e.g.\ \rid{RF101}, \rid{RF105}, \rid{RF270}, \rid{RF271}, \rid{RF274} \src{NBH/ID_REGISTRY.md:1003-1007}. \\

REFEREE & A repair or counterexample supplied by a cross-family referee without a PROVED label \src{NBH/02b_theory_campaign_history.md:53}. \\
{[APPLIED] / [PENDING]} & A repair a campaign run has written / not yet written \src{NBH/02b_theory_campaign_history.md:55}. \\
not adopted; adoption; locking & ``Locked'' = files frozen and audited; ``Adopted'' = Felipe accepted the result into the paper's theory; nothing is adopted \src{Docs/THEORY.md:21}; adoption and locking are Felipe's decision (\rid{DEC12}). \\
APPROVED (F.B., 2026-11-07) & This document's mark for the paper-1 theorem list approved by Felipe as the current targets; it changes no proof status (\rid{DEC01}). \\

\textbf{Term} & \textbf{Meaning (source)} \\
\midrule

SF (same-family) & ``a Claude run (Opus or Fable) checked a Claude run'' \src{NBH/02b_theory_campaign_history.md:54}. \\
XF (cross-family) & A review by a different model family: Astra A-REV1--9; PC1 = gpt-6.1-sol on Seats A, B, D; PC2 = Opus 5.5 on Seat C (Astra); CHK = PC1 on inherited proofs \src{NBH/03_results_and_conclusions.md:7}. \\
VERIFIED, VERIFIED*, GAP, ERROR & Verdict tags of the SF/XF reviews; VERIFIED* = ``verified after a repair or with a stated qualification''. ``A re-review never raises an earlier adverse code on the original text; repaired texts carry the re-review verdict'' \src{NBH/02b_theory_campaign_history.md:54}. \\
VERIFIED WITH AMENDMENT; CORRECT WITH AMENDMENT & The check accepted the proof or output form subject to amendments; for N9-KERNEL §§0--3 under CHK\_INHERITED the source records ``(amendments adopted)'' \src{NBH/02_theory.md:727}. Whether an amendment was adopted is stated per item, not implied by the verdict. \\
SAME-FAMILY-ONLY & Checked only within the Claude family (e.g.\ PROVED-LOCKED items; Thm5-LH) \src{NBH/03_results_and_conclusions.md:23}, \src{NBH/03_results_and_conclusions.md:337}. \\
AUTHOR-ONLY & Checked by nobody but its author (registrar flag; 51 of 166 cited IDs at the theorem meeting) \src{NBH/01_overview_and_timeline.md:214}. \\
TRUE-AS-WRITTEN / COUNTEREXAMPLE / NEEDS-HYPOTHESIS / AMBIGUOUS & Check-wave verdict on a clause: no counterexample found (say how you searched) / explicit construction + script / a missing hypothesis named / the readings and which is true \src{NBH/branch_snapshots/MEET-THM/CHECK_W1_BRIEF.md:17}. ``a TRUE-AS-WRITTEN is not a proof'' \src{NBH/branch_snapshots/MEET-THM/CHECK_W1_BRIEF.md:23}. \\
DESIGN-VERIFIED / DESIGN-ASSERTED / DESIGN-FAILS / NONE-GIVEN & Non-vacuity verdict: a concrete (for correlation theorems: genuinely correlated) design checked to satisfy every hypothesis / named but not verified / violates a hypothesis / none given \src{NBH/branch_snapshots/MEET-THM/CHECK_W1_BRIEF.md:18}. \\
PLAUSIBLE / GAP / IMPLAUSIBLE (route) & Route verdict: key lemma right and steps fit / a named step missing / why not \src{NBH/branch_snapshots/MEET-THM/CHECK_W1_BRIEF.md:19}. \\
W1PC1, W1PC2, W2PC1, W2PC2 & Verdict rows of check wave 1 or 2 by checker PC1 (Sol) or PC2 (Opus) \src{NBH/02_theory.md:727}; files in \path{MEET-THM/evidence/W1_PC1/} etc. W2PC1 also tallied repairs: ``repaired 57, repaired differently 16, new 15, not repaired 3'' \src{NBH/03b_reasoning_and_traces.md:404}. \\
CHK, CHK\_INHERITED & PC1's line-by-line check of inherited proofs (OWS-WS3/4 VERIFIED; WS6(d), WS7, N9 VERIFIED WITH AMENDMENT) \src{NBH/03b_reasoning_and_traces.md:407}. \\
A-REV1 … A-REV9 & The nine Astra cross-family reviews of 2026-11-02 (scopes in \S\ref{sec:history:verif}) \src{NBH/03b_reasoning_and_traces.md:384-394}. \\
PASS WITH CORRECTIONS & Verdict of the Sol close audit of the theorem package; ``the chair applied all of them'' \src{NBH/03b_reasoning_and_traces.md:408}. \\
LOCKABLE (LOCKABLE\_v2) & Strict rule: author status PROVED (not PROVED-UNDER) and SF VERIFIED (or after repair) and XF VERIFIED (or with qualification); the list ``does not adopt, lock or upgrade anything'' \src{NBH/branch_snapshots/T38-RECORD/LOCKABLE_v2.md:3}. \\
Lean: sorry-free; \texttt{lake build} green; fidelity GREEN/RED & Formal proof with no \texttt{sorry}; the build passes; a reviewer judged the Lean statement faithful to the frozen informal statement (``statement fidelity only'') or not; \texttt{stmtdiff.py} checks frozen statements \src{NBH/03_results_and_conclusions.md:143-146}. \\
NEW / NEW-COMP / KNOWN—CITE / MODEST & Novelty tags of the theorem-meeting cards, ``with checked citations'' \src{NBH/branch_snapshots/MEET-THM/DOSSIER.md:73}. KNOWN—CITE results become cited lemmas (IC4). \\
NEW-SYNTH & A new statement proposed by THM-SYNTH, ``PROOF SKETCH at most'', ``a proposal for an HQ, not a result'' \src{NBH/branch_snapshots/THM-SYNTH/THEOREM_LIST.md:5-6}. \\
IC1--IC7 & Inclusion criteria of the theorem meeting: statement, route, non-vacuous (exhibited design), not merely known, serves the goals, Lean core named, fallback \src{NBH/branch_snapshots/MEET-THM/DOSSIER.md:60-69}. \\
LICENSED / INCONCLUSIVE / holds\_nondeliv & Outcomes of the pre-registered ABL-CONF rule (\rid{CJ164}, \rid{CJ165}); holds\_nondeliv = the comparison arm failed to deliver within the cap \src{NBH/03b_reasoning_and_traces.md:260}. \\
ACCEPTED; NOT binding; REPORTING, allowed & Statuses of HQ rulings \rid{RL01}--\rid{RL05} \src{NBH/ID_REGISTRY.md:187-191}. \\
recorded dissent & A seat's disagreement with a placement, kept in the minutes (\rid{DS01}--\rid{DS06}) \src{NBH/ID_REGISTRY.md:206-211}. \\
UNCERTAIN & Marks a conflict between sources or something they do not settle \src{NBH/05_open_questions_and_next_steps.md:7}; rendered here as \texttt{uncertain} boxes. \\
DECIDED / OPEN / NOT TRIGGERED / IN PROGRESS & This document's status words for its own \texttt{DEC}, \texttt{ENG}, \texttt{FR} items (\S\ref{sec:open}). \\
