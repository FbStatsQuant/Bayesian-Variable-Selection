\section{Empirical results}\label{sec:empirics}
% Agent E. Every table marked "Generated" is produced by scripts/make_tables.py from the cited file in Results/;
% re-run it with `python scripts/make_tables.py` (deterministic; prints every table and its consistency checks).

This section reports every empirical result of the project: the New Campaign study (2026-10-28 to 2026-11-03) at
low $p$ (exact posterior available) and at $p\gg n$ (truth only), and the numerical companions run during the theory
campaign (``New BVS Headquarters'', 2026-11-02 to 04). It adds no analysis: every number is either generated from a
machine-readable file in \path{Results/} by \texttt{scripts/make\_tables.py} (tables marked ``Generated''), or
transcribed with its source. The empirical phase is closed: ``the New Campaign's empirical phase is COMPLETE (HQ D94)''
\src{Docs/HQ/STATUS.md:3}. Key facts get stable IDs \texttt{EMP01}--\texttt{EMP27} (registry rows in
\path{registry/empirics.tsv}); \S\ref{sec:emp-theory} maps them to the theory IDs they inform.

\begin{plain}[how to read the numbers]
A fit ``recovers'' when its selected set equals the true support $S_0$ exactly; failed or stopped fits count as
misses \src{Results/2026-10-28_New_Campaign/H2_20261102/HEADLINE_H.md:3}. At low $p$ the SMC output is compared with
the exact posterior of the \emph{same} model (full enumeration of all $2^p$ models); at $p\gg n$ ``the true Z, exact HPM
and exact PIPs are not computed'' (D22) \src{Docs/HQ/DECISIONS.md:34}, so only selection against the truth is reported
\src{Results/2026-10-28_New_Campaign/H2_20261102/HEADLINE_H.md:24}. The reports' verdict scale (support / contradict /
silent) is about what one $n=200$ design can show; ``failure of a finite-n \emph{premise} of a conditional statement is
reported as such, and is \textbf{not} a contradiction of the implication''
\src{Results/2026-10-28_New_Campaign/H2_20261102/EVIDENCE_H.md:14-17}.
\end{plain}

\subsection{Study design}\label{sec:emp-design}

\paragraph{Data generator (both regimes).} $n=200$; $X$ is drawn as $N(0,\Sigma_s)$ rows (``X first via chol, then
noise''), column-standardised (\texttt{scale}, sd with $n-1$), $\varepsilon\sim N(0,1)$, and $Y_c = c\,X\beta+\varepsilon$
for the stored scales; only $c\in\{1,0.5\}$ (\texttt{c100}, \texttt{c050}) are fitted
\src{Scripts/Data_generators/gen_prod_data.R:28-40} \src{Scripts/Campaigns/2026-10-28_New_Campaign/code/lowp/LOWP_SPEC.md:13-17}
\src{Scripts/Campaigns/2026-10-28_New_Campaign/code/lowp/LOWP_SPEC.md:70}. The scenario covariances are built on
\textbf{groups of 5 consecutive predictors} (\texttt{group <-} \texttt{ceiling(seq\_len(p)/5)}), $H=\mathbf 1\{\text{same group}\}$
\src{Scripts/Data_generators/gen_dev_data.R:18-30}:

scenario & $\Sigma_s$ (verbatim construction, \path{gen_dev_data.R:23-29}) \\
\midrule
S1 & $I$ \\
S2 & $0.5^{|j-k|}$ (AR(1)) \\
S3 & $0.7H+0.3I$ (equicorrelation 0.7 within each group of 5) \\
S4 & $0.85H+0.15I$ (equicorrelation 0.85 within groups) \\

\paragraph{Truth.} One signal per group of 5, placed by \texttt{truth(p)}: $k=0,\dots,s_0-1$, block $=1+\text{step}\cdot k$,
active index $=5(\text{block}-1)+\text{pos}[k\bmod 6]$ with pos $=(3,2,4,1,5,3)$, $\beta=\text{pattern}[k\bmod 6]$ with
pattern $=(1.4,-1.0,1.8,-1.2,0.8,-1.6)$; $s_0=14$ with step 7 ($p=500$) and 13 ($p=1000$)
\src{Scripts/Data_generators/gen_dev_data.R:33-42}. At low $p$ the same rule with step 1 gives $s_0=4$ at $p=20$
(active 3, 7, 14, 16; $\beta=1.4,-1.0,1.8,-1.2$) and $s_0=5$ at $p=30$ (active 3, 7, 14, 16, 25; $\beta=1.4,-1.0,1.8,-1.2,0.8$)
\src{Scripts/Campaigns/2026-10-28_New_Campaign/code/lowp/LOWP_SPEC.md:21-26}. Nominal SNR $c^2\beta'\Sigma_s\beta$ at low
$p$ ranges from 5.65 to 8.28 at $c=1$ and 1.41 to 2.07 at $c=0.5$
\src{Scripts/Campaigns/2026-10-28_New_Campaign/code/lowp/LOWP_SPEC.md:39-48}; the smallest eigenvalue of $\Sigma_s$ is
0.15 in S4 and 0.10 in S6 at $p=30$ \src{Scripts/Campaigns/2026-10-28_New_Campaign/code/lowp/LOWP_SPEC.md:32-35}.

\begin{agentnote}[the study design is not a theory family]
The study generator has groups of size $g=5$. The theory's look-alike exhibit LH uses $g=9$ (``SIZE: $g_{\max}\le B<2g_{\min}$;
holds on LH for $n\ge 9^6$ with $g=9$'') \src{NBH/notation_and_conventions.md:434}, and ``The study generator ($g=5$,
$2g\le B$) is the obligation PART-pairing (N8)'' \src{NBH/notation_and_conventions.md:259}, recorded as
``OPEN; Prop Q PROOF SKETCH; W7-PART CE2 COUNTEREXAMPLE'' \src{NBH/05_open_questions_and_next_steps.md:94}. The archive
states it plainly: ``the $g=5$ study is not the LH exhibit'' \src{NBH/03_results_and_conclusions.md:74}. No theorem about LH or
LH-iid therefore applies to these data as stated; the data can only be consistent or inconsistent with observable
consequences.
\end{agentnote}

\subsection{Low \texorpdfstring{$p$}{p}: SMC-BVS against the exact posterior}\label{sec:emp-lowp}

PROD-L: ``1,200 datasets (p ∈ \{20, 30\}, S1–S6, 100 replicates) × c ∈ \{1, 0.5\} = 2,400 cases. The exact posterior comes
from full enumeration of all 2\textasciicircum p models. Truth was used only in scoring. Reconciliation: enumeration 1200/1,200,
SMC primary 2400/2,400, SMC repeats 12000/12,000. Failed: 0. Missing: 0.''
\src{Results/2026-10-28_New_Campaign/L_20261102/HEADLINE_L.md:3}. Review: ``Terra tie-outs PASS, Sol ACCEPT (D75, D94)''
\src{Docs/HQ/STATUS.md:24}.

\begin{divergence}[which PIP the study reports]
Every SMC-BVS PIP in this section (low and high $p$) is pooled over islands with \emph{equal weights} by the frozen v2.2.1 core:
the RB PIP is \texttt{pip\_primary}, the equal-weight mean of the island RB PIPs
\src{Scripts/Campaigns/2026-10-28_New_Campaign/code/core/v2_2_1/core/v3_run.R:126-129}, and the plain PIP is \texttt{pip\_plain},
the equal-weight mean of the island plain PIPs \src{Scripts/Campaigns/2026-10-28_New_Campaign/code/core/v2_2_1/core/v3_run.R:167-168}
(see \S\ref{sec:method-outputs}). Equal-weight pooling is the v3.1 \emph{secondary} PIP; the v3.1 primary ($\hat Z$-weighted) exists only through POST HOC rescoring,
done on 3 designs \src{NBH/branch_snapshots/MEET-v31/METHOD_v3.1_FINAL.md:54}. See the divergence box ``primary PIP'' in
\S\ref{sec:method-impl}. Theorems stated for the $\hat Z$-weighted PIP are therefore not directly tested by these tables.
\end{divergence}

\begin{itemize}
\item \defid{EMP01} \textbf{HPM is exact; 0 search misses.} ``SMC HPM = exact HPM in 2,400/2,400 primary fits and
12,000/12,000 repeat fits. HPM = truth 92.3\%. Every miss is a miss of the exact posterior itself; there are 0 search
misses'' \src{Docs/HQ/STATUS.md:29}. Miss classes: 2,216 hits, 0 search misses, 184 posterior misses, ``F(S₀) ≤ F(HPM)'' in
every miss, no ties (closest gap $-0.0017$, median $-1.10$) \src{Results/2026-10-28_New_Campaign/L_20261102/EVIDENCE_L.md:101}.
\item \defid{EMP02} \textbf{Evidence $\hat Z$.} Over the 2,400 primary fits $e=\log\hat Z-\log Z$ has ``bias -0.0002, RMSE 0.012,
median |e| 0.007, and 90th-percentile |e| 0.020'', and ``the 0.05·Ẑ lower bound covered Z in 100\% of fits (0 violations)''
\src{Results/2026-10-28_New_Campaign/L_20261102/HEADLINE_L.md:7}. Over all 14,400 fits the mean relative error
$\hat Z/Z-1$ is $-0.00011$ with SE 0.00010 (``within 1.1 SE of 0''; ``cancellation across cases cannot be excluded''), and the
lower bound ``never fails, in 14,400/14,400 fits'' \src{Results/2026-10-28_New_Campaign/L_20261102/EVIDENCE_L.md:150-154}.
\item \defid{EMP03} \textbf{PIPs and MPM.} Mean per-fit max $|\mathrm{PIP}-\mathrm{PIP}^{\rm exact}|$ is 0.0005 (RB, 90th pct
0.0014) and 0.0020 (plain, 90th pct 0.0033); ``The MPM equals the exact MPM in 99.9\% of cases (RB) and 99.8\% (plain). The RB
PIPs are about 4× closer.'' \src{Results/2026-10-28_New_Campaign/L_20261102/HEADLINE_L.md:9}.
\item \defid{EMP04} \textbf{Seed stability.} ``all 6 seeds gave the same HPM in \textbf{100\%} of cases (2,400/2,400)''; the sd
of $\log\hat Z$ across seeds has mean 0.011 (90th pct 0.018, max 0.030), ``similar to the RMSE against the exact value
(0.012), consistent with seed variability dominating the error''; the 12,000 extra-seed fits ``are as accurate as the
primaries'' \src{Results/2026-10-28_New_Campaign/L_20261102/HEADLINE_L.md:150-153}. The spread uses primary $+$ 5 extra seeds,
``a slight widening of the brief's `across the 5 extra seeds' definition'' \src{Results/2026-10-28_New_Campaign/L_20261102/HEADLINE_L.md:148}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{What the empirics do not show}\label{sec:emp-limits}

\begin{notproved}[empirical scope]
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{One $n$.} Every study fit has $n=200$; consistency, rates and asymptotic statements are ``silent'' in every evidence report
\src{Results/2026-10-28_New_Campaign/L_20261102/EVIDENCE_L.md:87} \src{Results/2026-10-28_New_Campaign/H2_20261102/EVIDENCE_H.md:189}.
Two values of $p$ per regime ``cannot establish a rate'' \src{Results/2026-10-28_New_Campaign/L_20261102/EVIDENCE_L.md:88}.
\item \textbf{One generator, not a theory family.} Groups of 5; not LH ($g=9$) or LH-iid (\S\ref{sec:emp-design}).
\item \textbf{No accuracy at $p\gg n$.} No $\hat Z$ or PIP accuracy is claimed at $p\gg n$ (D22)
\src{Results/2026-10-28_New_Campaign/H2_20261102/EVIDENCE_H.md:8}; no head-to-head $\hat Z$/PIP comparison against other Bayesian methods
exists \src{NBH/03_results_and_conclusions.md:181}.
\item \textbf{Discovery at $p\gg n$.} Whether $S_0$ was scored in the 565 completed misses is unidentified in the repo reports and asserted by S-ELS
(\rid{EMP12}); T21's probability bound is untested; $2E_0[1-\pi(S_0)]$ was not evaluated at $p\gg n$.
\item \textbf{Repair.} The final local search never made an improving move at $p\gg n$; repair ``by improving moves: not exercised''.
At low $p$ first-score attribution ``neither tests the hypothesised local-search repair mechanism nor rules out a later role''.
\item \textbf{TD at $p\gg n$} covers $d=0$ only, on a 196-case sample enriched for misses; the move-type diagnosis covers 4 cases
\src{Results/2026-10-28_New_Campaign/H2_20261102/EVIDENCE_H.md:231-233}.
\item \textbf{Stops.} The P5 retry was not run, so ``The observed retry effect is unknown''.
\item \textbf{Benchmarks.} AI-written code, partial G5; ABESS not RSS-optimal; EN $\alpha$ at the grid edge; benchmark comparisons are ``a
comparison of \emph{targets} as much as of \emph{algorithms}'' (F-REFEREE) \src{NBH/03_results_and_conclusions.md:181}.
\item \textbf{Theory-campaign runs} are mostly author-only, post hoc, or on 3--12 designs; LS0 is not in the production core.
\end{itemize}
\end{notproved}

\begin{agentnote}[empirical facts a theorist must explain or must not contradict]
\begin{enumerate}
\item At low $p$ the SMC HPM equals the exact HPM in 14,400/14,400 fits and there are 0 search misses (\rid{EMP01}); any theorem whose
finite-$n$ reading predicts search failures on such designs must explain this.
\item At $n=200$ \textbf{every observed wrong report among completed fits is a posterior (target) error}: 184/184 at low $p$ (exact), and at $p\gg n$ each of the 565
completed misses has a scored competitor strictly above $S_0$ (min gap 0.0049 nats); the other 6 high-$p$ errors are stopped fits with no output (\rid{EMP10}) (\rid{EMP11};\rid{LIM07} ``true by definition of the
fixed target''). These observed competitor defeats establish target-mode misses \emph{for the existing fits} only.
\begin{uncertain}[withdrawn inference: ``a start change can add at most the 6 stopped fits'']
One archive summary still states: ``no start or search change can fix them (API-RESID: a start change can add at most the 6 stopped
fits, 0.25\%)'' \src{NBH/03_results_and_conclusions.md:183}. This inference was withdrawn at the method meeting: ``The argument that the
backfit start could gain at most 6 of 2,400 fits doesn't follow'' \pos{1480} \src{NBH/03b_reasoning_and_traces.md:92} (see \rid{MD01} and
reversal H.18 in \S\ref{sec:history}). What stands is the definitional fact \rid{LIM07}: under the fixed target, no search can make the
HPM equal $S_0$ when $F(S_0)<F(\hat S)$ \src{NBH/branch_snapshots/MEET-v31/MINUTES.md:92}. Do not cite the 6-fit cap.
\end{uncertain}
\item Do not claim ``$S_0$ was scored in every completed fit'' at $p\gg n$ without the \rid{EMP12} conflict.
\item The start is masked almost everywhere ($Q_f\pi_0(S_0)<1$ in 2,393/2,394; median $\log_{10}\pi_0(S_0)$ down to $-25.6$), yet at high $p$ the reported HPM was
first scored in the pilot in all 2,394 completed fits (for the 1,829 hits mostly by Gibbs flips, 1,724, and neighbour swaps, 104), and at low $p$ the truth was always first scored in the pilot (initial draw 1,304, Gibbs flip 1,038 of 2,400) (\rid{EMP15}, \rid{EMP17}). At $n=200$ ``The rigorous a-priori bounds (T21 routes) are \textbf{vacuous}''
\src{NBH/branch_snapshots/API-N200/REPORT.md:12}; EVIDENCE\_H: ``This motivates a pilot-discovery / T24-type route for T38''
\src{Results/2026-10-28_New_Campaign/H2_20261102/EVIDENCE_H.md:123}.
\item Literal truth dominance fails in every checked case (low $p$ 2,400/2,400; high $p$ TD(0,0) 196/196, a $d=0$ check on a miss-enriched sample), via truth-in swaps ($d\ge1$) and lateral
truth-for-truth swaps ($d=0$) (\rid{EMP16}); a usable repair premise ``must tolerate lateral (|S Δ S₀|-preserving) moves''
\src{Results/2026-10-28_New_Campaign/H2_20261102/EVIDENCE_H.md:102}.
\item Error shapes: supersets dominate at $c=1$; equal-size wrong supports (37\% of completed misses) and subsets at $c=0.5$ (\rid{EMP13}).
\item The N200 prediction (observed $P(U)$ plus the one-move term $\sum\hat P_0(L\mid X)$, ``a lower bound plus a measured term'') is 558 vs 571 observed wrong over 2,400 fits (\rid{EMP20}); an upper bound on $\Pi(X)$ at finite $n$ is open.
\item RB PIPs are about 4$\times$ more accurate than plain PIPs at low $p$ (\rid{EMP03}); $\hat Z$'s pooled relative bias is within 1.1 SE of 0.
\item Cost from $p=500$ to 1000 grows like $2^{1.07}$--$2^{1.5}$ in work (\rid{EMP18}); two points, no rate.
\item SSL beats the exact HPM of SMC-BVS's own model at low $p$ (2,251 vs 2,216; \rid{EMP05}): ``The exact posterior is that of the
SMC-BVS model, not an oracle, so another prior can recover more often''
\src{Results/2026-10-28_New_Campaign/L_20261102/EVIDENCE_L.md:265}.
\end{enumerate}
\end{agentnote}
