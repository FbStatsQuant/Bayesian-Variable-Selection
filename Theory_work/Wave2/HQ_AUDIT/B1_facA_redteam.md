### 1. MINOR — numerical seeds depend on the interpreter's randomized string hash

**Locations:** `Theory_work/Wave2/W2-FAC-A/numerics/fac_common.py:72`; consumer `Theory_work/Wave2/W2-FAC-A/numerics/d_run.py:14`, seed metadata at `d_run.py:25`, process pool at `d_run.py:100`.

`build()` adds `hash(design) % 9973` to the nominal random seed. Consequently the saved tuple `(design, mode, p, n, c, seed)` does not specify the generated data. With `PYTHONHASHSEED` unset here, two fresh Python invocations returned `2493` and `779` for `hash('CS3') % 9973`. Windows spawned workers can also have different hash salts, making job-to-worker assignment relevant. This affects reproducibility of the D1 observations, not the exact rational certificates or the proofs. The separate D2 exact simulator and FNA driver use explicit integer seeds and do not have this particular defect.

**Exact fix:** replace the hash term with a fixed mapping, e.g.

```python
design_id = {"S5": 1, "CS3": 2, "CS5": 3, "F2": 4, "F2r": 5}[design]
effective_seed = (1000003 * seed + 7919 * n + 104729 * p
                  + design_id + (0 if mode == "iid" else 17))
rng = np.random.default_rng(effective_seed)
```

Record `effective_seed` in newly generated raw rows, and distinguish regenerated D1 summaries from historical rows whose effective seeds were not recorded. Do not silently present the new runs as exact reproductions of the old ones. No theorem amendment is needed.

## 2. Key lemma: the exponential family is controlled, not wished away

**Checked:** `lemmas/L1_uniform_add.md:14–65,74–121`; `STATEMENT.md:104–114`. Paths in this and subsequent sections are relative to `Theory_work/Wave2/W2-FAC-A/` unless specified otherwise.

The exact decomposition is correct. In the latent Gaussian Hilbert space, the span of the observed coordinates in T has codimension one inside `span(ξ₀, ζ_T)`. Its orthogonal complement is generated by

\[
\omega_T=\xi_0-\lambda_T^\top\Psi_{TT}^{-1}\zeta_T,
\qquad \operatorname{Var}(\omega_T)=1+s_T.
\]

The local residual `ζ_l − Ψ_lU Ψ_UU^{-1} ζ_U` is orthogonal to that whole larger span. The residual-factor coefficient is exactly `λ̃_{l|U}/sqrt(1+s_T)`. Dividing the orthogonal sum by its variance gives (1.1), including the empty-set case. The independence assertion is for each fixed pair `(T,l)`; no independence across the entire lattice is assumed or needed.

**The essential distinction:** the proof does not establish `sup_T |W_T| = O_P(sqrt(log p))`. Such an assertion would be false. In singleton-group compound symmetry, selecting a positive-noise fraction of the coordinates makes `|W_T|` of order `sqrt(p)`. The factor contribution to the add statistic has an additional coefficient of order `1/sqrt(|T|)`, and it is that weighted contribution that the proof bounds.

Explicitly, with `h = |𝒢(T)|`, block quadratic energies `Q_g = ζ_g' Ψ_gg^{-1} ζ_g`, and the notation of L1,

\[
|Z_{T,l}|\le |\Xi_{l,U}|+
 \kappa'\frac{|\xi_0|}{1+s_T}
 +\kappa\sqrt{\frac{\sum_{g\in\mathcal G(T)}Q_g}{h}}.
\]

The deterministic steps are valid: restriction of the quadratic variational problem gives `q_{T∩g} ≤ Q_g`; Cauchy–Schwarz gives the factor numerator bound; and `s_T ≥ c_min h` supplies the denominator. These inequalities hold for every T on every realization, including a noise-adaptive T.

The probability argument really has three components:

1. At most `p 2^(γ̄−1)` local innovation normals.
2. One scalar `ξ₀`.
3. For each h, all `binom(G,h)` sets of h **groups**, with a chi-square threshold charged at `log binom(G,h) + 2 log(h+1) + log log n`.

Thus exponential subset entropy is present and paid for. After division by h it becomes `log(eG/h)`, producing the stated size-dependent envelope. Arbitrary within-group choices are covered by `q_{T∩g} ≤ Q_g` without a second union over those choices. The sum over h costs at most `1/log n`. The `2^p` individual W-statistics are never union-bounded as if they were a polynomial family. L1:25–29 already states this accurately; preserve that explanation when summarizing the result.

The bound on `c_min` and hence on `κ'` is also uniform in the class: quadratic variational monotonicity gives `c_min = min_j λ_j²/Ψ_jj`, while the unit diagonal and `Ψ ⪰ ψ₀I` give `c_min ≤ (1−ψ₀)/ψ₀`. No unmentioned lower bound on the factor loading size is needed.

**Independent falsification:** for p=9 I exhaustively evaluated every `(T,l)` with `l∉T`, including T empty, in 12 designs: four CS, four block CS, and four correlation-normalized designs with signed loadings and independently generated positive definite 3-coordinate local blocks. Seed: `20261109`. There were **27,648 subset/addition pairs**. Maximum direct-innovation versus split error: **1.7763568394002505e−15**. Violations of the deterministic L1 bound: **0**. These were direct covariance solves, not calls to the ticket's `innov()` or `decomp()` functions.

## 3. Start containment, dense starts, K, and the search cap

**Checked:** `STATEMENT.md:18–22,43–59,90–95,115–122,168–171`; `lemmas/L2_sandwich.md:8–43`; `lemmas/L4_assembly.md:13–36`; inherited `Theory_work/Wave1C/C-GROW/lemmas/L1_descent.md:24–73`.

The statement explicitly excludes the dangerous eventual `p>K` regime: `(P)` gives `p≤n^α₁` for some `α₁<α₀≤1/6`, hence `p<n/2` and `K=p` eventually. At that point a start maximizer either has room to add a missing truth, or has size K=p and already contains every truth. The block-score increase from (TV) dominates the `O(log n)` prior/determinant costs and the polynomial-family noise envelope. This proves simultaneous containment for **every** start maximizer, not just a preferred tie winner.

On containment, NA and TL imply that every best neighbor of a nontruth lattice state is a deletion of an extra: extra deletions improve by reversing NA; a swap deleting an extra is worse than the corresponding pure deletion by NA; truth-removing moves lose by TL. Thus there are exactly `τ=|S̃₀\S₀|` moves. The merged cap `ℓ_n+|S̃₀|` exceeds τ, and the last needed scan is included. Production agrees: `Scripts/SMC_BVS/core/cpp/v3_core.cpp:469–476` computes the mode and adds its size to the LS0 cap. This argument does not require a logarithmic-size start.

**Dense-start wording must remain empirical.** Universal convergence `S̃₀=[p]` is not a consequence of these hypotheses, and the locked statement correctly labels it “observations, not proofs.” A counterexample to that stronger interpretation is exact CS with `a=0.3`, p=9, truths at coordinates 0 and 1 with coefficients `(1,−1)`. It satisfies (GC-CS), (TV), the Gram floor, and fixed-p (P). In exact arithmetic the complete-linkage tie rule and B=3 give blocks `{0,1,2}`, `{3,4,5}`, `{6,7,8}`. Null-only blocks have zero population signal; after conditioning on both truths, coordinate 2 also has zero signal. Fixed-dimensional score penalties therefore exclude these nulls asymptotically. The start tends to S₀, rather than [p].

I independently enumerated all 512 models for that example using the shipped determinant/ridge/IG score and BB prior, and built γ₀ from the block scores. With seed `1092026`, at n=1000 and n=1,000,000, both the start mode and exact HPM were `{0,1}`, with **zero NA and TL violations** over all relevant lattice moves. This is a scope check, not a counterexample to FAC-L. No extra “start becomes full” assumption should be inserted into its proof.

For `p>K`, even containment is not justified by this argument: a capped maximizer can omit a truth and have no feasible addition. The theorem makes no such extension. Larger-cap swap heuristics in Part III do not repair that missing argument and are correctly outside Parts I–II.

## 4. Approximate Grams and iid-row transfer

**Checked:** `lemmas/L1_uniform_add.md:123–165`; `lemmas/L3_NA_TL.md:11–80`; `lemmas/L5_random_instances.md:9–59`.

The perturbation proof does not lose a hidden factor `||Σ||`, despite a pervasive factor eigenvalue of order p. Both regression vectors have uniformly bounded Euclidean norm because `c_M' M_TT c_M ≤ M_ll=1`; both innovation variances have a fixed lower bound. Consequently the innovation coefficient vectors are uniformly Lipschitz in operator-norm Gram error. The square-root coupling

\[
\gamma=\Sigma^{1/2}R^{-1/2}x
\]

also has a Lipschitz constant controlled by the minimum eigenvalues, not the maximum eigenvalues. Combining these yields the stated pathwise bound `L_ψ δ ||x||`, simultaneously over all T,l. The weighted chi-square norm bound is valid even when `||R||` is order p; at `u=log log n` it supplies the deterministic high-probability allowance `L_ψ δ sqrt(5(p+1) log log n)`. The assumption `δ_n sqrt(p log log n)→0` is sufficient. The latent-factor construction on an enlarged space is correctly converted to an event concerning only the original innovations, so it can be intersected with the response-norm events without claiming independence.

In the same 27,648 independently enumerated cases I also checked L1(d) using `R=.999Σ+.001V`, where V was a separately generated positive definite correlation matrix, and the prescribed square-root coupling. Violations: **0**. Largest discrepancy divided by the explicit proved allowance: **0.011231976368392382**.

For iid Gaussian rows the entrywise event yields `δ_n≤p δ'_n`, with `δ'_n=O(sqrt(log n/n))`. Hence

\[
\delta_n\sqrt{p\log\log n}
=O\!\left(p^{3/2}\sqrt{\log n\log\log n/n}\right)\to0
\]

under the strict exponent condition. The argument is conservative but sufficient. The design-dependent actual partition causes no conditional union-bound problem: conditional on X it is fixed; (TV-pop) covers it on the stated design event. Fixed dimension of `U∪{j}∪S₀` and the Gram floor give a common Lipschitz constant for the population signal transfer. Thus one obtains common energy, beta-min, visibility and Gram envelopes on the same events.

The posterior transfer uses the required every-deterministic-sequence argument, not the invalid implication “conditional convergence implies joint convergence.” A nonvanishing supremum of conditional expected posterior error would select a deterministic design subsequence contradicting T03♯; bounded posterior error then permits integration. I found no missing joint-law step.

For completeness, the NA exponent calculation pays the BB ratio correctly. With `P=log p`, `x=log(p/k)`, the leading squared envelope is `2P(1+κ̄ sqrt(x/P))²`, and the budget includes `2x+O(1)`. Maximizing after subtracting `2x` gives `2κ̄(κ̄+2)P`, hence `α₀=1/[2κ̄(κ̄+2)]`. The uniform remainders are `o(sqrt(log n))` before squaring. The Gram-based residual floor is uniform for all supersets because `p=o(n)`. ## 6. SHARP survives the noise-adaptive witness attack

**Checked:** `STATEMENT.md:154–159`; `lemmas/L6_sharp.md:9–47`.

For singleton CS, the witness's innovation is exactly

\[
Z_{T,l}=\frac{\eta_l-H_T/(k+c)+\xi_0\sqrt c/(k+c)}
 {\sqrt{1+1/(k+c)}},\qquad c=(1-a)/a.
\]

Taking m=ceil(log p) extreme positive null coordinates while adding the extreme negative null coordinate gives two contributions of the same sign. The mth-largest threshold used in the proof has expected exceedance count much larger than m; the minimum threshold likewise has diverging expected exceedance count. Fixed s₀ and fixed c are negligible compared with m. Hence `Z²≥(8−o(1))log p` with probability tending to one. An elementary upper bound by `max_j |η_j|=(1+o_P(1))sqrt(2log p)` also supplies the equality-in-order stated in STATEMENT:157.

The score comparison is separately valid. It uses only the particular small sets `T,A`, not the full-lattice RSS lower bound requiring `p=o(n)`. Since `RSS_T≤||Cε||²/s_Y²`, the likelihood log-ratio has lower bound `(1−o(1))Z²−O(1)`. Ridge and determinant corrections are bounded as claimed; the prior costs at most `2log p+O(1)` in the doubled score. This gives `(6−o(1))log p−log n−O(1)>0` under the strict liminf condition. The witness has only `O(log p)` coordinates and is feasible even in L6's stronger `p≤n−1` version. The locked statement's additional `p≤K` is therefore safe.

The latent witness is legitimate: on the enlarged space it produces an actual violating pair of coordinate sets, so its success implies the original-space event `NAᶜ`; it need not itself be measurable from the original noise. No conditioning on a noise-selected set's nominal normal law occurs.

As a separate score check, I generated 100 exact-Gram sufficient-statistic draws at each of `(n,p)=(10^6,1000)` and `(10^9,5000)`, a=0.3, one true coefficient 1, continuing seed `1092026`. I used exact chi-square draws for residual noise and the stable `log1p` score gain for the specified top-ceil(log p)/minimum witness. There were respectively 39 and 42 positive gains. These finite-sample counts neither prove the asymptotic proposition nor contradict its slow extreme-value convergence; they confirm that the witness can genuinely increase the shipped score. They are not full-lattice scans, and no “NA holds” conclusion is drawn from a negative witness gain.
