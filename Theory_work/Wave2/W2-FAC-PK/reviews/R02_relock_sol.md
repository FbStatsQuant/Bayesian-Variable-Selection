Required changes

1. **Separate P4's unrestricted statistical cap theorem from its bounded-cap delivery theorem.** STATEMENT.md:70–78 quantifies over every integer-valued cap rule satisfying only a lower bound, then supplies an allocation charged for caps at most ℓ_n + 2K. That allocation does not cover the quantified class. For example, a(data) = 2^n + ℓ_n + 2K satisfies the lower bound with δ = 1, but an archive/preflight charge proportional to a + 1 cannot be covered by the displayed polynomial allocation. Early certification of the realised search does not repair a preflight charge based on the cap's worst case. The same mismatch appears explicitly in lemmas/L4_assembly.md §L4.2: the proof uses a + 1 ≤ ℓ_n + 2K + 1, although the theorem assumes no such upper bound.

   Keep (P4)(i)–(ii) for the general lower-bounded class. For (iii), add the explicit, deterministic requirement a(data) ≤ ℓ_n + 2K on every admissible input to which its delivery allocation applies, or give envelopes and allocation based on a declared deterministic upper envelope A_max(n,p). A high-probability upper bound is insufficient for the claimed no-output identity. In the list of examples, ℓ_n + ⌈(1 + δ)|S̃₀|⌉ is covered by the displayed delivery envelope when 0 < δ ≤ 1; arbitrary fixed δ > 1 needs a different envelope. The other two listed examples fit the envelope in the stated exact-Gram range (the p-based example eventually under its statistical hypotheses). This is an allocation/quantifier defect, not a refutation of asymptotic discovery for large caps.

2. **Restrict P5's realised-work equality to caps that do not decrease MR1, or qualify it asymptotically.** STATEMENT.md:83 says that realised LS0 work is identical wherever MR1 ends certified. Lemma CAP supports this for an enlargement of MR1, not every cap admitted by (P4)'s lower bound or (P5)'s upper bound.

   There is already a concrete counterexample in numerics/N1_raw.jsonl, its first row: n = 600, p = 360, seed = 0, |S̃₀| = 244, ℓ_n = 10, m* = 248, first scoring index k_hit = 247. MR1 has cap 254 and ends certified. Choose the single fixed rule a = max(ℓ_n, ⌈1.001|S̃₀|⌉). It satisfies (P4)'s condition with δ = 0.001, and a ≤ ℓ_n + 2K, but on this row a = 245: it truncates, scans fewer centres, and does not even score S₀. Thus the asserted exact work identity is false for the stated cap class. This tests a deterministic identity, not a missing statistical guarantee at a particular n.

   State the equality for a ≥ ℓ_n + |S̃₀|, which includes the three named candidate rules, or state the weaker high-probability equality on the saturation event for the broader class once δK ≥ ℓ_n. Make the same restriction wherever METHOD_REQUIREMENTS.md or L4 asserts the identity. Their general transfer claim also needs care: CAP transfers results from the legacy ℓ_n cap for a ≥ ℓ_n, but it does not establish containment of MR1's scored set unless a ≥ MR1.

3. **Flag the positive theorem's bounded-gap frontier in the scope paragraph and open deliverables.** STATEMENT.md:17–21 describes the exact-Gram stage as K < p ≤ n − 1 and flags only the stronger restriction for NEG. But (P1) and (P3)–(P4) assume p ≥ K + 16s₀ + 1. The asymptotic sequences K < p < K + 16s₀ + 1 are therefore also unresolved by these statements, not merely unresolved for NEG. Add them explicitly to §0 and §5, or supply a separate theorem covering them. This includes sequences such as p_n = K_n + 1 for all n; it is an asymptotic scope issue.

   Saturation cannot simply be extended to that entire missing range. For example, take one fixed nonzero truth in a size-16 block with 15 nulls, all other blocks truth-free, and p = K + 1. Once the truth is included, those 15 same-block nulls have zero population partial covariance; including them loses order log n per null, whereas all truth-free-block variables have nonzero factor leakage and gain order n. FACB L2's positive/zero-pair argument gives the unconstrained maximiser S* = [p] minus those 15 nulls with probability tending to one. Its size p − 15 = K − 14 is feasible, so it remains the capped maximiser and is not saturated. The required block layout is permitted by the deterministic-partition setting of (P1). This does not contradict (P1) under (PK′); it explains why its omitted restriction matters.
