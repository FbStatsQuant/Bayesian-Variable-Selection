Optional suggestions / proof gates

- **NA-R: prove a bound adequate for c_n, rather than the stronger numerical heuristic.** Write τ²_{T,l} = Var(e_{T,l}) ≥ c and g = Ce_{T,l}. Gaussian regression gives e_{T,l} independent of W_T. Uniform relative χ² concentration over at most p2^q residuals and a conditional χ² bound on P_Te_{T,l} give sample normalisation and projection errors tending to zero at polylogarithmic rates. Since T ⊆ Q, control every P_Tε by P_Qε. In particular the projection contribution is O_P((q + log n)/√n), and normalisation errors times a O_P(√log n) noise supremum also vanish. This is the essential sample-versus-population step; a population variance inequality alone does not prove a statement about Z.

- **NA-R: a frame is a subspace, not one direction.** Whiten each frame on its population support, allowing singular frames by reducing to their rank. Uniform sample covariance concentration over N_n frames of rank at most m₀ then controls all linear combinations. Conditional Gaussian projection tails yield a frame noise bound of size √(2 log N_n) + o_P(√log n), with a fixed-dimensional term absorbed into the remainder. R3 and the residual floor transfer that bound to residual units. A union bound over one arbitrarily chosen vector per frame would be invalid.

- **R2 does not actually bound atom cardinality by a polylogarithm.** It permits enormous atom families when L_n is tiny. Do not silently use log|𝒜_n| = o(n) or uniform sample atom-norm concentration. There is a way to retain R2 as written: conditional on ε, each atom statistic (Ca)ᵀε is Gaussian with variance at most ‖Cε‖², because the entire Gaussian extension is independent of ε. A Gaussian union bound, choosing an additional diverging tail parameter slowly enough that L_n² times it is o(log n), gives an atom contribution o_P(√log n). No independence between atoms is required. Together with the frame argument this gives

  max_{T,l}|Z_{T,l}| ≤ √(2 log p) + o_P(√log n),

  which suffices since p ≤ n^A and c_n − 2 log p = log n − O(log log n). It does **not** follow from general R1–R3 that the remainder is O_P(√log|Q|), as the plan's motivating heuristic suggests. D1's window count itself has log(N_n/p) = O((log log n)^d), so keep the general and application-specific rates distinct.

- **D1 is plausible on minimum-separated sites, without a regular grid.** Packing gives uniform polynomial ball/shell counts. Use a radial distance weight in the Combes–Thomas conjugation and a summable exponential shell bound, uniformly over inherited subsets. Apply the resulting inverse decay to obtain conditional decay and regression coefficient decay; explicitly bound the regression convolution and its ℓ¹ sum. For the NA window r_n of order log log n, log(N_n/p) = O(r_n^d) = o(log n). The additional explained variance from variables beyond the window is bounded by a polynomial in r_n times an exponentially decaying factor, giving uniform R3. These are new proofs, not a literal substitution into one-dimensional CT.

- **D2 has a concrete viable NA-R representation and automatic PTV.** Take independent standard Gaussian factors/idiosyncratic coordinates, ψ_v = 1 − b_v² and W_v = b_vF_G + √ψ_v ξ_v. For H = Σ_{v∈T∩G}b_v²/ψ_v,

  β_v = b_l b_v/[ψ_v(1 + H)],
  r_{T,l} = (b_l − Σ_v β_v b_v)F_G + √ψ_l ξ_l.

  The remaining atoms are ξ_v for v ∈ Q in one-factor groups, with coefficients β_v√ψ_v. Their ℓ¹ norm is bounded uniformly: Σ_v|β_v| ≤ b̄/b_lo. They are independent of r_{T,l}, so Var(r_{T,l}) ≤ Var(e_{T,l}); η_n = 0 works. Bounded groups use the entire group as a frame of fixed dimension, with no atom correction. Independent groups imply PTV with a₀ = θ_lo min(c, 1 − b̄²), even for conditioning sets mixing groups. Thus a separate PTV assumption is unnecessary for D2. The covariance-group partition must remain deterministic; the implementation's partition may be any X-measurable partition of size at most 16 and need not respect the covariance groups.

- **The sandwich can survive polylogarithmic Q, but preserve the global size ladder.** For D1, neighbourhood counts in selection removal become O((log n)^d), hence the size-at-most-15 reference-set count remains exp(O(log log n)). For D2 use G(l) ∪ Q⁰, also polylogarithmic. The joint spherical count bound stays k n^{-1/2+o(1)}; the ladder still excludes near-cap maximisers, and its final deletion step uses log k = O(log log n). A proof that starts by assuming the mode already has polylogarithmic size would be circular. Full-lattice TL has signal of order n and polylogarithmic contrast errors, so it is not the apparent bottleneck.
