**(d) LT: correct inequality; incorrect claimed shortcut to all coded summaries.** Conditional on data and a valid frozen calibration, put W = Ẑ_r/Z and let A be the mean fraction at S₀ over the four measured endpoint populations. N1 gives E[W(1−A)] = δ. For any ε,η > 0,

\[
P(1-A\ge\varepsilon\mid X,Y,\mathcal C)
\le \delta/(\varepsilon\eta)+P(W<\eta\mid X,Y,\mathcal C).
\]

The displayed LT in the critique is exactly ε = 1/2. With η_n = n^(−1/2) log n, δ/η_n = O_P(1/√log n); if the lower-tail term vanishes in joint probability, the bounded conditional probability bound integrates to zero. This does **not** require an expectation rate inferred from O_P. Taking every fixed ε gives A → 1, which is stronger than merely A > 1/2. Plain PIPs then converge uniformly, and their MPM is correct; discovery plus posterior uniqueness gives the ledger HPM. For the four shipped islands, require the premise islandwise; a lower bound for their averaged evidence alone is insufficient for equal-weight PIPs. Uniform claims over growing R′ need additional uniform control.

The RB conclusion does not follow from endpoint A: even if every endpoint is S₀, its recorded Gibbs conditional probabilities need not be 0 or 1, and intermediate states may differ. N1 as checked is an endpoint identity. An augmented-path/RB identity is plausible from elementary-kernel invariance, but simultaneous control of p recorded coordinates needs a separate argument. Coordinatewise weighted bounds followed by a union bound can cost pδ/η, unacceptable for arbitrary A. Thus “RB PIPs are within 1−A” is false as stated. Likewise, evidence non-collapse alone does not imply Ẑ/Z → 1: a mean-one random variable can remain nondegenerate while bounded away from zero.

Two further corrections: the claimed one-step equivalence to N_eπ₀(S₀) → ∞ needs restrictions. With N_e = 1 and π₀(S₀) → 1, non-collapse already holds although N_eπ₀(S₀) → 1.
