## Findings

1. **BLOCKER — The coded posterior needs its raw-unit and input-domain conventions.** Definitions §§1, 4 and 4.3 specify a standardized slab and then use its symbols directly in the original-unit conditional law and residual projection. For coded draw `(b, v)`, set `β_j = s_y b_j/s_{x_j}` and `σ² = s_y² v` before using `ȳ + (x−x̄)ᵀβ`. Define the score's signal vector using the raw centered design with raw coefficients, or the standardized design with the corresponding sample-scaled coefficients in raw response units. Restore the manuscript's removed-column labels and count undefined posteriors in the TD/TS-a exceptional event. In particular, TM-a0 currently admits one candidate, while the staged distribution setting declares fewer than two retained predictors undefined. State `p_C ≥ 2` for the coded TM-a0 or explicitly define a mathematical one-candidate extension. Under A-MOM, each of finitely many nonconstant columns and the nonconstant response eventually have strictly positive sample variance a.s. Use the fixed, eventually untruncated BB(1, p_C) prior or require fixed positive model masses if generalizing. Positivity of arbitrary n-dependent masses permits exponential prior changes that defeat the sketch. [Staged manuscript setting](/home/fb31/BVS-wt/THEORY-DEPT/.rc_tmp/song/ms/or6_theorems.tex:116)

They also leave the exact conclusion and inheritance of TS-b's rate implicit. Give M1's `max{Pr_n(d_B,m_B), ϖ_n}` and growing `ϖ_n`, M2's `R_n(B∪N) ≥ (1−ϑ_n)R_n(B)` with quantified B, N and bounds on `ϑ_n`, and a cutoff tied to the actual sieve. State the claimed rate or consistency separately and mark the unclosed argument. Alternatively, present MARGIN* solely as an open refinement and remove it from the list of defined theorems. [Fable's MARGIN* formulation](/home/fb31/BVS-wt/THEORY-DEPT/Theory_work/Dept/song_assumptions/advisors/FABLE_R1.md:72)

It suffices to impose the residual margin on feasible `|S|≤min(K,floor(s+C_sM))`, with the SUBG window covering `S∪S0`. The omitted-model tail adds to the contraction tail. 8. **SUBSTANTIVE — Lock fixed M and the exact-odds comparison for TS-b.** Definitions §4.2 and Paths §§2 and 3.3. Fix an admissible M independently of n, take J integer, omit empty classes and quantify the random-width condition on a design event tending to one (or use a deterministic upper bound). In the mixed comparison against U, write `Q_U−RSS_U=O_P(σ²)` uniformly from the Gram floor, since the in-span shrinkage eigenvalues are O(1/n) and the centered response energy is `O_P(nσ²)`. The lower comparison uses `Q_S≥RSS_S`; no uniform upper ridge bound on arbitrary underfits is needed there. For fixed m and growing p, truncate `−(n/2)log(Beta)` at `c_m log p` with large enough c_m. The beta density gives an integrated truncated exponential moment `O((log p)^{m/2})` and a union tail over the `C(p−s0,m)` subsets. Multiply by `O(p^(−m)n^(−m/2))` and use tightness directly at bounded p. Simultaneous control over fixed J then follows from a finite union and sum. State the additional obligations if M or J is allowed to grow.
