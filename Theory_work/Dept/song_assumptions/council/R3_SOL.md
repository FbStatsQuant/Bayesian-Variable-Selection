### Step 3 verification

In noise units let A_U = (RSS_U + 2b₀s_y²)/σ² and Δ_U = (Q_U − RSS_U)/σ². The projected signal direction gives RSS_S − RSS_U ≥ σ²W on the concentration event. The log likelihood loss against U is bounded below by
`(n/2+a₀)[log(1+W/A_U) − log(1+Δ_U/A_U)]`.
Here Δ_U is uniformly tight, A_U is uniformly of order n on the truncation event (directly by the finite set of RSS ratios at bounded p), and A_U ≤ V_n. Summing the containing-model odds yields the stated bound. For B_Dm ≤ 0, nested RSS alone permits W = 0. For B_Dm > 0, evaluating the monotone log at 2(1+δ)B_Dm gives (1+δ)B_Dm(1+o_P(1)) ≥ B_Dm with high probability. This establishes the accounting without linearising at the actual residual.
