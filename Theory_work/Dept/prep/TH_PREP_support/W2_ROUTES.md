## 7. What published routes cover the common alternatives (one paragraph per element)

All paragraphs separate "what the repo says" (cited) from "reader inference" (marked I). Literature statements marked "lit." come from the repo's own summaries, not from the papers themselves (PDFs not in repo, per `prior_probe/REPORT.md:58`).

**E1 (slab / g).** *g = n plus ridge (coded).* R1-R3, R5 use the Occam factor (n+1)^{-1/2} directly (X.md:126, M_misr.md:72, Q1_sandF_c.md:83,93). *g = p^2 or g = max(n, p^2) (Fernandez-Ley-Steel type; as in YWJ 2016 g ~ p^{2 alpha}):* the repo maps YWJ's g-prior into the coded setting at `W3P/ASSUMPTION_MAP.md:89-131` (Q) and records that "YWJ16's faster O(p^-1) comes with a larger per-variable penalty ... i.e. a different prior" (`:129`, Q). I: the Occam factor becomes (g+1)^{-1/2} ~ p^{-1}; every place the displays contain (n+1), replace by (g+1); with p <= n^A, log g = 2A log n so beta-min constants change only by a factor of A (constants), but the rate r_n would improve to the superset term of order (g+1)^{-1/2}sqrt(log n) (UNVERIFIED), and Prop LB becomes 1/(1+sqrt(g+1)). Fernandez-Ley-Steel (2001) itself: NOT FOUND in the repo (grep over Docs, Theory_work Wave1C/Wave3, no hit). Narisetty-He 2014: nu tau_1^2 ~ n v p^{2+3 delta} (`W3P/ASSUMPTION_MAP.md:71-75`, Q) is the same order as g = max(n, p^2); their Theorem 4.1 gives P(Z = t | Y, sigma^2) -> 1 under log p = o(n) with known sigma^2 (Q at `:~84`). *V = I or 9I (data-free slab):* NOT FOUND as a worked route; `ROUTES.md:17-28` Q says Song-Liang's Jiang-style route would apply to a data-free V_xi, but Theorems 1-2 are not implied for the coded prior because V = n(G+I)^{-1} is data-dependent. I: the closed form D_S = prod(1 + 9 lambda_i) loses the bounded-above property D_S <= (n+1)^{|S|} (`X.md:126`), costing log-K factors.

**E2 (BB(1,p)).** *BB(1,1):* I: the cancellation of the C(p-s0,j) multiplicity still holds exactly (it is a property of any w(k) ~ 1/C(p,k): C(p-s0,j) w(s0+j)/w(s0) = C(s0+j, s0) <= (j+1)^{s0}), but the 2^{-j} factor and the geometric size tail vanish (size marginal uniform), so T02(i)/Lemma S (`X.md:140-142`, `T03R:13-123`) must be replaced by an Occam-only dimension bound; the repo says that route is available only under (ND) (`ROUTES.md:30-31`, Q). *BB(1,p^u), u > 0, and Castillo-van der Vaart complexity priors pi(s) ~ c^{-s} p^{-a s}:* `prior_probe/REPORT.md:56-58` (Q): BB(1,p) has ratio -> 1/2, "the u = 1 boundary of their Beta(1,p^u), u > 1 example", so it fails the complexity-prior condition A1 p^{-A3} pi(s-1) <= pi(s) <= A2 p^{-A4} pi(s-1) with A4 = 0. I: for u > 1 (A4 = u-1 > 0) the sieve route of Jiang/Song-Liang (R2) and the Castillo-Schmidt-Hieber-van der Vaart 2015 route (R1, beta-min precedents `supplement.tex:59-62`, Q) become available; the per-variable penalty log(2p) -> u log p changes (2p)^{s0} to p^{u s0}, a constants-only change for the proofs of R1 (the beta-min constant s0(A+1) log n becomes s0(uA + 1) log n; I). YWJ 2016's prior p^{-kappa|gamma|}, kappa + alpha >= 2, is the same family; the repo notes the coded prior's per-variable cost is log(2p) + 1/2 log n versus (kappa+alpha) log p (`W3P/ASSUMPTION_MAP.md:~125-131`, Q).

**E3 (cap floor(n/2)).** No source uses the cap as a residual-degrees-of-freedom bound (NF); it enters as S0 in Omega, end of the tail sum, the W_K normaliser in [3/4,1] (`prior_probe/REPORT.md:44-45`, Q), kernel invariance (`v3_engine.h:116`) and R5's (L-feas). YWJ 2016 Theorem 1 uses no truncation (D(s0) with s0 := p, `W3P/ASSUMPTION_MAP.md:~100`, Q) and Theorem 2 (mixing) uses the cap. Narisetty-He: m_n(nu) = p_n and n/((2+nu) log p_n). I: dropping the cap (K = p) would require p <= n-1 for the closed-form posterior to be proper, since H_S, A_S are invertible regardless (ridge); the sources do not test this (NF).

**E4 (IG(0.01,0.01)).** Constants only in every proof (c_IG, kappa, c_E, kappa_E); structural only in R3 where b = b0 s_y^2 makes Lemma R' possible. Any fixed IG(a0,b0) with a0, b0 > 0 keeps all steps (I). Narisetty-He Theorem 4.2 (IG-prior version) was "not re-read" by the repo (`W3P/ASSUMPTION_MAP.md:~84`, Q).

**E5 (centring/scaling).** R3's reduction is exactly the statement that the coded posterior depends on y^r only through U'y^r up to scale (`R_reduction.md:24-27`, Q). Divisor n versus n-1: constants (`M_misr.md:110`, Q). An alternative scaling of X (e.g. unit L2 norm per column) rescales G_jj from N to 1 and the slab n(G+I)^{-1} would be unnormalised; I: this changes E1 effectively (g would need the matching factor).

**E6 (tempering/MCMC/islands).** Only R4 and R5(delivery) depend on it. Published routes: Del Moral 2004 for unbiasedness (Chopin 2004 NOT FOUND) (`MAN/s4_results.tex:427`, Q); Beskos et al. 2016 for stability/adaptive tempering: NOT FOUND in the repo's proof files (the repo uses its own pathwise bounds, `SoP:209-212`, Q). Song-Liang 2015 Thm 4/Cor 2: cited at `SoP:243` (A) as the target the M2 package is positioned against.

**E7 (MPM vs HPM).** Purely deterministic given delta = 1 - pi(S0) (`M_misr.md:38-43`, Q; lem:summaries `s3_method.tex:58-63`). YWJ 2016 states posterior mass of gamma* (HPM-type); Narisetty-He states P(Z = t | Y) -> 1 (same); no published route needed.
