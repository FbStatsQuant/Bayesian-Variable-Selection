## HEADLINE (final)
- No paper scores 4 or 5. Nobody combines (SMC over the model space) + (tempering path) + (theory on selection/posterior consistency or on the algorithm's reported output). About 32 candidates screened, ~45 searches plus ~20 citation chains.
- Closest methodological relatives (score 3): Schafer-Chopin 2013 (SMC on {0,1}^p, BVS, tempered, adaptive binary proposals), Shi-Dunson 2011 (particle stochastic search), Ma 2015 (LIPS, SMC over models, no tempering), Youssfi-Chopin 2024 (tempering waste-free SMC, bi-level BVS in GLMs), Vuong 2024 thesis (annealed SMC), van Assen-Hinne 2026 (anchored RJ-SMC), Fan-Pan-McCormick 2026 (AIS with a non-prior start law seeded from high-evidence models, the nearest analogue of our block-exact pi_0), Schafer 2012 thesis (not retrieved).
- Theory: only generic SMC theory (CLT, Feynman-Kac stability, finite-sample / local-mixing complexity: Schweizer, Marion-Mathews-Schmidler, Paulin-Jasra-Thiery, Lee et al., Dau-Chopin, Le Fay et al., Syed et al.) and MCMC-mixing theory for BVS (Yang et al. 2016, Zhou et al. 2022). A single algorithm-level PIP-error bound exists for a variational (non-SMC) BVS method (Mai-Quiroz-Tran 2026). No theorem about consistency of an SMC-BVS output.
- Not found anywhere: SMC with a block-exact / non-prior start law for BVS at p up to 10^6; frozen-calibration unbiased evidence in BVS SMC; Rao-Blackwellised or list-renormalised PIPs in an SMC; certified hill-climbed reported model.
- Correction to the brief: the arXiv version of Dau-Chopin has no variable-selection example.

## 4. Negative findings and caveats

1. No paper scores 4 or 5. I found NO work that proves posterior or selection consistency, or any guarantee about the reported model, for an SMC / particle sampler over the model space. The only BVS-specific theory near SMC is Mai-Quiroz-Tran (#31, variational, not SMC) and MCMC mixing results (Yang et al. 2016, Zhou et al. 2022).
2. The three scale-relevant SMC-over-inclusion-vector papers are Schafer-Chopin 2013 (conjugate g-type prior, tempering on likelihood, adaptive binary proposals, p up to a few hundred in examples), Youssfi-Chopin 2024 (GLM, waste-free tempering SMC, p up to a few thousand) and Ma 2015 (LIPS, no tempering). None goes near p = 10^6 and none uses a block-exact start law or a frozen calibration.
3. Nobody I found reports a Rao-Blackwellised PIP, a certified hill-climbed reported model or a best-list renormalised PIP in an SMC. The OPAD paper (#19) and shotgun stochastic search (section 2) are the nearest on "list of models with renormalised weights".
4. Dau-Chopin 2022 (the arXiv text) contains no BVS example (contrary to the task brief). The BVS use of waste-free SMC is Youssfi-Chopin.
5. Not obtained: Schafer's PhD thesis (host did not resolve); Shi-Dunson full text (only abstract/HTML page seen); Jasra et al. 2008, Fouskakis et al. 2009, Karagiannis-Andrieu 2013 (paywalled; only abstracts and snippets); Duan 2019 (the saved file is an XML error page); Satpathy-Shah, Weisang, Wang et al.
