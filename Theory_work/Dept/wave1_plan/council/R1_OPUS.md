## Open items (LS §5)
1. **Anchored vs all-subsets A7′(a).** Keep the anchored form as the assumption. It is exactly what (F_λ) consumes
   ([W3P] l.68–70, [T03R] SE_λ(j₀)), it is strictly weaker (Astra's example checks out), and it still excludes
   duplicated columns by interlacing. Lead with one plain sentence that is faithful to it: "the true predictors together
   with any (2A+1)s₀+2 others are not nearly collinear". Give the all-subsets form on (2A+2)s+2 predictors as the
   checkable sufficient condition, as [LS] already does. Fable's readability point is met by that sentence; it does not
   require changing the assumption.
2. **Non-nesting of Cor S1 with old Thm 3.2.** Acceptable, but the note's stated reason is wrong (OPUS-4). The true
   reason is that A4–A5 are assumed for every fixed c, whereas the old fixed floor gives A5 only up to
   c = 5(A+1)(s₀+1). Recommend the corrected note plus a supplement remark: in the per-n form (single window c_S =
   C_q + 1), the old hypotheses imply the new ones for every (s₀, A) except s₀ = A = 1. So the generalisation can be
   claimed honestly there at no cost.
3. **C_sel in the main text or only the supplement.** Main text: "for every fixed C_β larger than a constant
   C_sel(s, A), given explicitly in the supplement and not sharp". Supplement: the symbolic form 4√c₀, the definition
   of c₀ and C_*, and the numbers 111.4 (uniform in p) and 49.8 (p = n², asymptotic), in the units of A7′(b). This is
   the draft's proposal, and I agree. A symbolic 4√c₀ in the main text would force C_* (a union-bound artefact) into
   the main text without adding information.
