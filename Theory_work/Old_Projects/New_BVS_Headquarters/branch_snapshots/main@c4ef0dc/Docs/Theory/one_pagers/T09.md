- (per LOCKED_PROOFS.md, all parts (i)-(v)) The exact start sampler (backward sampler of the composite start) draws from the start law pi0 with the right normaliser Z-bar0, and successive draws are iid. Every elementary move (Gibbs flips, uniform and neighbour swaps, independence proposal) and the randomly ordered round leave the bridge distribution q_phi invariant. The round has a whole-round minorisation constant eps, and a tiny eps alone does not imply slow mixing.
