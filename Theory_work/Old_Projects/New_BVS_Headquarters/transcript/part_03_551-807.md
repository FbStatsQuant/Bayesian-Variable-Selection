## How the original draft maps onto today's theory (F-DRAFT, Fable)
**The draft's design is exactly what v3 does.** The "product of block posteriors" is v3's start distribution, and the "correction" is its tempering bridge.

| Draft goal | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Prop 1, MH exactness | **Already proved** (T09, locked) |
| Prop 2, SMC consistency | **Already covered** by T09 + T12 + T31(i), proved and locked. Consistency as n grows is W2-T26's result; the generic Del Moral et al. theorem is cited, not reproved. |
| **§5.3, weight stability** | **The one real gap, and the draft's mechanism is wrong for v3.** See below. |
| §3.2, naive merge fails | **New proposition, ready for the main text.** Local PIPs for a pair of correlated predictors both go to 1, the merged start puts mass on the pair, but the posterior picks the truth. Every uncertainty measure is wrong, yet the truth is one Gibbs flip away. It doesn't follow from T06, T07 or T27. |
| §13.3, partition as a random variable | Not needed. Validity doesn't depend on the partition, so the partition is a design choice of the start, not a parameter to infer. A mixture of block starts can be sampled exactly and undoes masking at a logarithmic cost. |

**On weight stability:** the discrepancy between the start and the posterior is **not** driven by cross-block correlation, as the draft guessed. It is driven by each block table using its own estimate of σ², a "dilution" term that grows linearly in n. With known σ² and orthogonal blocks, the start equals the posterior exactly. Theorem W gives the rate, and shows that one-shot importance correction fails for p ≫ n even from a favourable start. **That justifies the tempering bridge.**

## Novelty against the literature (A-DRAFT, Astra)
**The novelty risk the draft flagged is real.** The general local-to-global idea already exists in Lindsten et al. (2017), and Zanella & Roberts (2019) and Schäfer & Chopin (2013) cover the BVS-specific SMC machinery. **The paper's defensible contributions are narrower:**
1. the specific block start, which can be sampled exactly under a global size prior and cap;
2. a verified design family where the start misses the truth but the method still finds it within budget, which is T38's masked regime;
3. quantitative control of weights and estimators.

**What follows:** pitch the paper as "when and why a scored-HPM SMC succeeds", not as "local-to-global correction". That is how the current theorem set is already framed. Three of the six key references aren't in `Papers/`; Astra checked them from their primary sources online.

## A possible method change (your call, under the investment rule)
F-DRAFT flags that **building every block table with one shared plug-in σ²** would make the draft's §5.3 theorem literally true. It would keep the locked finite-sample results unchanged, and it attacks the n = 200 dilution masking at its source. It needs a pilot before any decision.
