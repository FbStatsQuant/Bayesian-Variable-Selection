### The problem
Bayesian inference over 2^p models has to scale in p, and sequential MCMC can't get there by adding hardware. A chain's cost is its mixing time, and that work is inherently serial. Running a thousand chains doesn't shorten burn-in; each chain still has to mix on its own. So past some p, a better MCMC isn't enough. The algorithm has to be organised differently.

### Why split-and-merge isn't the answer
Song–Liang is parallel, which is the right instinct. But their merge is a heuristic: it is valid only when the blocks are close to independent, and the split then becomes part of the answer. Correlation is exactly what breaks it, and at large p correlation is the norm rather than the exception (genomics, for instance). So the parallel methods are fast but fail precisely where large p takes you.

### Why SMC
SMC is a population method whose correction step is exact. The importance weights and invariant move kernels keep it targeting the true posterior, however bad the starting point. In our design:
- **Local step:** exact enumeration of correlation-clustered blocks, all in parallel.
- **Merge:** the product start π₀. This is Song–Liang's merge, but it is used only as a starting point, never as the answer.
- **Correction:** the tempering bridge repairs the cross-block dependence the merge ignored.
- **Width:** work is parallel across particles, and across independent islands whose unbiased Ẑ lets them be combined exactly.

### Thesis sentence
**In split-and-merge, correlation breaks correctness. In local-to-global SMC, correlation costs only sequential depth and particles, never correctness.**

O-STAGES already quantifies the depth cost: about log₂A₀ stages when cross-block correlation masks the start, O(1) when it doesn't. That is the number to compare against MCMC's mixing time. ## Song–Liang (2015)

**What they do.** They split the predictors into K random groups and run sampler-based BVS on each group in parallel. Each group keeps the variables whose inclusion probability passes an FDR cut, with two passes. They take the union of the survivors and run BVS once more on that union.
