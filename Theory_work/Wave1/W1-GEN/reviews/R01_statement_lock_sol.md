## Required changes

1. **Make H0 and the empty-family conventions explicit.** Give j0, the prior-tail definition of m_n^sharp, and the normalized versus unnormalized eigenvalue requirements, rather than only their names. Define H1b's maximum as zero when V=[p]; omit the H2c average when p=s*, and state the zero-denominator convention for E and the fixed epsilon_0>0 used in h_n. Restrict residual directions to the families where SE ensures their existence: the current blanket definition for every T,l is invalid when p>n. Sources: GEN/STATEMENT.md:30-32,44-64; SoP/04_theory.tex:775-778; T22iii/STATEMENT.md:59-73. H0 is faithful by reference, but not yet self-contained/checkable as advertised.

2. **Replace the claimed canonical reference construction.** Minimizing Lambda_U exactly is not a valid way to obtain a reference satisfying VIS plus small leakage. Concrete counter-instance: W's block containing truth j, another truth k outside it, and a null l in j's block. With rho=1/4, cross correlation epsilon_n>0, and equal signal theta, the partial covariance of x_l with mu given x_j is theta*epsilon_n*(1-rho), nonzero. Thus Lambda decreases strictly beyond {j}; an exact minimizer includes nulls, although their incremental gain is of order n*theta^2*epsilon_n^2, far below log n. Removing such an included null violates VIS. A valid reference {j} nevertheless exists. State an existential, population/oracle certificate, or give a thresholded construction and prove it works; these conditions depend on beta, not on X alone or observed data alone. Sources: GEN/STATEMENT.md:55-68,92; SoP/04_theory.tex:77; T22iii/STATEMENT.md:45-49. Define the order denoted by prec if retained.

3. **State the precise generalized T22 lemma, rather than importing its old support interpretation.** C1 can use a feasible reference S* of uniformly bounded size, without mu belonging to span(X_S*). The residual-control proof uses the actual mu and bounded energy; the leakage integral allows nonzero delta; completion uses VIS, not true-support membership. The only substantive extension is NL_C plus NL_avg in place of NL_eta: NL_C gives max x=O_P(log n/n), and NL_avg supplies the averaged integral bound. Record these substitutions and keep the true S0 in G/R. Sources: GEN/STATEMENT.md:72,114-115; T22iii/PROOF_v2.tex:149-244,249-329,338-401,429-468; Docs/Theory/proofs/T22_T16_start_aggregation/STATEMENT.md:21-38. This reuse is plausible, not an already locked theorem about arbitrary references.

4. **Strengthen the L3 proof obligation to cover swaps and the full BM-infinity range.** The right deterministic condition is TD'_infinity on 2^V, not progress dominance. At every state missing a truth, compare a best truth addition against (a) outside addition at S and (b) outside swap S-i+l, using SEP at S-i and Lambda_(S-i)>=Lambda_S. At supersets, prove uniformly that outside null additions are unfavorable at every relevant S and S-i; the swap identity then makes an extra deletion beat an outside replacement. Also prove truth-removing moves unfavorable at S0. These are not consequences of Lemma 3 alone. Crucially, E5's absolute O(n^(2/3)) RSS error and U3's fixed positive population margin do not handle allowed signals theta_min^2=(log n)^2/n. Add a contrast-level Gaussian bound with error O_P(sqrt(gain*log n)+log n), or equivalent, before claiming the whole BM-infinity regime. Sources: GEN/PLAN.md:7-9,18-26,45-46; BS/W1-T37R/PROOF.md:71-88,118-128,132-154,203-240; BS/API-PXNGAP/PROOF.md:172-174,299-305. No margin between progress and other moves *inside* V is needed, but an improving internal move must beat every external move.

5. **Repair the cap and operational quantifiers.** Define tau(C) for the uncapped greedy path, then state the capped scoring/certification implications; as written, a production run cannot unconditionally reach S0 in tau moves if its cap is smaller. State eventual |V|<=K and ell_n>=2^vbar-1 explicitly in the assembly; the displayed C3 bound applies for sufficiently large n. Distinguish path centres staying inside V from scanned/scored neighbors: complete scans certainly evaluate models outside V. C3-fav needs only H0, the centre-law assumptions, and operational premises; remove the dangling need for a descent event, and use D_n<=P(S_tilde0!=S0). For R'>4 retain the extra search from c4 premise as well as IND-T. Sources: GEN/STATEMENT.md:36-40,75-87; SoP/02_method.tex:463-467,490-506; SoP/04_theory.tex:475,484; BS/API-PXNGAP/PROOF.md:372-391. The emitted-centre tie rule and complete-scan/scoring semantics are otherwise correctly identified; N3 remains OPEN.

Require deterministic envelopes, uniform on A_n, for BM-infinity, VIS, SEP=o(n*theta_min^2), and NL_avg, or explicitly require the conditional conclusions for every deterministic sequence x_n in A_n and prove the diagonal argument. V and S* must be measurable functions of X (and fixed model parameters), never epsilon/y; beta may be X-measurable if that is the intended conditional model. ## Optional suggestions

- Keep the core theorem and favorable corollary separate from instance classification and C6, so auxiliary failures do not block a useful general result. Candidate proxy construction for C6: x_i=r*x_0+sqrt(1-r^2)*z_i with orthogonal z_i and d_n about (log n)^2; check the actual clustering and all retained hypotheses before using it.
- Add a tiny deterministic test reproducing the look-alike detour in BS/W1-T37R/PROOF.md:238-240. It illustrates why the new finite-region argument can succeed although progress dominance fails.
- Cost is reasonable for the core: bounded-size score expansions, O(2^vbar*vbar*p) neighbor comparisons, and finite-state descent. Constants are exponential in vbar; for 14 signal groups this is a mathematical sufficient condition with a potentially enormous onset, not a useful finite-budget certificate. Enumerate only p<=10 and use targeted/analytic checks for larger relevance sets, consistent with Theory_work/Wave1/PROTOCOL.md's compute limit.
- No prohibited dead route is being retried in the core: bounded-region closure addresses RF176's masked ties, LS0 avoids RF208's no-LS0 locking route, and strong SE excludes the cited barriers. Do not call every F-increasing path “Lambda-descent”: noise can select moves with no population residual decrease. Sources: GEN/PLAN.md:7-15; SoP/05_history.tex:223-239; BS/W1-T37R/PROOF.md:132-154. The main cost/risk is the new L3 contrast bound and uniform random-design instance checks, not deterministic search.
