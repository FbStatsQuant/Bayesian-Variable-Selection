# R10 — Independent exhaustive sweep (Sol)

Reviewed STATEMENT.md and all nine requested proof files, against the protocol, the State-of-Project method/theorem cards, T22(i) and the full T22(iii) proof, the cited LH/LH-iid/W source material, and the generator. I did not consult LOG.md or existing reviews. Locations below are relative to Theory_work/Wave1/W1-GEN unless a full repository path is given. Repeated occurrences of the same problem are grouped.

1. **Major — L8 omits the bounded-energy hypothesis it needs.** Location: `lemmas/L8_certificate.md:7`, `lemmas/L8_certificate.md:20`, `lemmas/L8_certificate.md:28`, `lemmas/L8_certificate.md:32`; also `STATEMENT.md:89`. The stated setting assumes (H2a), but neither that setting nor (H2a) supplies a constant M with ||mu||² <= Mn. Both certificate arguments use precisely this extra assumption to turn their coefficient thresholds into VIS, and M is otherwise undefined. This is substantive: in different blocks one can have a coefficient with squared signal gain of order log² n and another coefficient of order n. The displayed coefficient lower bound can hold with M treated as a fixed number and M_n of order log n, while the residual variance seen by the first block is of order n²; its VIS ratio then tends to zero. Within-block SE alone does not prevent this. **Fix:** explicitly assume ||mu||² <= Mn, with the same fixed M as in the thresholds, in the L8 setting and in (C1′). State that the resulting certificate still needs the separate feasibility, bounded-cardinality and relevance-set requirements before it can be used in GEN.

2. **Minor — The certificate proof uses a false finite-n variance ceiling.** Location: `lemmas/L8_certificate.md:20`, `lemmas/L8_certificate.md:28`, `lemmas/L8_certificate.md:35`, `lemmas/L8_certificate.md:41`; mirrored in `STATEMENT.md:91`. Even after adding the energy hypothesis, the available bound is v_U <= sigma² + Mn/(n-1), as line 28 correctly says, not v_U <= sigma² + M. Thus the last inequality at line 41 does not follow, and (BBM) does not deliver the named VIS envelope M_n literally. This does not destroy the existential VIS conclusion: a constant-factor reduction of M_n suffices. **Fix:** replace sigma²+M by sigma²+2M throughout the sufficient thresholds (n >= 2), or explicitly conclude VIS with the reduced deterministic envelope M_n(sigma²+M)/(sigma²+2M), and use that envelope consistently in (C1′).

3. **Minor — The W example is not a disjoint-support decomposition of its actual block regression.** Location: `lemmas/L8_certificate.md:32`, `lemmas/L8_certificate.md:56`; also `lemmas/L6_instances.md:101`. In a signal group, the other truth contributes theta*epsilon_n*Sigma_CC^{-1}1 to the block coefficients. For the nine-member equicorrelation block, Sigma_CC^{-1}1 = 1/3, so the coefficient on the active coordinate is theta + theta*epsilon_n/3. Taking gamma^L = theta e_j leaves a nonzero gamma^S on j itself, contrary to L8.2's disjoint-support requirement. **Fix:** take gamma^L = gamma_j e_j using the actual block coefficient, and gamma^S equal to the coefficients on the other coordinates. Its small-part energy remains O(n theta² epsilon_n²), and the large coefficient still satisfies the threshold.

4. **Minor — Empty coefficient supports have no stated minimum convention.** Location: `lemmas/L8_certificate.md:20`, `lemmas/L8_certificate.md:35`; also `STATEMENT.md:91`. The exact and thresholded conditions take minima over supp(gamma_b) or supp(gamma_b^L), including blocks on which these supports are empty. Such blocks occur in the very examples used to advertise the certificate (null and masked LH groups). The conditions are therefore undefined as written on those examples. **Fix:** impose the coefficient inequality only for blocks with a nonempty relevant support, and make (BBM) vacuous if the union of all supports is empty; equivalently state an explicit +infinity convention for empty minima. The local convention in L6(a):18 does not define the independent L8 lemmas.

5. **Minor — Lemma SEL needs an explicit zero-residual convention in its deterministic enlarged family.** Location: `lemmas/L6_instances.md:135`, `lemmas/L6_instances.md:140`, `lemmas/L6_instances.md:151`, `lemmas/L6_instances.md:157`. The deterministic family T0(l) includes sets containing all of S0 (s0=14 and the allowed size is 15). For those sets Lambda_T0=0 identically, so the partial correlation rho_T0,l is undefined and cannot satisfy rho²_T0,l = Z_T0,l with a Beta-distributed Z. Line 143 handles the selected leakage gain when this happens, but the later event K_n and the sum over the full deterministic family still use the undefined statistic. **Fix:** define Q_T0,l = delta²_T0,l/Lambda_T0 when Lambda_T0>0 and Q_T0,l=0 otherwise. Assert the Beta law only in the positive-residual case; in the other case Q=0 has the needed tail and moment bounds. Use Q throughout K_n and the exponential envelope. This keeps every event X-measurable and leaves the estimates unchanged.

6. **Minor — The study-generator proof silently specializes the locked statement's sparsity/layout.** Location: `STATEMENT.md:132`, `lemmas/L6_instances.md:107`, `lemmas/L6_instances.md:135`, `lemmas/L6_instances.md:171`, `lemmas/L6_instances.md:178`. It does not prove the full quantified layout as written in the statement. The cited R function itself defines truth only for p=30,500,1000, so an asymptotic extension must be stated explicitly rather than inferred from a literal call to that function. **Fix:** either lock (C5)(d) to s0=14 with one specified fixed layout (or explicitly either of the two layouts), for sufficiently large p containing those indices, or generalize the proof: use 2^{5s0+5}, establish the relevant pattern sum for the stipulated s0, and give the last-truth covariance argument for the stipulated spacing. Record the extension as a theoretical sequence, not a property of the finite-p function beyond its domain.

7. **Editorial — The AR(1) covariance lower bound has the wrong sign.** Location: `lemmas/L6_instances.md:178`, `lemmas/L6_instances.md:180`. The displayed q is negative for the specified fourteen-entry pattern: its last coefficient is -1 and the earlier contributions are too small to change the sign. Consequently “this covariance is >= |q| n^{-alpha/2}” is false; it is its absolute value that has this lower bound. **Fix:** replace “this covariance” by “the absolute covariance,” and retain the absolute-value bound through the sample-error comparison before squaring it.

8. **Minor — The claimed sharp LH move count is not established by the cited instance proof.** Location: `lemmas/L4_search.md:44`, pointing to `lemmas/L6_instances.md:28` and `lemmas/L6_instances.md:40`. They do not establish that the chosen best flip/swap never takes another improving state inside the signal units. This sharper route needs the stronger LH lattice/score comparison argument, not just the separated-relevance-set argument. **Fix:** remove the refinement, or cite and explicitly match the hypotheses/status of the LH-LAT/TD score theorem in `Theory_work/Old_Projects/New_BVS_Headquarters/branch_snapshots/W4-T38FULL/T38_PROOF.md:217` and its subsequent local-search assembly, with its high-probability qualification. In particular, the selected-to-T0 reduction in SEL is valid for the covered block-diagonal scenarios: the own-group truth is included in T0 when necessary, the Gaussian residual is independent jointly of T0 and all truth columns, and this independence survives constructing mu from sample-standardized truth columns. Uniform bounds on a and b follow from correlation concentration and the fixed truth support, including the sample-standardized signal; the perturbation changes the exponential envelope by o(1). The X-events in L6(b),(d), after the zero-residual convention above and for sufficiently large n, provide the fixed constants and deterministic envelopes required by (C4); selection is not being conditioned into a Beta law. The growing-reference counterexample L7 also does not require applying the bounded-reference concentration theorem.
