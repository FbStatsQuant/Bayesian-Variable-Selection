## Adversarial checks that passed

- **The shared-stream objection does not refute R3.** For a fixed statistical realization, let `τ_i` count uniforms consumed before particle i and let H be the statistical history before the population round. Conditional on `F_{τ_i}`, fresh-tail independence gives `E[f_i(order_i,path_i)|F_{τ_i}]=Qf_i(x_i)`, where entering `x_i` is H-measurable. Earlier particles' pairs are `F_{τ_i}`-measurable. Iterated conditioning therefore gives `E[∏_i f_i(order_i,path_i)|H]=∏_i Qf_i(x_i)`. This establishes the full conditional independence demanded by `T12/STATEMENT.md:44`, not merely independent marginal orders. State-dependent consumption and zero-consumption self-loops are stopping-time operations. The finite-budget correction in issue 2 supplies finiteness. R1 follows by the same argument and N9 Claim S. The Fisher–Yates choices have constant joint mass `1/m!`, not just a bijection, by the fresh ideal uniforms (`CODE/cpp/v3_engine.h:196–201`).
- **The guide table is correct under the stated idealizations.** For `u>0`, `b=floor(Nu)` lies in `0,…,N−1`; the monotone construction at `CODE/cpp/v3_engine.h:243–247` gives the first index with `W_k≥b/N`. It cannot exceed `k*=min{k:W_k≥u}`. The forward loop at `:255` reaches k*, and `:256` cannot move back because its predecessor is strictly below u. This includes `N=1` and exact bucket/cumulative boundaries. Leading zero masses are skipped for positive u; at an internal plateau the earliest crossing is selected, not a later zero-mass index; trailing zeros cause no problem since `u<1`. At `u=0`, `:252` substitutes `denorm_min`; L1 correctly uses the fact that this is a null event under H-U. All-zero masses are outside scope: exponential bridge weights are strictly positive under EX-A. Thus zero-weight behavior need not be invoked to rescue R2, and no positive-probability boundary defect exists in this model.
